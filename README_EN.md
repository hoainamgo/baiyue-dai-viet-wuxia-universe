## 🌟 1. UNIVERSE MANIFESTO & CORE PHILOSOPHY

For centuries, Eastern martial arts lore has been predominantly shaped by northern archetypes. Yet, buried deep beneath thousands of years of sedimentation, **Baiyue civilization and the realm of Dai Viet** possess an extraordinary, sovereign, and deeply humanistic epic heritage:
* **Naval Warfare & Close-Quarter Combat:** Sons of the Southern waters cut their hair, tattooed the Giao Long, dove like otters, treated oars as steed, and deployed the Bach Dang iron-tipped wooden stakes to thrice crush invading imperial armadas.
* **Pure Monotheistic Sky Worship:** Inheriting the post-flood open-air stone altars, ancient Vietnamese built the **Bàn Thờ Thiên** (Altar of Heaven) under open skies to offer pure spring water and golden rice grains to the Supreme Creator (*Ông Trời*), strictly refusing artificial joss sticks or idols.
* **Humanistic Herbal Medicine:** *"Nam Dược Trị Nam Nhân"* (Southern medicine heals Southern people) — a comprehensive pharmacopoeia of 580 native herbs, holistic care, and wound recovery; strictly distinct from medical treatment substitutes.
* **The Ethos of Eternal Love & Loyalty:** *"Love is patient and kind; love does not envy or boast; it is not arrogant. The people of Dai Viet cherish righteousness, loyalty to family, and selfless devotion to the homeland."*

**The Bai Yue & Dai Viet Wuxia Universe** was born to bring this magnificent five-thousand-year soul to the global stage!
