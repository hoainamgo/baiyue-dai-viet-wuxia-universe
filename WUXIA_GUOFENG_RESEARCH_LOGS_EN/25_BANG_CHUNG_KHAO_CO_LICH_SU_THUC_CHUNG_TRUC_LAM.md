### 1. National Treasure: Ngoa Van – Yen Tu gold box (14th century):
* **Excavation location:** In 2012 at Trai Loc slope, An Sinh commune, Dong Trieu (on the way to Ngoa Van temple - where Tran Nhan Tong passed away).
* **Object characteristics:** Crafted of pure gold, carved with patterns of **multi-layered lotus flowers and soft chrysanthemum branches** with mounding techniques, highlighting the peak of the Tran Dynasty.
* **Empirical meaning:** Proves that Tran dynasty aesthetics favored **elegance and sophistication, taking natural lotus and chrysanthemums as the supreme symbols**, completely different from the magical, gloomy offerings.

### 2. Archeology of Ngoa Van Am Relic & Hoa Yen Pagoda:
* **Foundation scale:** Excavation of stratigraphic layers of the 13th - 14th centuries shows that the meditation temples were built of **natural composite slate, brick foundations**, on a very modest scale (about 30 - 50m²), nestled next to mountain cliffs and forest streams.
* **Lifetime artifacts found:**
  * Celadon ceramic dishes, small mouth terracotta teapots, ivory white ceramic cups.
  * **Small copper and ceramic incense burner:** Used to burn agarwood resin and dried herbs.
  * ** COMPLETELY ABSOLUTELY ABSENT: ** There is no pit to bury votive ashes, no statues of hellish demons, no cards to offer stars to relieve drought.

### 3. Lotus nose tiles & Tran dynasty statue of lovebirds (Thang Long Imperial Citadel & Yen Tu):
* The roof tiles are embossed with the image of a pure lotus bud and a pair of lovebirds, symbolizing love, family harmony and peaceful country nature.
