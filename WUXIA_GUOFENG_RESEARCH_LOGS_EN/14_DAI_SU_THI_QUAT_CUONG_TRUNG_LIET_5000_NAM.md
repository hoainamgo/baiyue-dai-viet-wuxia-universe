### 🌾 PHASE 1: INSTITUTION OF THE BACH VIET COMMUNITY & VAN LANG - AU LAC (5,300 BC - 2nd century BC)
* **Institutional Nature:** The primitive state was based on an alliance of rice tribes and rural communes.
* **Apparatus Structure:**
  * **King Hung / An Duong Vuong:** Leader of the alliance, plays the role of supreme celebrant before the Thien Altar and military commander in case of foreign invasion.
  * **Lac Hau (Quan Van):** Political advisor, coordinator of seasonal agriculture and water management.
  * **Lac Tuong (Quan Vo / Local Leader):** Governs the member tribes, with high autonomy.
  * **Bo Chinh (Village Elder):** Represents the villages and resolves disputes using rustic customs.
* **Institutional Cultural Characteristics:**
  * The institution is **Primitive Democracy**, in absolute harmony with nature.
  * Promoting the role of women in the economy and society (matrilineal/biological Bach Viet mark).
  * The original belief is to worship God in the open air, offering clear spring water and golden sticky rice grains to thank the Creator.

### 🐉 PHASE 3: SHAPING THE INDEPENDENT AND AUTONOMOUS EMPIRE (DINH - TIEN LE - LY - TRAN: X TO XIV CENTURIES)

#### 1. Dinh - Early Le Dynasty (10th Century): Declaration of Equal Empire
* **Dinh Tien Hoang (968):** He did not call himself King (like the vassals of the North) but called himself **Emperor** (Dinh Tien Hoang De), named the country **Dai Co Viet** (Big Viet Country), minted *Thai Binh Hung Bao* coins, and gave it its own era name. This is a strong institutional declaration: The Southern Kingdom is an independent empire with absolute sovereignty equal to the Northern Dynasty.
* **Organization of the Ten Dao Army:** Build a regular army of 10 divisions under the command of the Ten Dao General, ensuring national defense deterrence capacity.

#### 2. Ly Dynasty Era (1009 - 1225): Cultural Institutions & Three Religions of the Same Origin
* **First Written Code of Law (1042):** King Ly Thai Tong issued **The Penal Code**, ending the era of arbitrary trials, opening the nation's first era of written rule of law.
* **Construction of the Temple of Literature (1070) & Quoc Tu Giam (1076):** Opened the national university education system, organized examinations to select talented people based on education instead of just relying on aristocratic lineage.
* **The "Three Religions of the Same Origin" Institution:** Confucianism (social governance order) combines harmoniously with Truc Lam Buddhism (thoughts of compassion, patriotism, tolerance) and Taoism (living according to nature), creating a pure, humane civilization at its peak.
* **Policy of "Reserving Soldiers in the Nong" & "The Marriage of the Border":** Marrying princesses to chiefs in border areas (Lang Son, Cao Bang) to strengthen the great unity of ethnic groups.

#### 3. Dong A Era Tran Dynasty (1226 - 1400): Two-Headed Institution & Dien Hong Spirit
* **Dual-Headed Regime (Thai Thuong Hoang & Emperor):** Creation of a unique institution: The father abdicated the throne early to become Thai Thuong Hoang to guide and impart experience in governing the country to the young King. This regime completely eliminated the bloody royal throne struggle and maintained political stability through three resistance wars against the Mongols.
* **Manor Estate System:** Allows nobles to establish fiefdoms to reclaim the sea, turning estates into autonomous economic and military bases ready to provide food and soldiers when the country is in danger.

### 📜 PHASE 4: THE PEAK OF dharma rule & virtuosity of the later Le dynasty (15th century)

#### 1. Comprehensive Administrative Reform of King Le Thanh Tong (Hong Duc Era):
* Eliminate the scattered decentralization regime, abolish the position of Prime Minister, and establish the **Six Ministries (Lai, Ho, Le, Binh, Hinh, Cong)** responsible directly to the Emperor.
* Divide the whole country into 13 Thua Tuyen, with agencies controlling each other's power (Dou ty, Thua Tien, Hien ty).
* Measuring land and drawing the first national map: **Hong Duc Map**, affirming the sacred territorial boundaries from the mainland to the islands.
* King Le Thanh Tong's immortal declaration of protecting border sovereignty:
> *"One meter of mountain, one inch of my river, should I throw it away? You must resolutely argue... If you dare to use one meter of mountain, one inch of land of Thai To as bait for the enemy, then you must be guilty of the crime!"*

#### 2. Hong Duc Code (National Penal Code) — The Pinnacle of Eastern Humanities:
* It is the most progressive, strict and humane law in the history of Eastern feudal law:
  * **Protecting women's rights:** Women have the same right to inherit property as sons when their parents die; The wife has the right to divorce if the husband neglects her for 5 months.
  * **Protection of public land in villages and communes:** Nobles and mandarins are strictly prohibited from oppressing and appropriating farmland from poor people.
  * **Severe punishment for corruption:** Punish the crime of beheading or exile for officials who take bribes, abuse their power and harass the people.
