# VISUAL ART CANON OF ĐÔNG A: TRẦN DYNASTY (1225–1400)

> **Public Release:** 1.0 · **Updated:** 2026-09-25  
> [`57_CANON_DONG_A_THAI_THOI_TRAN_1225_1400.md`](../WUXIA_GUOFENG_RESEARCH_LOGS/57_CANON_DONG_A_THAI_THOI_TRAN_1225_1400.md) is the primary historical and material standard.

## 1. Visual Directives

1. Every scene must be anchored in a specific geography, season, weather, time of day, and ongoing human activity.
2. Historical figures must maintain documented physical stature, attires, and verified weaponry. Fictional characters are clearly designated.
3. Natural color palettes derived from botanical dyes and artisanal materials: root brown (*củ nâu*), indigo (*chàm*), glazed stoneware, ironwood, brass, raw silk.
4. Natural lighting; human skin features pores, perspiration, dust, alluvial mud, sea spray, calluses, and real-world wear. No plastic skin or glossy CGI artifacts.
5. Completely free of internal software prompts, workflow tags, or production metadata.

## 2. Strictly Prohibited Elements

- Northern imperial dragons, flying dragons, or five-clawed palace dragons as primary emblems.
- Northern Hanfu, Ming tall collars, Qing qipaos, Japanese kimonos, obis, or fantasy glowing Guan Dao blades.
- Flying swords, mystical cultivation levels, magical spells, shamanic possessions, or futuristic gadgets.
- Anachronistic weapons: heavy artillery in the 13th century, automatic bows, or Western plate armor.
- Text overlays, watermarks, distorted faces, extra fingers, or synthetic plastic sheen.

## 3. Authentic Attire Guidelines

### 3.1. Male Attires
- **Daily Living & Labor:** Short or long cross-collar tunic (*Áo Giao Lĩnh*) in indigo or root-brown; woven raw silk, hemp cords; tied trousers, rolled cuffs; conical palm hats, wooden clogs, or bare feet where appropriate.
- **Combat & Garrison:** Buffalo hide armor treated with wood oil, copper rivets, bronze chest mirror (*Hộ tâm kính*), forearm bracers; lined with raw silk or coarse linen.
- **Rules:** No exposed bare chests without specific labor context; no modern heraldic emblems.

### 3.2. Female Attires
- **Daily Life:** Cross-collar (*Giao Lĩnh*) or four-panel dress (*Áo Tứ Thân*) in raw indigo/brown silk; sleeves tied up when crafting pottery, gathering herbs, or cooking; woven bamboo baskets.
- **Scholarly & Artistic Settings:** Moon-white, cobalt, or amber raw silk; carved wooden or lotus hairpins, silk sashes.
- **Court & Ceremonial:** Brocade and formal jade sashes only for explicit court ceremonies; never dress ordinary civilians in royal court regalia.
- **Rules:** No tight-fitting modern garments, translucent dresses, mini-skirts, or contemporary accessories.

## 4. Primary Canon Characters

### An Thái
- Square jaw; subtle scar on the left cheek; hair bound with a wooden pin or headscarf; Giao Long tattoo; wrist marked with the *Sát Thát* pledge.
- Attire: Indigo cross-collar tunic, laminated leather armor vest, bamboo sword, short leaf-blade dao, barbed spear; no northern firearms.
- Visible traces of life: Lục Đầu alluvial mud, salt-crusted ropes, tattoo ink, calloused palms, clay residues.

### Mộc Tĩnh Lan
- Low or high chignon; carved wooden or lotus hairpin; clay stains on hands, subtle kiln burns; woven bamboo herbal satchel.
- Attires: Celadon-green cross-collar dress for craftwork; moon-white silk for quiet reflection; brown/indigo four-panel dress for night journeys and herbal gathering.
- Role: Master potter, medic tending wounded soldiers, herbal keeper, acoustic musician; no supernatural spells.

## 6. Fortresses and Military Structures

- **Cổ Loa:** Concentric rammed-earth ramparts, alluvial clay mixed with sea shells, deep water moats, bronze crossbows.
- **Thung Lau:** Rugged limestone karsts, natural caves, tall reeds, narrow defiles.
- **Yên Tử:** Cliffside trails, mountain pine and bamboo groves, modest thatched hermitages.
- **Bạch Đằng 1288:** Muddy riverbanks, ironwood stakes, supply barges, spears, shields; no artillery.
- **Tây Sơn Artillery:** Confined strictly to the late 18th century, with wooden gun carriages, powder horns, and specialized crews.
