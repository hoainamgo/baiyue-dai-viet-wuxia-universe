# YEN TU IN HISTORY AND WORLDVIEW OF THE orient A

> **Public Document · Updated 2026-09-25**
> Priority Canon: [`57_CANON_DONG_A_THAI_THOI_TRAN_1225_1400.md`](57_CANON_DONG_A_THAI_THOI_TRAN_1225_1400.md) and [`58_CANON_VISUAL_DONG_A_TRAN_1225_1400.md`](58_CANON_VISUAL_DONG_A_TRAN_1225_1400.md).

## 1. The two times should not mix

- **Year 1237:** King Tran Thai Tong went to Yen Tu when An Sinh king Tran Lieu rebelled; Tran Thu Do built Thien An Palace. This is **a mobile court in chaos**.
- **At the end of the Tran dynasty, around 1299:** Tran Nhan Tong abdicated the throne and ascended to Yen Tu to become a monk following the legendary Truc Lam Zen class. The circumstances of 1237 cannot be used to prove a “secret capital move” in 1299.

## 2. Geography

- Yen Tu - Phu Van belongs to Dong Trieu, on the road connecting mountains, lagoons and rivers towards Bach Dang.
- Plateaus, forests, streams and clouds create areas for searching, medicine, shelter and exchange; not a view of “the entire Northeast” like modern maps.
- Tides, fog, treacherous roads, and rainy season currents affected movement, guarding, and supplies.

## 3. Religious and social class

- Truc Lam Zen during the Tran Dynasty had hermitages, monks, monks and the bamboo forest tradition.
- Pharmacy warehouse, medicine kitchen, ambulance service and exchange with the community can appear as practical activities.
- Military police, woodcutters, intelligence networks or “secret communications stations” are **controlled fiction** for extensive documentation, not verified conclusions.
- When talking about Tue Tinh, one must use the legend of the boat or the theory of medicine; Don't turn historical figures into magic.

## 4. Sources and sites

When mentioning the site of Ngoa Van Am, Hoa Yen, Am Duoc, Hue Quang Tower or Ngoa Van gold box, it must be written:

- official name of the site;
- keywords in excavation reports or regulatory agency publications;
- found object;
- chronology and level of preservation;
- Uncertain point.

Do not combine multiple characteristics into one “unifying physical evidence” without inventorying each artifact. The contents "underground pharmaceutical warehouse", "martial arts training ground", "monitoring posture" and "monitoring system" need to be labeled fictional if there is no evidence.

## 5. How to use it in the story

- The potter, herb picker, doctor, teacher, patrolman and transporter characters can appear at huts, kitchens, trails, warehouses and duty points.
- Disputes between weavers, potters, farmers, dike builders, and mandarins created conflicts over taxes, food, land, and labor obligations.
- There is no need to declare Yen Tu an "absolute outpost" to create drama; Terrain, rainy season, news and forces can create a more difficult situation than declaring excellent strategy.

## 6. Short scene sample

**Controlled Fiction — Yen Tu After the Rain:**
The dew still lies on the pine needles, the sound of a stream flowing under the cliff, a physician carries a basket back to the hut; In the distance, potters brought bread and food up the path. A news from Luc Dau wharf made everyone stop: the royal court was no longer in Thang Long as before.

This scene uses the **historical phenomenon of 1237** as a backdrop; only the work, supporting characters, and relationships are fictional.
