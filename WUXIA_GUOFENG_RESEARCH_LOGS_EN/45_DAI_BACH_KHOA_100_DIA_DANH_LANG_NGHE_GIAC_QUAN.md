## 🧭 I. THE IMPORTANCE OF REAL LIFE DETAILS
Clichés in works often stem from **3 poverty in cultural documents**:
1. **Poor Place Names:** Can only mention in general terms *"rivers and mountains, rivers and lakes, frontiers, moon and stars"*.
2. **Poor Craft Village & Life:** There are no actual labor details (blacksmithing, pottery, silk weaving, paper making, tea brewing, stone carving...).
3. **Poor Senses (Hold - Smell - Touch - Taste - Hear):** Always use abstract adjectives (*sad, lonely, majestic*) instead of **physical entities**.
