# MYSTERIOUS SPIRITS, STATUES AND LEGENDARY CHARACTERS

> **Public Document · Updated 2026-09-25**
> Content about Hai Ba Trung must separate history, mythology and fictional stories.

## 1. Classification

- **[REAL HISTORY]:** year 40, the uprising, Me Linh, female generals, 65 citadels according to historical traditions and related documents.
- **[MYTH]:** founder, deification, warrior statue, sacred elephant image, ancient rituals.
- **[CONTROLLED FICTION]:** sect name, martial arts, costumes, soldier set and detailed tactical techniques.

## 2. Me Linh context

- Me Linh and Hat Mon are located in the delta, near the Hat River and waterways leading to the inland.
- The insurgent force needs to organize food, information, transportation and battlefields; Don't just rely on pride.
- If using elephants, it must be clearly stated whether the details are historical, legendary or fictional; Do not attach gold armor, bronze ivory armor or supernatural powers to historical events.

## 3. Female appearance and morality

- The determination, organization, sacrifice and community spirit of the Trung Sisters can be honored.
- Do not use “65 cities” as an absolutely proven number; say "according to historical tradition" or cite specific sources.
- Don't use women just as symbols; describes decisions, occupations, care of wounded soldiers, negotiations, and command.

## 4. Statue and sound

- Soldier statues are legendary material, not evidence that Dong Son drums control elephants with infrasound waves.
- When appearing in stories or images, statues must be labeled **controlled fiction**; describes the wood, paint, rope, beam, pull and ground.
- If the Dong Son drum appears, it is described as a festival sound or a signal in the story; no frequency attached, unverified frequency or ability to control animals.

## 5. Set of 12 animals: changed to fiction

Names such as Bach Tuong Me Linh, Bach Hac, Hac Bao, Golden Rooster, Thiet Ma, Red Dragon and Huyet Wolf can be kept as **fictional mascots**. Do not attach them to places, tribes, historical gods or real fighting abilities without a source. Plants like “ropes,” “iron horses,” “giant whales,” or “giant snakes” should be treated as fictional magic, not biological.

## 6. Structure used for stories

- **History:** year, place, source, role.
- **Legend:** the way characters and communities retell soldiers' statues.
- **Fictitious:** statue names, missions, infantry, weapons, animals.
- **Emotions:** fear, hope, solidarity, determination; Do not replace it with scientific statements.
