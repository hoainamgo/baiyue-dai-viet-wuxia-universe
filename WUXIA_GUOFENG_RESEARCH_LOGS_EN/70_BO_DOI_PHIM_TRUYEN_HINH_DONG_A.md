- **Rice leaf knife (~40cm):** Dong Son bronze - iron artifact with wide blade, sharp tip; suitable for fighting boats and trenches - where the long spear cannot rotate in time. This is the prototype of An Thai's short sword: short, slightly curved, peach wood handle.
- **Co Loa God Crossbow (~257 BC):** 3-pronged bronze arrow from Co Loa excavation; *History* records Lien Chau crossbow shooting 300 steps away. In the movie: the scene where Cao Lo tests the crossbow - the arrow penetrates 3 layers of rattan armor.
- **Leg choke:** traditional wrestling technique (Lieu Doi wrestling field, Ha Nam) — using the leg to grab the neck to knock down the cavalry. In the rap movie: An Thai flipped around and grabbed the neck of an enemy general who fell off his horse, filmed in slow motion for 3 seconds.
- **Water warfare to dig a boat:** *Dai Viet Su Ky Complete Book* records Yet Kieu diving deep into the bottom of the enemy's boat (1288). On film: underwater scenes of Bach Dang River — red mud, iron piles, chisel hammers.
