## 🌊 I. ORIGIN & MISLEADING OF THE PHRASE "THREE BIRTH"

### 1. Legend of China's "Sansheng Stone" (三生石 - Tamsheng Stone):
* **Origin:** Originated from the story of monks Yuan Ze (圓澤) and Ly Yuan (李源) during the Tang Dynasty on the banks of the West Lake River (Hangzhou). Vien Trach promised to be reincarnated in his next life as a child herding buffalo, meeting his old friend next to a stone called *Tam Sinh Thach*.
* **Philosophical nature:** Belongs to the ideology of **Reincarnation of Karma (Samsara)** introduced to China, the concept of human life being divided into **Previous life (Previous life), Present life (This life), and Future life (Next life)**.
* **Limitations in pure Vietnamese poetry:** The phrases *"Wish for three births"*, *"Debt for three births"* gradually become **cliché literary paths**, turning love into the vagueness and mystery of the next life instead of appreciating and living fully every moment of the **only existing life**.

## ☀️ II. THE IDENTITY OF LOVE OF THE ANCIENT VIETNAMESE: "A LONG IRON SWORD · THE HAI MINH SON"

Completely different from the concept of reincarnation and reincarnation, the ancient Vietnamese consciousness from early times (inherited from the original religion of Sacrifice to Heaven) always considered **each life as a unique sacred gift bestowed by the Creator (God).

### 1. "The Son's Heart" — The Immortal Symbol of Faithfulness:
* In Dai Viet folk songs, folk songs and Zen poetry: The color vermilion (the crimson of alluvial clay, of the blood of people protecting the country) symbolizes loyalty that never fades in the face of time and adversity.
* *"Even if the river dries up and the rocks wear away / I'll keep my heart intact for the rest of my life!"*

### 2. "The Oath to Hai Minh Son" (The Oath to Meet the Sea Under the Sky):
* When the ancient Vietnamese people took a vow, they did not burn incense or bow to statues. They stood before **Blue Sky, Sacred Mountains (Yen Tu, Ba Vi) and Big Sea (Bach Dang, East Sea)** and swore:
> *"The high mountains bear witness, the wide ocean illuminates our hearts, a life together is full of love!"*

### 3. Cao Khang's Love Is Associated With Real Life:
* The love of knights An Thai and Moc Tinh Lan is not a false promise in the afterlife, but rather:
  * Together, they carried alluvial clay from the Luoc River to build a pottery kiln at the age of 17.
  * She embroidered a silk ribbon with the word *Binh An*, and he handed over a piece of brown flower ceramic to keep her chest warm.
  * Sharing a bowl of steaming green tea on the porch in the summer rain.
