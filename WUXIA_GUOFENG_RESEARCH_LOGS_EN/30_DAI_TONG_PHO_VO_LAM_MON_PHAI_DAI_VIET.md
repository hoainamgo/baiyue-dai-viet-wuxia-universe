## 🌊 1. DIVISION I: LAC LONG THUY PHAI (RIVER AND DAO WARFIGHTS)

* **Origin of inheritance:** Inherited from Patriarch Lac Long Quan, flourished under the Tran dynasty through famous generals **Yet Kieu, Da Tuong, Hiep Khach An Thai**.
* **Secret headquarters:** Luc Dau Giang Valley (Van Kiep) & Chu Dau River Wharf.
* **Mind Dharma & Martial Arts:**
  * **"Cuu Long Chan Hai Quyet":** Qigong regulates breath underwater, holding a reed breathing tube at the bottom of the river for many hours.
  * **"Short Knife with Rice Leaves (Lac Viet Dao Phap)":** The method of melee combat is zigzagging, short-armed, cutting off the tendons of Mongolian war horses' legs.
  * **"Short Soldier Breaks Cavalry Technique":** Throws people up 2m high and grabs the enemy general's neck.
* **Suppression weapons:**
  * **Rice leaf short knife made of bronze and tempered iron:** The blade is gently curved in the shape of a rice leaf, the handle is covered with stingray skin engraved with the image of a Lac bird.
  * **Iron Drill & Boat Bottom Hammer:** Naval assassination weapon.
* **Attire & Identity:**
  * **Indigo shirt with chest slit (Giao Linh/Tu Than):** Hemp fabric dyed with dark blue-black indigo root, waterproof.
  * **Giao Long tattoo:** Full body tattoo of water dragon monster, wrist with two words **"SAT THAT"**.

## 🏔️ 2. DIVISION II: TAN VIEN SON MON (HIGH MARTIALISTS & GREAT IRON HAMMER)

* **Origin of inheritance:** Inherited from **Tan Vien Son Thanh (Son Tinh)** and the brave warrior **Thach Sanh**.
* **Secret headquarters:** Ba Vi ancient forest (Tan Vien Son) & Northwest deep forest.
* **Mind Dharma & Martial Arts:**
  * **"Tan Vien Chan Son Kinh":** Qigong's internal force is as powerful as a basalt mountain, its body is made of iron and skin, and it is difficult for a sword to hurt.
  * **"Khai Son Phu Phap":** The billowing line of iron hammers cut off the ancient tree, with one blow beheading the demon beast Chan Tinh.
  * **"Hung Ke Quan":** The fighting cock's boxing method approaches the dangerous acupuncture point.
* **Suppression weapons:**
  * **Dai Khai Son Phu (Forest Ax Weighing 50 Pounds):** Forged from great grade steel, engraved with the ancient words Bach Viet.
  * **Ancient String Instrument Divine Ancient Music:** A mulberry wood instrument with silk strings, the sound of the instrument dispels evil spirits and disarms soldiers and swords.
* **Attire & Identity:**
  * **Indigo shirt lined with tiger/leopard skin:** Going barefoot, head wrapped in a red fringed scarf, belt braided with wild hemp.

## ⚡ 3. DIVISION III: PHU DONG THIET GIA (MASTER OF METALLURGY & TIE CON)

* **Inheritance:** Inherited from **Phu Dong Thien Vuong (Thanh Giong)** and **General Cao Lo**.
* **Secret headquarters:** Soc Son Mountain (Hanoi) & Co Loa magic crossbow forging workshop.
* **Mind Dharma & Martial Arts:**
  * **"Phu Dong Kim Than Quyet":** Condenses blood and energy into a layer of diamond protection.
  * **"Thanh Truc Truong Phap (Bagua Kun)":** The bamboo and ivory stick fist is flexible but has the power of lightning to wipe out thousands of troops.
  * **"Thanh No Van Tien Battle":** Crossbow shooting technique of launching 10 bronze arrows at the same time, hitting the bull's eye.
* **Suppression weapons:**
  * **Bronze-covered Bamboo Cone:** Old bamboo is burned to increase its strength with appropriate fibers/glue; The two ends are wrapped in brass, and the dragon motif is only used when there is a reference to the relic.
  * **Lien Chau crossbow:** Wooden frame, horn, crossbow string and arrow consistent with Co Loa period techniques; Name numbers must follow archaeological evidence.
* **Attire & Identity:**
  * **Bronze riveted leather armor & head wrap:** Properly constructed over leather, brass, canvas armor and shoulder guards; not automatically associated with the "dragon head of the Ly-Tran dynasties".

## 🌸 4. DIVISION IV: TRUC LAM ZEN PHAI (ZEN, MEDICINE & SWORD)

* **Origin of inheritance:** Inherited from **Chu Dong Tu - Tien Dung (Tien Dao Da Trach)**, **Buddhist Tran Nhan Tong** and **Holy Medicine Nam Tue Tinh**.
* **Secret headquarters:** Ngoa Van Pagoda (Yen Tu), Thay Pagoda (Sai ​​Son) & Da Trach Lagoon.
* **Mind Dharma & Martial Arts:**
  * **"Da Trach Quy Tuc Cong":** Body movements are as light as clouds of smoke, unpredictable and unpredictable like morning mist.
  * **"Old Mai Quyen & Truc Sword Thien Tam":** Use a soft, curved bamboo sword to neutralize all killing blows of your opponent.
  * **"Shen Nong Herbal Medicine":** The art of acupressure on meridians and medicinal herbs to save poisoned people.
* **Suppression weapons:**
  * **Yen Tu Bamboo Sword (Sharp Old Bamboo Sword):** Weapon that does not kill, specializes in pointing acupuncture points and deflecting swords.
  * **Bamboo Stick & Divine Leaf Hat (Da Trach Chi Bao):** The leaf hat protects against rain and sun and rotates to form a shield to block arrows.
* **Attire & Identity:**
  * **Rose Brown Jute Shirt & Raw Silk Four-Body Shirt:** Hair with a peach wood brooch (character of Moc Tinh Lan), a bamboo medicine basket on her back, radiating the scent of wormwood and lotus flowers.

## ⚓ 5. DIVISION V: HAI LONG MAI GIA (SEA KING HIEP & ARMY OF THE EASTERN SEA)

* **Origin of inheritance:** Inherited from the courage of **Ba Trieu (Slaying whales in the East Sea)**, **Mai An Tiem (Isolation of the island)** and **Hai Vuong Tu Hai (Hegemon of the East Sea)**.
* **Tantan headquarters:** Ha Long Bay, Cat Ba Island & South China Sea.
* **Mind Dharma & Martial Arts:**
  * **"The Overlord Slaying the Sea Knife":** The blade weighing thousands of pounds shattered the wooden boat's hull, causing the waves to roar.
  * **"Diamond Indestructible Body":** Train your body against the waves and storms of the ocean.
* **Suppression weapons:**
  * **Dao Hai Dai Dao (80 Pounds Long):** The blade is wide, the blade is thick, carved with the image of a rolling sea dragon.
  * **Flame-Infused Bow & Arrows & Flamethrower:** Long-range ship-destroying weapons.
* **Attire & Identity:**
  * **Great Sea King's Brocade Robe (Tu Hai):** Dark blue with fur trim, gold belt with pearl touches.
  * **Happy Phuong Phuong Robe & Green Chiffon Scarf (Thuy Kieu / Moc Tinh Lan):** The silk dress flutters proudly in the sea breeze.
