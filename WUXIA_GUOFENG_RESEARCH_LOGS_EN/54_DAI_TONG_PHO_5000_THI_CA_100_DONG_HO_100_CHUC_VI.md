* **An Thai (Ao Cham brave man):** Born on the banks of Luc Dau river, descended from Lac Long sailors. Bearing the scars of the battlefield, having a calm and steadfast personality, carrying the sword and spear to protect the territory from invasion.
* **Moc Tinh Lan (Artist Ao Tu Than & Physician):** The eldest daughter of the Moc family in Chu Dau pottery village, a true disciple of the Truc Lam Yen Tu Zen sect. His hands skillfully polished brown ceramic painted with blue glazed lotus flowers and expertly soaked 580 herbal medicines to aid soldiers.

### 2. Harmony Relationship:
* Before An Thai set out to command the battleship at Bach Dang, the two of them stood in front of the **Open Heaven Altar** and broke a newly fired blue ceramic bowl into 2 halves:
  * An Thai wears a piece of pottery on the chest of an indigo shirt as a protective talisman.
  * Tinh Lan buried half a piece of pottery under the kiln turntable all night waiting for the day of victory.
