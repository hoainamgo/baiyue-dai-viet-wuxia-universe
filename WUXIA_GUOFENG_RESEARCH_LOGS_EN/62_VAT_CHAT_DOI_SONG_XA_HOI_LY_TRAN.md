## 🍚 PHẦN I: THẾ GIỚI ẨM THỰC & ĐỒ ĐỰNG (BÁT CƠM, CHÉN CỐC, ẤM TRÀ, ĐĨA THÌA)

### 1. Rice Bowls & Cups of the Aristocracy / Royal Family (Ly - Tran):
- Celadon Jade Celadon Bowl (Emerald Glaze / Jade Glaze): Thin, smooth ceramic bone with light luminescence, thick flowing glaze of turquoise or melon seed green, the bowl's heart is embossed with two sparrows or 16-petal double lotus petals symbolizing Imperial Buddhism.
- Ivory White Enamel Bowl Embossed with a Dragon of the Ly Dynasty: The dragon's body is smooth and soft, winding in a sinusoidal shape (Dragons of the Ly Dynasty have no horns, 3-4 clawed feet), the rim of the bowl is covered with a real gold/silver border.
- Noble Wine Cups & Tea Cups: Celadon enamel buffalo eye cups with high legs, melon cups with mother-of-pearl inlaid lids, brown flower enamel water cups with curved lotus petals.
- Teapot & Wine Bottle: Celadon pot with spout shaped like a rising phoenix head, curved dragon-shaped handle; The bottle gourd (Ho Lo) has a pattern of undulating water waves.
- Noble Chopsticks & Spoons: Chopsticks made of ebony or ivory with silver on both ends; Leaf-shaped celadon-coated ceramic spoon or gold-plated copper spoon.

### 2. Rice Bowls & Cups of Common People / Folk Culture:
- Ash-Glazed Terracotta Bowl (Wooden Porcelain Pottery): Coarse earthenware bowl the color of eel skin, gray glazed with ash from straw kiln, the inside of the bowl still has traces of ceramic kiln millet, the thick bottom of the bowl retains heat for hot rice.
- Lo Chu Dau Brown Flower Enamel Bowl: Simple ivory enamel bowl painted with brown iron oxide in the shape of daisies, reeds or swimming goby fish.
- Cracked Coconut Shell for Drinking Water: Cut the old coconut in half, smooth out the inside, and use it to scoop rainwater from a clay jar in the corner of the yard.
- Bat Bat Yeu (Bat Tie Waist): Flared mouth, tightened waist, small bottom, used to eat rice stuffed with corn and potatoes or spinach and eggplant soup.
- Folk Chopsticks & Spoons: Chopsticks sharpened from old bamboo (male bamboo) soaked in mud to prevent mold, spoons peeled from pieces of dried gourd peel or jackfruit wood.

## 🏠 PART II: HOUSE ARCHITECTURE & LIVING SPACE STRUCTURE

### 1. Mansions, Noble Palaces & Royal Palaces (Ly - Tran Dynasties):
- Structure: Multi-compartment house (5 rooms, 2 wings or 7 rooms), large ironwood column frame that can be held by one person, jointing technique without using an iron nail.
- Roof: Roofed with green/yellow enameled green-glazed funny-nosed tiles or terracotta leaf-tiled tiles carved with dragons and phoenixes. The curved roof of the knife's head rises up like the bow of a boat breaking the waves. On the roof, there is a statue of a dragon head (Si Vi) releasing pearls and a statue of a lovebird made of terracotta ceramics.
- Foundation & Garden: The floor is tiled with square terracotta lotus/chrysanthemum tiles (archaeology of Thang Long Imperial Citadel). The stone steps are carved with a pair of winding stone dragons. There is a semi-moon lotus pond and a moon-shaped tea room above the water.

### 2. Housing of Civilians / Korean Guests:
- Structure: The house has three rooms and two wings, roofed with palm leaves or thick sticky rice straw, with walls plastered with mud and straw mixed with molasses, warm in winter and cool in summer.
- House frame: The rafters are made of old bamboo soaked in mud, taro wood, and jackfruit wood from the home garden.
- Ground floor & Porch: The ground is pounded tightly with a wooden pestle, and the large thatched porch hangs bunches of yellow corn, dried garlic, and bamboo baskets drying licorice and traditional medicine.
- Heavenly Altar: In front of the yard, always erect an outdoor stone or bamboo pillar as a Heavenly Altar, pointing straight up to the roofless dome of the sky.

## 🌲 PART III: WORLD OF WOOD SPECIES & PROOF MATERIALS

### 1. The Four Great Famous Mocs of the Court and the Rich (Dinh - Lim - Sen - Tau):
- Green Ironwood: Extremely heavy, sinks in water, wood grain has a smooth luster, used to make royal pillars, mandarin gates and pointed stakes at Bach Dang beach.
- Grindwood: Extremely hard as rock, used to make sword handles, catapult levers and marble chessboard tops.
- Rosewood & Horned Ebony: Jet black, glossy like horn, used to carve mahogany tea chests, seal boxes and aristocratic sword hilts.
- Thi Wood & Huynh Dan Wood (Sua Do): Give off a gentle fragrance to repel insects, five-colored wood grain, used to make sword scabbards and caskets to hold royal edicts.

### 2. Wild Wood & Herbal Materials:
- Garden Jackfruit Wood: The wood is bright yellow when first cut, turns dark red over time, never gets termites, used to carve Zen statues, and make columns for people's houses.
- Ivory Bamboo & Tam Vong Bamboo: Universal materials - making spear handles, weaving basket boats, making shoulder poles, house walls, secret letter containers.
- Do Tree & Duong Tree Bark: The bark of the tree is soaked in water and pounded to make Aquilaria paper, which lasts thousands of years without rotting, and is used to write medical books and bibliographies.
