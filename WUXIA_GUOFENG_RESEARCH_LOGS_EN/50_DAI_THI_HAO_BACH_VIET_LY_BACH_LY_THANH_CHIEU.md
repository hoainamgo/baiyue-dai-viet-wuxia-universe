## 🌟 PART I: BIOGRAPHIES & IMAGES OF 2 GREAT VIETNAMESE POETS

---

### 🗡️ 1. "LY BACH PHUONG NAM" — TRAN HAN THI TIEN (NAM THIEN THI TIEN)
* **Proper name:** Tran Nguyen Han (name: *Fu Van Cuong Khach*, *Luc Dau Thi Tien*).
* **Image & Atmosphere (Archetype):**
  * Her hair was tied freely in a bun, she wore a rustic indigo shirt, carried a Yen Tu bamboo wine gourd, and had a mahogany Day Dan and a rice leaf knife on her back.
  * Exported into poetry, every time he gets drunk, he dances with his sword under the full moon, his sword energy streaks into the air with generous poems that shake mountains and rivers.
  * Despising the royal titles and benefits, he once threw away his mandarin hat to row a dugout canoe up and down the South Truong Giang, Dong Dinh Ho and the Red River.
* **Poetic declaration:** *"We were born with talent and we must use it. Thousands of gold will be spent to find it again! Fame and fortune are like clouds flying over Luc Dau, only the moon and the wine bottle are immortal!"*

---

### 🌸 2. "LY THANH CHIEU OF THE SOUTH" — MOC TINH LAN (QING LIEN FEMALE FROM THE SAINT)
* **Proper name:** Moc Tinh Lan (name: *Thanh Lien Lay Si*, *Chu Dau Nu Tu Si*).
* **Image & Atmosphere (Archetype):**
  * She was a talented young woman with eyes as clear as autumn lake water, dressed in a four-piece dress the color of a celadon lotus flower, with a blue ceramic brooch in her hair, and in her hand holding a wolf feather pen dotted with blue enamel and sharpening the ceramic.
  * Đạt đến cảnh giới **Thi Họa Song Tuyệt**: Đề thơ trên đáy bình gốm Chu Đậu, mỗi nét chữ Nôm bay bổng tựa phượng múa rồng bay; ngón đàn dây 16 dây tơ ngân nga tần số Cổ Nhạc làm dịu tan mọi nỗi sầu nhân thế.
  * Rich in compassion, deeply empathizing with the pain of working people and women whose husbands are away in the army, carrying the most subtle, discreet and painful sadness of separation in the history of Southern poetry.
* **Declaration from the melody:** *"Flowers flow on their own, water flows on its own, a love sent to two places... Ceramic enamel, even if love breaks up, will not change, a thousand years of virginity will remain intact!"*

### 🌹 3. CLASSIC LITERATURE & FIVE-WIDE FAMILY LOVE
Like the legendary love between Trieu Minh Thanh and Ly Thanh Chieu, **Tran Han Thi Tien (later passed down to the brave warrior An Thai)** and **Moc Tinh Lan** created the most beautiful poetic love story of the era:
* Enjoy Tay Ho lotus tea together and recite poetry under the full moon night.
* Together, we wandered through caves and alleys to collect and restore **5,000 sets of Bach Viet ancient books** and 5,300-year-old Luong Chu characters.
* A blue-glazed ceramic plate broken in half at Luc Dau wharf became a token of immortal love that could overcome the fire and smoke of the battlefield!

### 🍷 BLOCK 1: GENEROUS THI THIEN — TRAN HAN THI TIEN (LY BACH STYLE)

#### 01. Nam Thien Tuong Tien Tuu (Wine Advice Under the Moon)
> Unseen army: Kinh Thay river water flows out from the top of the mountain,
> Flowing to the East Sea for a thousand lifetimes, never to return!
> Again, I don't see: The old mother looked in the mirror and felt pity for her gray hair,
> Green in the morning, covered in snow and frost in the afternoon!
> Life is full of contentment, let's be happy,
> Do not leave the golden cup alone under the moon.
> God created me to be useful,
> Thousands of gold returned to your hands!
> Slaughter the goat, beat the Dong Son drum,
> Drink three hundred cups of wine in one gulp!
> Let's get drunk tonight,
> Destroy all ancient sorrows!

#### 02. Luc Dau Cuong Ca
> A jug of bamboo wine and a sword,
> Laughing arrogantly at the world and walking miles.
> Waves crashing on the boat's side, the moon shining brightly,
> Dong A's pride shines in all directions!

#### 03. Moisture Under the Red River Moon
> Petals fall onto the surface of the clear ditch,
> Raise your cup and invite the moon to satisfy your heart.
> The moon and my shadow become three friends,
> Take a deep sleep in the middle of nowhere!

#### 04. Sword Dance on Phu Van Peak
> Draw your sword and tear apart the clouds,
> The sword's energy is overwhelming, overwhelming the autumn wind.
> Standing on the top of the mountain, laughing loudly,
> Thousand miles of Jiangshan, warm fortune!

#### 05. Song of the Empty Wharf
> People on the wharf are absent at sunset,
> The sound of a flute echoes at the end of the dunes.
> Fame and fortune are like bubbles,
> Only friends left in the village!

#### 06. Bach Viet Passenger Association
> Indigo shirt, conical hat, pillow with dew,
> Using a short knife with rice leaves to suppress violence.
> Kill the enemy to save the people without making a sound,
> Take off your clothes and smell the scent of incense!

#### 07. Admire Bao Thien Tower
> Twelve-story skyscraper tower,
> The morning bell rang and the wind blew away.
> I stood on the dragon's steps and recited a song,
> Thang Long is brilliant every month and the same day!

#### 08. Drinking Wine by the Forge
> The red fire of the forge glows with coal,
> Hammers pound thousands of steel.
> Raise a cup of spicy wine to celebrate finding treasure,
> Preserve the integrity of the country!

#### 09. Sleeping Deeply Under the Bamboo Forest
> Yen Tu Bamboo Forest, the wind blows,
> The stone pillow lies in a blind sleep.
> Waking up, the moonlight reflects on the stream,
> My heart is free from any worries!

#### 10. Sending You Off to the East Sea
> Three-masted sailboat sails through the rising tide,
> Sending you off to the sea of ​​ecstasy.
> Wide sea and high sky, my friend,
> See a day of victory under the spring sky!

#### 11. Sing Vietnamese Nhan Ca
> Let's go boating on the river tonight,
> Meet the warm-hearted brave man.
> Five thousand years of strong Vietnamese blood,
> The depth of love is as heavy as the mountains!

#### 12. Raise your head and smile arrogantly
> Ha ha ha ha! Life is like a dream,
> What's the point in fighting for wealth?
> We raise our glasses and sing the sun and moon,
> Thousands of mountains and thousands of autumn light up your head!

### 🌸 BLOCK 2: MEMORIAM & SORRY OF SIMILARITY — MOC TINH LAN (LY THANH CHIEU STYLE)

#### 13. Like a Dream - Rainy Night at the Pottery Kiln
> Last night the wind and rain poured down,
> Cooked blue enamel is still fragrant.
> Ask someone who stays up all night worrying about:
> "Is the lotus flower at the bottom of the bowl on fire yet?"
> Condolences... Dear condolences!
> Celadon shines brightly to welcome the morning light!

#### 14. Nhat Chi Mai — Ben Luc Head Sad Farewell
> The lotus flower loses its bright pink color,
> The blue water of the river bank silhouettes a person.
> A piece of pottery handed over on the farewell day,
> Tears kept falling from the scarf.
> Clouds drift lazily across the sky,
> The person goes through a long and restless sleep.
> Grief and love are as wide as the ocean,
> As soon as I left my eyebrows, my heart stopped!

#### 15. Thanh Ngoc An — Full Moon Festival of Flower Lanterns
> The spring wind blows down thousands of blue branches,
> Flower lanterns shine brightly in the sky.
> Horses and carriages roll through the streets,
> The sounds of instruments and flutes are bustling everywhere.
> Searching for someone hundreds of times in a sea of ​​people,
> Suddenly turned around...
> He stood there beside the banyan tree,
> The flickering lights smiled!

#### 16. Diep Luyen Hoa - Leaning on a railing to look at Yen Tu
> Leaning on the railing to watch the green mountains,
> The wind blew gently and gently.
> The swallow flew back to the South without sending a message,
> A long stretch of river with fast moving water.
> Ink pens for pages,
> Chu Dau ceramic glaze painted with green lotus.
> Pray that the mountains and rivers will be peaceful and free from enemies,
> Let him come back to me soon!

#### 17. Drunk on Hoa Am — Full Moon Night Tea Room
> Lotus tea marinated with ivory night dew,
> The hazy moonlight illuminates the silhouettes of people in the distance.
> Sixteen elegant silk threads,
> Pluck a song of love and send thousands of flowers.
> The curtains are thin and the wind is cold in the early autumn night,
> You are thinner than an apricot branch!

#### 18. Nostalgia — Dao Thinh Bronze Vase
> Admiring the engraved patterns on bronze jars,
> Ancient people pounded rice in mid-air.
> The Lac Bird flies around the sun and moon,
> Thousands of years later, the soul of the water is still bright and clear.

#### 19. The Sound of Piano at Midnight
> The rain falls gently on the western porch,
> Playing the string guitar with tired and thin fingers.
> The sound of silk vibrates with longing,
> Sent to the border with wind and clouds.

#### 20. Drawing a Lotus at the Bottom of a Vase
> Each stroke of the brush draws a fresh lotus flower,
> Turquoise glaze radiates a smile.
> Even though thousands of degrees of red fire burn,
> The color of ceramics has not changed for thousands of years.

#### 21. Bowl of Hot Soup Waiting for the Night
> A charcoal stove warms a pot of fragrant soup,
> Waiting for his shadow to return to the sunset.
> The north wind outside is bitterly cold,
> Family love is warm and full of love.

#### 22. Tears of Love
> Missing you, tears fall down the page of poetry,
> The ink blurs away the dreamy shadow.
> Pray for a love that lasts for thousands of lifetimes,
> Like crane wings gliding through the mist.

#### 23. Ceramic Grinding Hand
> The mud and dirt of the Teacher's Sutra through my fingers,
> Turn into a treasure of the same month and day.
> My heart entrusts every grain of earth,
> Make life beautiful with passion.

#### 24. Blue Enamel Hair Brooch
> Flower bud hair brooch,
> Standing waiting for someone's boat to part the bamboo grove.
> The afternoon sun shines on the silhouette of a village girl,
> Waiting with all my heart for the day I return.
