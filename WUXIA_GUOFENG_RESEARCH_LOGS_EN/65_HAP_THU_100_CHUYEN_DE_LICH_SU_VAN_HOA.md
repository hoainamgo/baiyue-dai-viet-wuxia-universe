## II. UPGRADED KNOWLEDGE CODEX

### 🏛️ 1. SUBFAMILY: BACH VIET & EARLY EUROPE (III century BC)

* **Qin – Yue War (221 BC – 214 BC):**
 - *Field survey:* The 5-line march of 500,000 Qin troops was led by Do Thu and Su Loc. The excavation of Linh Cu canal (connecting Tuong and Ly rivers).
 - *Character history:* Dich Hu Tong (Leader of Tay Au who died in battle), Thuc Phan (Leader of the Au Viet - Lac Viet alliance retreated into the forest to fight guerrillas at night, shooting a crossbow to kill Do Thu).
 - *Co Phong application:* Portraying the military identity of "Short control of the field", ambushes at night on rivers, reed crossbows poisoned with soapberry.
* **Co Loa Citadel & Lien Chau Crossbow Industry:**
 - *Architectural survey:* Co Loa Citadel is 500 hectares with 3 spiral citadels (Inner Citadel - Central Citadel - Outer Citadel), a moat connecting the Hoang Giang River.
 - *Military technology:* Cao Lo's crossbow that shoots many bronze arrows at the same time; Tens of thousands of Dong Son three-sided bronze arrows unearthed at Cau Vuc.

### 👑 2. SUB-FYM: HOA LU ERA — Dinh & Le Dynasty (968 – 1009)

*(Filling an extremely important historical gap before the Ly - Tran dynasties in the old Master Codex)*

* **Van Thang Vuong Dinh Bo Linh & Suppressing the 12 Warlords:**
 - *Strategic terrain:* Thung Lau base, Gia Vien cave, and dangerous closed Hoa Lu limestone valley; Strategic skills depend on the swampy terrain of rivers and streams.
 - *5 Queens Regime:* Dan Gia, Trinh Minh, Kieu Quoc, Co Quoc, Ca Ong - demonstrates the political alliance and reconciliation with the local noble families (Duong Van Nga, daughter of Ngo Quyen...).
* **Previous Le Dynasty & Dai Hanh Emperor Le Hoan:**
 - *Brilliant martial arts:* In 981, destroyed the Song army on the Bach Dang and Tay Ket rivers; In 982, the south conquered Champa and maintained the border.
 - *Tich Dien field plowing ceremony:* The king himself went down to plow the fields at the beginning of the year at Doi Son (Ha Nam), a sacred symbol of the blood-and-blood bond between the king and wet rice civilization.

### 🏺 3. SUBDIVISION: LUONG QUANG ARCHEOLOGY & ANCIENT TOMB OF TRIEU VAN DE (1983)

* **Artifacts of the ancient Bach Viet site:**
 - *Xanggang Tomb (Guangzhou):* Excavated in 1983, the tomb of Trieu Mat (Emperor Trieu Van - Trieu Da's nephew).
 - *Treasures revealed:* Golden scrolled dragon seal *"Van De Hanh Ty"*, jade shroud strung with silk, hundreds of Dong Son musical instruments, bronze bells, bronze cauldrons with Bach Viet - Han mixed patterns.
 - *Value for the Worldview:* Clarifying the geographical boundaries and maritime trade space of ancient Vietnamese residents from Mesopotamia to the Red River Delta.
