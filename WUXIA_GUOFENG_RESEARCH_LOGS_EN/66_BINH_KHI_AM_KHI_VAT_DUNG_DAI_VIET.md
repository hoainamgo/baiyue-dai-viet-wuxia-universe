## 🗡️ PART I: DAI VIET CLEAR COMBAT & REAL COMBAT WEAPON SYSTEM

### 1. Thuan Viet Sword (Dong Son Sword & Dragon Snake Truong Sword):
- Dong Son Bronze Sword (5th century BC - 1st century AD): The hilt is carved in the image of noble women or warriors carrying each other, short T-shaped hand guard, double-edged blade shaped like a tapered bamboo bud, cast in solid bronze.
- Dai Viet Long Sword (Ly - Tran Dynasties): The thin, flexible but hard blade is tempered with bamboo charcoal and jade well water. The hilt is covered with stingray leather or rattan, carved with lotus flowers, leaves or chrysanthemums. The case is made of wood or lacquer inlaid with conch.
- Bach Viet Short Sword: About 40-50cm long, specialized for close combat, hidden in sleeves or medicine bags.

### 2. Melee & Battlefield Knife System:
- Short Dao Tam Ma (Shui Chien Dao): The sword's spine is thick, the blade is slightly curved at the tip of the knife, the sturdy ironwood handle has an iron ring at the end of the handle to tie the wrist strap to avoid slipping when immersed in water.
- Truong Dao Dong A (Infantry Anti-Cavalry Great Knife): 1m6 - 1m8 wooden handle covered with bronze, heavy and hand-tied blade, designed to focus on the tip of the knife to break armor and cut the legs of Thoat Hoan cavalry horses.
- Dragon Scale Knife (Yem Nguyet Dao Thuan Viet): Not flamboyantly curved like the Three Kingdoms Quan Dao, but streamlined, optimizing the swinging speed according to the wavy posture of the sea monster.

### 3. Spear, Halberd & Spear Armor:
- Go Liem Giao (Dong A Specialty of Breaking Ma Battle): The tip of the spear is long and sharp, combined with a hooked blade bent in reverse. Effect: hooking the horse's ankle, tearing off the armor strap or pulling the rider off the saddle.
- Dong Son Sugarcane Bud Spear: The tip of the spear is made of bronze or cold iron and has a wide flat sugarcane leaf shape, creating deep wounds and causing rapid blood loss.
- Dinh Ba Thuy Quan: Three pointed prongs used to launch large fish or to catch spies jumping into the river to escape.

### 4. Battle Ax & Smashing Weapons (Blunt Weapons):
- Dong Son Sandalwood Ax: Unique comedy-shaped ax head, pointed tip can both chop and pierce wooden shields.
- Scaled Ax: The ax blade expands into a fan shape, engraved with patterns of Lac birds flying and brave men rowing boats.
- Copper Mace & Iron-Coated Tam Vong Cone: Male bamboo cone (tam vong) dried and soaked in mud is as tough as steel, the copper tip is grooved to break the opponent's cone.

## 🎯 PART II: DARK WEAPONS & RANGE WEAPONS OF BACH VIET

### 1. Lien Chau Magic Crossbow & Bach Viet Horned Crossbow:
- Lien Chau Co Loa crossbow: Exquisite bronze cast crossbow lever, each time the trigger is pulled, a series of three-sided bronze arrows are released (Cau Vuc - Co Loa arrows). The penetrating power penetrates leather armor and ironwood shields at a distance of 100 steps.
- Strawberry Wood Crossbow with Bamboo Pressed with Buffalo Horn: The crossbow arm is light, mobile, the crossbow string is made of buffalo tendons soaked in beeswax, used for silent sniping in the jungle canopy.

### 2. Dark Qi Poison & Hidden Manipulation:
- Swallow Wing Dart (Yen Vi Tieu): Thin piece of cold steel sharpened at both ends, shaped like a swallow's tail, flying in circles without sound.
- Bamboo Pipe Blowing Toxic Poison (Bamboo Shooting Arrow): Use air to blow needles impregnated with Sui tree resin (Wood Arrow) or cobra venom. Hitting the flesh paralyzes the heart for three breaths.
- Arrows Impregnated with Sap and Palm Leaves: Field arrows of hunters and insurgents in the mountains and forests.
- Bag of Horse's Eye Spicy Lime Powder: Finely ground quicklime mixed with dried chili and wild pepper, thrown into the wind to destroy the enemy cavalry formation.

## 🛡️ PART III: ARMOR & WEAPONS OF DAI VIET MARINE WARFARE

1. Ho Tam Phien Dong Son: Round or square cast bronze plate on the chest and back, connected by a badger leather belt, engraved with sun and Lac bird patterns.
2. Chopstick Scale Buffalo Leather Armor (Tran Dynasty): The supple buffalo leather leaves are sewn in layers like bronze fish scales. Lightweight, resistant to salt water, does not rust when swimming and diving in rivers.
3. Rattan Shield Covered with Rhinoceros Skin: Light shield with elliptical or teardrop shape, gently blocking crossbow arrows and sword slashes.
4. Bach Dang Stake Beach: Ironwood and bamboo stakes with pointed ends, covered with iron, tilted at a 45-degree angle to catch the receding tide, piercing the hull of the Mong Nguyen frigate.

## APPENDIX — FORGING WORKSHOP — MAINTENANCE — BATTLEFIELD OBJECTS

### 1. Da Si Blacksmithing Workshop — Chu Dau (real process for writing knife forging scenes)

- **Oven:** bamboo charcoal + bamboo fan; Green fire is enough. The worker looks at the color of the fire to judge the heat: young red, medium yellow, old white - An Thai sword forged in yellow fire.
- **Strike:** 999 hammer blows for 1 short blade; Beat and sprinkle bamboo charcoal. The sound of the hammer "clang - clang" is like the rhythm of a drum - the foreman sings vi miles to the beat of the hammer.
- **Me:** dipped my red tongue into the water of the jade well (laterite well, cold water all year round) — heard "sizzling", white smoke rising. I am 3 times: hard - flexible - sharp.
- **Sharpening:** stream grinding stone, one-way forward grinding; Test by cutting the banana: only when cutting the banana will it be sweet and not fibrous.
- **Handle:** Peach wood soaked in mud for 1 year, glued with buffalo leather, wrapped with rattan; Engrave the word SAT THAT on the hilt before handing the knife.

### 2. Maintenance of military weapons (details of An Thai's daily life)

Every night, I wipe my sword with a cloth soaked in caulking oil; Check the blade under the light of a torch — if you chip a tooth, it will be sharpened immediately. Wristbands are changed every month (sweat rots). The scabbard is dried in the sun to prevent mold. The crossbow is lubricated and the crossbow string is removed when crossing the river. These scenes are the "breath" of true martial arts stories - the hero must also clean his sword like a farmer wipes his hoe.
