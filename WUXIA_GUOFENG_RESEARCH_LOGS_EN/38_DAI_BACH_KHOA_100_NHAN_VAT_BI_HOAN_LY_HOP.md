## 🌾 BLOCK I: BACH ART MASTERS & WORKING FOLK (20 CHARACTERS)

### 01. Moc Than Lo (Father of Moc Tinh Lan · Chu Dau Pottery Village)
* **Background & Profession:** Great artisan firing blue glazed ceramics and brown floral ceramics for 40 years.
* **Bi - Hoan - Ly - Hop:** He once lost his gentle wife in the great drought and crop failure of the Kinh Thay River (**Bi**). He devoted all his love to the rooster to raise his children, teaching Tinh Lan to shape each ceramic bowl (**Hoan**). The day I sent my daughter off to Yen Tu to study medicine, I shed tears of separation (**Ly**). Upon triumph, father and son hugged each other next to the brightly lit pottery kiln (**Hop**).
* **Philosophy of Love & Virtue:** *"Earth that passes through a thousand degrees of fire becomes a pearl, only those who pass through trials can truly love. Enamel may crack, but a parent's love for their child never fades!"*
* **Souvenir:** Hundred-year-old ironwood pottery wheel.

### 02. Nguyen Kim Duc (Great Artist of Dong Son Bronze Casting)
* **Personality & Occupation:** The master of casting melted wax and determining the sound of the Dong Son Ngoc Lu Drum.
* **Bi - Hoan - Ly - Hop:** 7 failed drum castings, exhausted family fortune and laughed at by people (**Bi**). With the support of the villagers who gathered each bronze bracelet, he successfully cast a magical Dong Son Drum that echoed 100 miles away (**Hoan**). Saying goodbye to my younger brother who went to reclaim the South (**Ly**). At the age of 80, I met my grandchildren again at the foot of the bronze jar (**Hop**).
* **Philosophy:** Sincerity is not arrogant, let the sound of the drum be the beat to unite all people.
* **Souvenir:** Bronze hammer engraved with the image of a Lac bird.

### 03. Mrs. Chieu Lua Nuong (Master of Brocade Weaving Van Phuc)
* **Occupation & Occupation:** Master of weaving thin silk with cicada wings dyed brown.
* **Bi - Hoan - Ly - Hop:** Her husband sacrificed his life when she was only 18 years old (**Bi**). Turning pain into threads to weave warm clothes for elderly mothers and wounded soldiers (**Hoan**). Sending students off to all four directions to teach their craft (**Ly**). Old age peacefully looks at the whole Van Phuc village bustling with the sound of the shuttle (**Hop**).
* **Philosophy:** Love does not seek personal gain, taking the warmth of others as its own joy.
* **Souvenir:** Golden jackfruit wooden loom.

### 04. Mr. Nguyen Da Hoi (Old Dong A Swordsmith)
* **Occupation & Occupation:** Master of cold forging 3-layer steel rice leaf short swords.
* **Bi - Hoan - Ly - Hop:** The only son fell at Bach Dang wharf (**Bi**). He did not hold grudges, day and night he used a hammer to forge 1,000 plowshares to give to the poor (**Hoan**). Left his homeland to pick up medicine and forge agricultural tools on the frontier (**Ly**). Meeting the old insurgents and drinking wine to celebrate peace (**Hop**).
* **Philosophy:** Sharp swords protect peace, do not breed hatred, devote yourself to a great cause.
* **Souvenir:** Steel anvil heated with 1,000 batches of coal.

### 05. Old Ngu Ba Ben (The Luc Dau Giang Ferryman)
* **Personality & Occupation:** The old farmer took the ferry across three dangerous rivers.
* **Bi - Hoan - Ly - Hop:** The whole family was robbed by robbers and burned down their bamboo boat (**Bi**). He saved the lives of hundreds of children and transported the poor for free for 50 years (**Hoan**). Witnessed so many sad separations at the water wharf (**Ly**). On the day of victory, we welcomed the triumphant army in radiant tears (**Hop**).
* **Philosophy:** Truong means carrying people across stormy rivers, regardless of salary, completely generous.
* **Souvenir:** The wooden oar has become worn and worn over the years.

### 06. Lady Applying Bell Hats (Ba Tam Hat Stitching Artist)
* **Personality & Occupation:** A village girl from Chuong village sews hats with straps to protect her from the sun and rain.
* **Bi - Hoan - Ly - Hop:** Orphaned at a young age, living on the help of neighbors (**Bi**). Skillfully sew each hat to give to poor brides (**Hoan**). The loved one went to the army far away and had no news for 10 years (**Ly**). The day he returned with his old conical hat still intact (**Hop**).
* **Philosophy:** Loyalty never fades over time.
* **Souvenir:** Silver sewing needle encrusted with mother-of-pearl.

### 07. Mr. Bui Nong Dien (Old Farmer Embanking the Red River)
* **Background & Occupation:** A person who spent his entire life building banks to prevent floods and preserving the golden sticky rice flower.
* **Bi - Hoan - Ly - Hop:** Big flood swept away crops and houses (**Bi**). He and the villagers worked together to dig soil and rebuild the strong dike body (**Hoan**). Saying goodbye to friends who fell in the storm and flood (**Ly**). Welcoming a bumper harvest and offering new rice to Heaven (**Hop**).
* **Philosophy:** Persistently protecting the community, love for motherland never dries up.
* **Souvenir:** Pair of bamboo baskets carrying soil to build dikes.

### 08. Thach Moc Chang Son (Wood Carving Artist)
* **Occupation & Occupation:** Carpenter sculpts rafters and columns in the shape of dragon, phoenix and lotus.
* **Bi - Hoan - Ly - Hop:** His arms were broken in an accident building a communal house (**Bi**). Thanks to the love and care of his gentle wife, he restored and carved a masterpiece of a communal house (**Hoan**). Away from home, he built the One Pillar Pagoda (**Ly**). Return to hug your wife and children under the roof of the three-room house (**Hop**).
* **Philosophy:** The love between husband and wife and suffering together overcomes all adversities.
* **Souvenir:** Sharp forged steel chisel.

### 09. Miss Tam Bai Dau (Female Mulberry Farmer Raising Silkworms)
* **Background & Occupation:** A young woman on the mudflats of the Day River, tending silkworms to make a living.
* **Bi - Hoan - Ly - Hop:** Silkworms died because of frost, losing the entire silk crop (**Bi**). Women in the village share each new mulberry leaf (**Hoan**). Sending my lover off to trade ceramics on Cu Lao Cham (**Ly**). Coinciding with the village festival of offering silk shirts to give thanks (**Hop**).
* **Philosophy:** Neighborly love is mutual love, sharing sweets and sharing joys.
* **Souvenir:** Silkworms woven from bamboo.

### 10. Old Stonemason Ninh Van (Master of Pagoda Thap Stone Carving)
* **Occupation & Occupation:** Stone chisel carved dragons on the palace steps and the Heavenly Altar.
* **Bi - Hoan - Ly - Hop:** Blind in one eye because of a flying piece of rock (**Bi**). By feeling his hands, he carved vivid stone dragon terraces for thousands of years (**Hoan**). Away from home to find the Yen Tu jade stone (**Ly**). Completion of the Altar of Heaven gives thanks to God for his children and grandchildren (**Hop**).
* **Philosophy:** Iron steadfastness, selfless love of art without boasting.
* **Souvenirs:** Sledgehammer and marble chisel.

## 🌿 BLOCK II: HERBAL DOCTOR, MEDICINE & MEDITATION MASTER (20 CHARACTERS)

### 21. Tue Tinh Zen Master (Holy Patriarch Nam Duoc)
* **Personality:** Eminent Zen master and physician, author of *The Divine Effect of Southern Medicine*.
* **Bi - Hoan - Ly - Hop:** Having to go on a mission to the North thousands of miles from home (**Bi** / **Ly**). Using medical techniques to save the lives of tens of thousands of poor people, he was honored as the Great Physician King (**Hoan**). Willing to send a handful of homeland land to the South (**Consistent** in the nation's mind).
* **Philosophy:** *"Men's Medicine Treats Men"* — Love has no borders, saving people without seeking fame and profit.
* **Souvenir:** Duffel bag containing 580 herbal medicinal seeds.

### 22. Diep Thanh Tam (The Lady of the Lotus Dress Tay Ho)
* **Personality:** A young girl who picks lotus and marinates tea and is also a battlefield ambulance doctor.
* **Bi - Hoan - Ly - Hop:** Lost his parents in the war, set up a tent alone by the lotus pond (**Bi**). Save the life of a seriously injured warrior with lotus heart and betel leaves (**Hoan**). Send him off to war with a tea bag filled with dew (**Ly**). Let's meet again when the country is clear of enemies, let's row the boat to enjoy the moon (**Hop**).
* **Philosophy:** Kindness is as gentle as a lotus flower blooming in the mud.
* **Souvenir:** Terracotta pot for lotus tea filled with dew.

### 23. Zen Master Huyen Giac (Head of Ngoa Van Yen Tu Monastery)
* **Personality:** Taoist hermit, combining meditation and 9-burnt bamboo sword.
* **Bi - Hoan - Ly - Hop:** Having experienced the pain of watching friends fall into the cycle of fame and fortune killing each other (**Bi**). Climbing to the top of the mountain, he realized impermanence and opened the door to help the homeless (**Hoan**). Sending the disciple off the mountain to help people (**Ly**). Smiling and passing away amidst the pure sound of the bell (**Compatible** with heaven and earth).
* **Philosophy:** Immense love does not make you arrogant, it is as quiet as space.
* **Souvenirs:** 9-burnt bamboo sword and ancient bronze bell.

### 24. Old Medicine Man Ma Pi Leng (The Mountain Medicine Picker)
* **Personality:** An old Mong man climbed a cliff to pick Panax notoginseng and wild ginseng.
* **Bi - Hoan - Ly - Hop:** His wife and children passed away from an epidemic when he had not yet found the precious medicinal plant (**Bi**). Determined to cling to the cliffs all his life to find medicine to save the village from the cholera epidemic (**Hoan**). Living alone in the mist of the border (**Ly**). They are considered by the villagers as biological parents who reunite on Tet (**Hop**).
* **Philosophy:** Sacrifice yourself for the lives of all people, be generous and chivalrous.
* **Souvenirs:** Tree bark ropes and bamboo climbing baskets.

### 25. Nun Dieu Khong (Dau Co Tu Pagoda)
* **Personality:** A nun who raised 30 war orphans.
* **Bi - Hoan - Ly - Hop:** Watching the village fall apart after the enemy (**Bi**). Take each hungry child back to the temple to cook porridge to raise them (**Hoan**). Each child grew up and left the temple to build the country (**Ly**). On the full moon day, people gather at the pagoda to worship their gentle mother (**Hop**).
* **Philosophy:** Motherly love is not related by blood, love never fades.
* **Souvenirs:** Jackfruit wood and copper pot for cooking porridge for charity.

*(... With 15 Physicians & Zen people: Acupuncturist of Cu Da Village, Female Herbal Nurse of Song Luoc, Seven Mountain Snake Treatment Master, Nui Dinh Southern Medicine Girl, Song Da Qi Training Taoist, Old Monk Cooking Water for Ten Thousand Lives, Fishing Village Midwife, Boy Pounding Squid Grass to Stop Bleeding, Am May Yen Phu Nun, Wrestler Bone and Joint Doctor, etc.).*

## 📜 BLOCK III: WRITERS, POETS, MASTERS & SINGERS (20 CHARACTERS)

### 41. Tran Han Thi Tien (Nam Thien Thi Tien)
* **Personality:** The madman recited poetry, carried a sword, played the Day Dan, and became a poet.
* **Bi - Hoan - Ly - Hop:** Being harmed by a treacherous mandarin must resign (**Bi**). Found freedom among the mountains and rivers, friends with Moc Tinh Lan under the moon (**Hoan**). Say goodbye to your loved one and enter the naval battle of Luc Dau (**Ly**). Meet again, shake off your weathered clothes on the veranda of the tea room (**Hop**).
* **Philosophy:** True love is not jealous of life, generous and righteous.
* **Souvenirs:** Bamboo wine gourd decorated with silver and mahogany bottom instrument.

### 42. Master Do Gia Song Ma (Teacher Who Teach Village Literacy)
* **Personality:** Poor Confucian students open a class to teach Nom and human ethics under thatched roofs.
* **Bi - Hoan - Ly - Hop:** Lost his young wife, taught alone without charging tuition (**Bi**). The student passed the poinsettia and brought glory to his homeland (**Hoan**). Students go to far away places and look forward every day (**Ly**). On the day of honoring the ancestors, the students knelt down and thanked the teacher, choking up (**Hop**).
* **Philosophy:** Devoted to the next generation, grateful to a noble teacher without personal gain.
* **Souvenirs:** Black stone inkstone and rabbit quill pen tip.

### 43. Lady Cam Ca Song Hoai (Singing Girl Who Keeps Her Heart Pure)
* **Personality:** Young woman strumming the Day instrument and singing ca tru on Hoi An riverbank.
* **Bi - Hoan - Ly - Hop:** Forced to marry a rich but cruel businessman (**Bi**). Determined to maintain chastity, singing to soothe the pain of merchants (**Hoan**). The soulmate went on a merchant ship across the ocean and disappeared (**Ly**). The sound of the guitar mixed with the sound of his flute on the day he returned to Hoai wharf (**Hop**).
* **Philosophy:** A noble soul does not care about gold or silver, full of fidelity.
* **Souvenir:** A pair of old bamboo beats beating the bow and arrow.

### 44. Academician Le Van Su (Academy's History Officer)
* **Personality:** A person who spent his life recording honest history and engraving poems on the cliffs of Bai Tho mountain.
* **Bi - Hoan - Ly - Hop:** Threatened to be beheaded if he did not correct historical records praising the crooks (**Bi**). I would rather die than keep my pen and mind, so King Tran restored his position (**Hoan**). Away from home, he traveled all over the caves and borders to collect 5,000 sets of ancient books (**Ly**). Complete the Tripitaka and offer it to the country to reunite with its children and grandchildren (**Hop**).
* **Philosophy:** Unwavering honesty, loving the truth more than life.
* **Souvenirs:** Knife carved with words on stone and silk history book.

### 45. Muse of Tay Do Silk Dress (Female Folk Song Composer)
* **Personality:** Talented woman who specializes in collecting her mother's lullabies and writing them into poetry.
* **Bi - Hoan - Ly - Hop:** Her mother died early before she could repay her (**Bi**). Bringing love into hundreds of lullabies for all people (**Hoan**). Saying goodbye to childhood friends (**Ly**). Her song is hummed throughout the caves and alleys (**Hop**).
* **Philosophy:** Love spreads to all generations, poetry is for humanity.
* **Souvenir:** Book of poems handwritten on Do paper.

## ⚔️ BLOCK IV: KNIGHTS, WARRIORS & MARTS (20 CHARACTERS)

### 61. An Thai (Long Tiep Quan Do Hiep · Dung Si Sa Truong)
* **Personality:** Young naval admiral Luc Dau Giang, knight wielding rice leaves.
* **Bi - Hoan - Ly - Hop:** Losing teammates in the bloody battle of Bach Dang underground stake (**Bi**). Together with the generals and soldiers, we defeated the enemy to protect the commune (**Hoan**). Leaving Moc Tinh Lan, entering the field of fire and smoke (**Ly**). Returning to Chu Dau pottery village, holding tightly the hand of your lifelong lover (**Hop**).
* **Philosophy:** Swinging the sword to protect the weak, loyal love never fades, courage and humility.
* **Souvenirs:** Cold forged rice leaf short knife and broken blue flower ceramic piece.

### 62. Yet Kieu Dung Si (Great God of Water and Destroying Ships)
* **Personality:** The man from Ha Bi village swam and dived like an otter, punching holes in enemy boats.
* **Bi - Hoan - Ly - Hop:** Arrested by the enemy, brutally tortured and forced to open an underground stakeout (**Bi**). Tried to jump into the river and swim back to inform the general (**Hoan**). Far away from my old mother in my hometown and into the deep river bed (**Ly**). Triumphant returned to hug his mother next to the pot of rice he had just offered to Heaven (**Hop**).
* **Philosophy:** Loyal and patriotic, loyal and generous, not afraid of death.
* **Souvenirs:** Steel boat chisel and leather diving belt.

### 63. Wrestler Lieu Khai Van (Master of Lieu Doi Wrestling)
* **Personality:** First folk wrestler, inventor of the "choke hold" move.
* **Bi - Hoan - Ly - Hop:** Severely injured while saving villagers from a fierce tiger (**Bi**). Trained hard to recover, opened a free wrestling arena to teach young men (**Hoan**). Saying goodbye to the students who are going to join the army of Sat That (**Ly**). The victorious student returned with the old man to raise a betel bowl to celebrate the festival (**Hop**).
* **Philosophy:** Martial arts are humane, win without arrogance, lose without discouragement, give your food and clothes to your peers.
* **Souvenir:** Red loincloth with elephant skin belt and bronze.

### 64. Bui Thuy Loi (Captain of Van Kiep Crossbow)
* **Origin:** Three-pronged magic crossbow marksman inheriting Cao Lo's technique.
* **Bi - Hoan - Ly - Hop:** Once blamed himself because an arrow missed and caused the loss of the stronghold (**Bi**). Hard training day and night to hit the bull's eye in the dark, shoot down enemy generals to save the citadel (**Hoan**). Away from his newlywed wife to the front line (**Ly**). Returned to the crossbow station, and with his wife planted corn fields along the river (**Hop**).
* **Philosophy:** Strength is associated with the responsibility to protect peace for all people.
* **Souvenir:** 3-pronged black buffalo horn golden crossbow.

### 65. Doan Dung Ky General (Commander of the Indigo Cavalry)
* **Personality:** Young general commanding a cavalry team riding horses across the Northwest mountains.
* **Bi - Hoan - Ly - Hop:** Lost his loyal war horse in the Mau Son blizzard (**Bi**). Captured the handsome horse leader, led the cavalry to raid the enemy's warehouse, saving thousands of people from starvation (**Hoan**). Saying goodbye to the family guarding the Chi Lang border (**Ly**). Meeting my old mother again at the village gate on the day the country triumphed (**Hop**).
* **Philosophy:** High-spirited, heroic, self-sacrificing for the people, disregarding dangers.
* **Souvenirs:** A horse saddle covered with tiger skin and a 2-foot-long bronze spear.

*(... With 15 Knights & Braves: Female General Me Linh Statue, Torch Boy to Break the Siege, Day River Rescue Knight, Mai Dong Wrestler, Bamboo Forest Archer, Sword Bearing Hero to Save Friends, Horse Archery Female Knight, Con Dao Pearl Diving Guy, Border Marker Guard, etc.).*

## 👑 BLOCK V: ROYALTY, GENERAL, MERCHANT & PERSONNEL (20 CHARACTERS)

### 81. Marquis Hoai Van Tran Quoc Toan (Eternal Spirit of Youth)
* **Personality:** The most courageous 16-year-old royal teenager in history.
* **Bi - Hoan - Ly - Hop:** Angrily crushed the orange because he was young and could not attend the military affairs discussion at the Binh Than conference (**Bi**). Self-recruited 1,000 soldiers to embroider the flag with 6 golden letters *"Breaking Cuong Dich, Reporting Hoang An"*, winning every fight (**Hoan**). Saying goodbye to my old mother and going to the front line (**Ly**). His name lives forever in the hearts of millions of Vietnamese people (**Hop** is immortal).
* **Philosophy:** Passionate patriotism, no pride and arrogance, pouring out youthful enthusiasm for the country.
* **Souvenirs:** Flag embroidered with 6 golden letters and command sword.

### 82. Princess Huyen Tran (Heart for Great Causes and Rivers)
* **Personality:** Princess Dai Viet shouldered the mission of peace in the South.
* **Bi - Hoan - Ly - Hop:** Wiped away tears and said goodbye to the prosperous capital of Thang Long to the South to become a bride (**Bi** / **Ly**). Bringing culture, wet rice agriculture and love to reconcile the hatred of the two peoples, expanding the territory to the two continents of O and Ri (**Hoan**). Rescued by the general, he returned to the Truc Lam Buddhist realm to practice (**Hop** peacefully).
* **Philosophy:** Virtue makes noble sacrifices for the greater good, immense love soothes all wars.
* **Souvenirs:** The jade lotus brooch and the book of poems saying goodbye to the homeland.

### 83. Old Mother Who Raised the Nghia Quan (Mother of Yen The Forest Land)
* **Personality:** The poor old mother dug a secret tunnel to raise a martyr.
* **Bi - Hoan - Ly - Hop:** 3 biological sons all sacrificed for the country (**Bi**). Adopted dozens of insurgents as children, gave each potato and cassava to feed the troops (**Hoan**). Sending off my adopted children to war every rainy night (**Ly**). On the day of victory, the adopted children returned and knelt down and called out, "Mom!" (**Matching** radiant).
* **Philosophy:** The love of a Vietnamese mother: never demanding, unconditionally sacrificing, as vast as the ocean and sky.
* **Souvenirs:** The base of the clay rice cooker and the oil lamp that illuminates the secret tunnel.

### 84. Merchant Bui Dai Nghia (Owner of East Sea Ships)
* **Personality:** Rich businessman who owns 20 sailboats doing international trade in Hoi An.
* **Bi - Hoan - Ly - Hop:** The boat was sunk by a storm and all of its property was lost (**Bi**). Thanks to maintaining trust, international friends lent capital to build a larger business (**Hoan**). Been away from my wife and children at sea for many months and days (**Ly**). Opened a silver grain warehouse to relieve famine for the entire Luc Dau region in flood disaster, the whole family gathered together in the blessings of all people (**Hop**).
* **Philosophy:** Money is a means to help people, to be generous, to truly enrich one's life.
* **Souvenirs:** Antique bronze compass and trust notebook.

### 85. Elderly Couple Planting Trees to Create a Forest (Mau Son Peak)
* **Personality:** The elderly couple has been planting pine forests for 70 years and preserving spring water for the village.
* **Bi - Hoan - Ly - Hop:** Experiencing war, poverty and loss (**Bi**). Together, we dig each hole in the ground and plant a vast green forest (**Hoan**). The children went away to settle down in a distant land (**Ly**). Holding hands and sitting on the porch, watching the forest protect our children and grandchildren forever (**Complete**).
* **Philosophy:** *"Love never fades"* — Living for nature, for the future of the race.
* **Souvenirs:** A pair of worn-out hoes and the stump of the first pine tree planted 50 years ago.
