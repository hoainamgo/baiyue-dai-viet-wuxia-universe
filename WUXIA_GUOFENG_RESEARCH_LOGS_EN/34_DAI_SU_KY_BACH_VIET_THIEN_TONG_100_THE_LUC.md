## 🗺️ I. ORIGIN "BACH VIET = 100 TRIBES / THOUSANDS OF FORCES":

The word **"BACH (百)"** in **Bach Viet** is not just a conventional number, but according to ancient books and archeology of the Yangtze River basin - Dong Dinh Ho:
* In the early days (5,000 - 3,000 years ago), from the south bank of the Yangtze River stretching down to the Gulf of Tonkin and the East Sea, there existed **thousands of clans, villages, guilds, cave owners and sea sects**, gathering into **100 Vietnamese Tribes (Bach Viet)**.
* Each tribe possesses its own unique martial arts, crafts, indigenous herbs, weapons and beliefs.

## 📜 II. MAP OF THE 8 GREAT REGIONS OF THE ANCIENT BACH VIET REGION (ORIGIN OF 100 TRIBES):

1. **Au Viet (Western Europe):** High mountains of Cao Bang, Lang Son, Guangxi $\rightarrow$ Traces: *The art of climbing cliffs, hunting falcons, grinding wooden longbows*.
2. **Lac Viet:** Red River Delta, Ma River, Ca River $\rightarrow$ Traces: *Short Knife of Rice Leaves, Lac Long Naval Battle, Dong Son Drum, Neck Leg Strike*.
3. **Min Viet (Fujian):** Coastal jungle $\rightarrow$ Traces: *The art of making herbal poisons, charms, deep forest stone traps*.
4. **Eastern Vietnam (Eastern Europe):** Zhejiang region, Qiantang river mouth $\rightarrow$ Traces: *Thousand-year silk weaving, elegant Bamboo Sword*.
5. **Yangyue (Yangtze River Basin):** Hunan Region, Jiangxi $\rightarrow$ Traces: *Ancient pottery craft, Shennong gave botanicals*.
6. **Nam Viet (Luong Quang):** La Phu mountain area, Linh Nam $\rightarrow$ Traces: *Medical doctor Thoi Vi practiced medicine, Agarwood eliminated poison*.
7. **Dien Viet (Yunnan):** Southwestern Plateau $\rightarrow$ Traces: *Metallurgy, casting bronze swords with human-shaped handles, taming elephants and horses*.
8. **Hai Viet (Hai Dao & Coastal):** East Coast Islands $\rightarrow$ Traces: *Conqueror Slaying Hai Dao, scuba diving to find pearls, taming orcas*.

## 🔍 III. TRACES OF "BACH VIET'S SECRET TRADITION" ARE STILL PRESERVED IN DAI VIET GIRLS:

Even through thousands of years of war and merger, 100 ancient forces have not disappeared but have transformed into 4 pillars of culture and martial arts:

### 1. Imprints in Martial Arts & Close Combat:
* **Giao Long (Lac Viet) Body Tattoo:** Relics from the ancient times of diving in the river to hunt without being bitten $\rightarrow$ Became the identifying mark of *Lac Long Thuy Phai* and *Sat That* martyrs.
* **Short Infantry Destroying Cavalry Technique (Au Viet & Lac Viet merged):** Using short techniques to cut off the legs of Northern cavalry horses $\rightarrow$ Predecessor of *21 foot blows to destroy Vovinam*.
* **Nhat Nam Het Phai (Traces of the ancient Vietnamese tribe of Ma River):** Uses sound to break the enemy's eardrum $\rightarrow$ The oldest remaining traditional martial art.

### 2. Traces of Crafts & Weapons Village:
* **Chu Dau Pottery (Remains of Duong Viet & Lac Viet):** The craft of blue-glazed, brown-flowered ceramics inherits the 5,000-year-old ceramic firing technique of Luong Chu $\rightarrow$ It is both an exported fine art product and also casts and hides short swords in the ceramic kiln.
* **Thieu Hoa Bronze Casting & Co Loa Iron Casting (Remains of Dien Viet & Lac Viet):** Brass smelting technique for casting magic crossbows to shoot ten thousand arrows and Dong Son drums to command soldiers.
* **Dong Xam Silver Carving (Legacy of Dong Viet):** The craft of making non-metal hidden acupuncture tools to save people.

### 3. Traces of Herbal Medicine (Duong Viet & Nam Viet):
* **"Shen Nong Ban Thao" & "Tue Tinh Than Phuong":** 580 herbal medicines growing wild in thousands of fields are the thousands of generations of medicinal plant tasting heritage of Bach Viet's ancestors.

### 4. Traces of Belief in Heavenly Sacrifice (Inherited from Noah):
* **Stone Incense Tree / Heavenly Altar in the Yard:** Although 100 ancient tribes are scattered everywhere, they all keep the same custom: **Set up an outdoor stone altar, offer a bowl of clear spring water & golden grains, look up to the sky and pray to God, absolutely DO NOT light incense sticks**.
