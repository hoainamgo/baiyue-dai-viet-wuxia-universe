## 🌾 PART III: WATER RICE CUSTOMS & DIFFERENT SOFT CULTURE

Wet rice civilization is the cradle that nurtures the entire soul, ethics and lifestyle of the residents of Bach Viet - Dai Viet:

---

### 1. ☀️ Agricultural Rituals & Sacrifices to Heaven and Earth
* **Tich Dien Ceremony (King Goes to Plow):** At the beginning of the first crop season of the year, the King himself went to the fields to plow the first furrows, showing his respect for agriculture and closeness to all people.
* **Water Procession & Rain Prayer Ceremony (Phap Vu):** Carry clear water from the middle of the Red River to the temple, offering prayers for favorable weather and rain to the Creator.
* **New Rice Ceremony (Thong Cuu/Ha Dien New Year):** Harvest the first yellow sticky rice flowers, blow fragrant sticky rice and offer it to the **Open Heaven Altar** to thank God for the bountiful harvest.

---

### 2. 🐉 The Custom of Dragon Tattooing & Eating Betel to Dye Your Teeth Black
* **Giao Long Tattoo Custom:** Right from the Hung Vuong to Tran dynasties, children in river areas all tattooed water dragons (Giao Long) on ​​their chests, backs and thighs so that when swimming and diving in the rivers and seas, they would not be harmed by sea monsters or dragons.
* **The Custom of Eating Betel and Phoenix Wings & Dying Teeth Black:** The betel piece is the beginning of the story, symbolizing the love between husband and wife, black teeth protect natural tooth enamel with cochineal resin.

---

### 3. 🍲 Ethnic Roots Cuisine
* **Banh Chung - Banh Giay:** Symbolizes the round Heaven and square Earth, crystallized from upland sticky rice seeds, green beans and homeland pork.
* **Grilled Lam Rice with Bamboo Tubes & Boiled Robusta Water:** The warm energy of junglers and knights in the field.
* **Western Lake Lotus Tea with Night Dew:** The elegant art of enjoying tea of ​​Truc Lam intellectuals and physicians.
