## 🏛️ PART I: THE "GOD" MIND — THE ONLY SUPREME BEING OF THE ANCIENT VIETNAMESE PEOPLE

Unlike the polytheistic religions introduced later, thousands of years before the Northern colonial period, in the original mind of the Lac Viet / Bach Viet people:
1. **"God" (Supreme Creator):**
   * Ancient Vietnamese people did not call Heaven an abstract "Heaven", but called it by an extremely loving but absolutely respectful title: **"GOD" (Oh God, Thank God, Thanks to God, God's Insight)**.
   * God is the One who gives birth to all things, controls the rain and wind, controls life, blesses and brings disaster, and is the symbol of **Absolute Justice** (*"Heaven has eyes"*, *"Heaven does not tolerate the world"*).
2. **Original Couple: "FATHER HEAVEN — MOTHER EARTH":**
   * Heaven is the Father (Yang, round, gives rain and light), Earth is the Mother (Yin, square, produces rice crops). The symbol of banh chung and banh giay is the embodiment of the religion of worshiping Father Heaven - Mother Earth.

## 🌿 PART II: HISTORICAL TRUTH — ANCIENT VIETNAMESE PEOPLE DID NOT LIGHT INCENSE

### 1. Origin of the Habit of Lighting Incense:
* Historical and archaeological research proves: **The custom of burning incense (incense sticks, incense sticks and ashes) is a custom imported from the North and Taoism/Buddhism during the Northern colonial period**.
* During the Hung King period and the time when the original country was founded, **Ancient Vietnamese people did not light incense sticks** when communicating with God.

### 2. How did the original Vietnamese Ceremony of Sacrificing Heaven take place?
* **Open Heaven Altar (No Cover):**
  * Ancient Vietnamese people believed: To worship God, one must **stand under the sky**, raise one's face up to the vast sky, and spiritually communicate directly with the sunlight or the starry night sky without any barrier separating it.
  * This is the origin of the custom of building **"Altar of Heaven"** (a stone incense tree in the yard, on top of a stone pillar) that has been passed down throughout Vietnamese villages to this day.
* **The Purest Sacrifice to Heaven:**
  * **A Bowl of Clear Spring Water:** Symbolizes absolute purity and purity of the soul.
  * **A Handful of Glutinous Rice Grains / Bowl of New Rice:** The most precious offering of wet rice residents to thank Heavenly Father for providing good rain and wind.
  * **A Nep Nuong Wine Gourd / Forest Fruit:** Sweet crystallization of Mother Earth.
* **Ritual "Looking Up to the Sun & Folding Hands to Pray":**
  * No knocking, no burning of votive paper, no incense smoke.
  * The man stood straight between heaven and earth, clasped his hands in front of his chest, looked up at the blue sky and spoke sincere words from the bottom of his heart: *"Dear God, please send good rain for the rice to bloom, please protect the villagers in peace"*.

## 🏛️ PART III: FROM FOLLOWER'S SACRIFICE TO HEAVEN TO THE PALACE'S "NAM Giao Sacrifice Ceremony"

1. **Concept of Nam Giao Altar (Sacrifice to Heaven and Earth):**
   * When the independent feudal state was formed (from the Ly, Tran, Le to Nguyen dynasties), the Dai Viet Emperors raised the level of folk worship of Heaven into **Dai Nam Giao Ceremony**.
   * The altar is built with 3 open-air floors: the top floor is circular (Te Heaven), the middle floor is square (Te Dat), and the bottom floor is octagonal (Te Nhan).
2. **Affirming Independence and Sovereignty:**
   * Only the Son of Heaven of an independent country with sovereignty equal to the Northern Emperor has the right to establish the Heavenly Altar $\rightarrow$ Proving that Dai Viet is an autonomous country and region of the Southern Heavens (*"Southern country, mountains and rivers, Southern emperors reside / Naturally destined in the book of heaven"*).
