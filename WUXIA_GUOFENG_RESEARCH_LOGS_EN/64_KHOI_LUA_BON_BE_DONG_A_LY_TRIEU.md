## ⚔️ II. DETAILS OF THE 4 HOSTILE FRONTS IN THE WORLDVIEW

### 1. 🌊 South: Kingdom of Champa (Champa)
- **Conflict area:** Hoanh Son range, Ngang pass, Nhat Le estuary, Gianh river, Ma Linh, O, Ly lands (Quang Binh, Quang Tri, Thua Thien Hue).
- **Military Characteristics & Tactics:** The Champa Navy is famous for its light, sharp-nosed warships that can surf fast waves, brave soldiers, and bows and arrows poisoned with forest bark.
- **History of fighting during the Ly - Tran dynasties:**
 - Ly Dynasty: Ly Thuong Kiet and the Ly kings repeatedly conquered peace to keep the South in peace.
 - Tran Dynasty: Champa once allied against Mongol Yuan (blocking Toa Do), but then border disputes broke out fiercely.
- **Artistic image:** Waves roaring at Nhat Le gate, dragon boats overcoming storms, Cham towers quiet under the moon, horn sounds mixing with the sound of ocean waves at night.

### 2. 🌲 West & Northwest: Abyss of Ai Lao Sacred Forest (Ancient Laos) & Border Mandarin Tribes
- **Conflict area:** Northwest high mountains, Da river basin, Ma Phi Phuc pass, Muong Lo region, Ai Lao border (Thanh Hoa, Nghe An bordering Laos).
- **Military characteristics:** Ambushes in deep canyons, jungle miasma (jungle fever), thousand-pound crossbows shooting poison arrows, riding war elephants through steep hills and mountains.
- **History of fighting:** King and I of the Tran Dynasty (Tran Anh Tong, Tran Minh Tong) and famous generals such as Doan Nhu Hai many times personally led troops to the western border to suppress the Ai Lao rebellion that invaded the village.
- **Artistic image:** The miasma of blue mist, the smoking cloudy pass, the sparse sound of rafts in the deep forest, the cold scales of armor next to the third-night fire.

### 3. ⛵ East: Austronesian Pirates (Java / Cha Va) & Far Away Sea Pirates
- **Location:** Northeast coast, Van Don commercial port, Cu Lao Cham, Bai Tu Long Bay.
- **Features:** Fierce groups of pirates sailed in large boats from the Southern islands to loot merchant ships of ceramics and silk.
- **History of fighting:** The Ly - Tran court had to establish the commercial port of Van Don and an elite coast guard force managed by famous generals (such as Nhan Hue Vuong Tran Khanh Du), build boats and destroy pirates protecting the maritime silk road (Cattigara).

### 4. 🛡️ North: Great Army Invades (Song & Yuan Mongols)
- **Location:** Nhu Nguyet River, Chi Lang Pass, Luc Dau Giang, Bach Dang Stake Yard.
- **Features:** Skilled cavalry, armored cavalry, overwhelming numbers.
- **Dai Viet's countermeasures:** The garden has no empty houses, so the school is short-lived, the leg is clamped around the neck, the naval war is underground, the spirit of *Sat That* agrees with the king and me.
