## 1. Material principles
- Bamboo, jackfruit wood, fig wood, volcanic stone, brass, horn, leather and silk are groups of materials that can be observed in ethnic groups and monuments.
- Durability, hardness, resonance and hiding properties must be investigated from the actual object, not inferred from representational images.

## 2. Ecological context
- Ancient musical instruments associated with forests, fields, seas, rivers and residential areas; Do not separate the instrument from its materials and methods of use.
- Traditional musical instrument making techniques are only publicly described; No manufacturing instructions or production recipes are included.

## 3. Contrast materials
- Wood gives a warm and durable sound; Bamboo gives bright sound; stone for dry percussion; Copper gives bells and drums resonance; dark silk for strings.
- Acoustic assessment only describes material properties, does not attach frequencies or psychological effects.

## 4. Nguyên tắc bảo tồn
- Ưu tiên tài liệu hoá nhạc cụ thật, kỹ thuật thợ làm, bối cảnh sử dụng và các biến thể theo vùng.
- Không mô tả trạng thái hiện vật được “đo” từ ảnh hoặc từ suy đoán.
