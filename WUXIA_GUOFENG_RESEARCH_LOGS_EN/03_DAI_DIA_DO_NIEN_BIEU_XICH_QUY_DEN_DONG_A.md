## 🏛️ DETAIL OF THE 9 HISTORICAL ERAS

---

### 🌊 1. XÍCH QUỶ & ANCIENT BAIYUE (5300 – 4000 BP)
* **Geographical Span:** Lake Dongting, Southern Yangtze river basin down to Lingnan.
* **Capital / Strategic Bases:** **Ancient Liangzhu City**, **Dongting Ancient Capital**.
* **Architectural Characteristics:**
  * Oldest large-scale rammed-earth hydraulic dam systems spanning tens of kilometers.
  * Elevated earthwork acropolis at Mojiaoshan with water-resistant ironwood pile foundations.
  * **Open-Air Stone Altars to Heaven** erected on elevated knolls to revere the Supreme Creator (*Ông Trời*) following the Great Deluge.
* **Mythological & Historical Figures:**
  * **Shennong (Viêm Đế):** Progenitor of wet-rice agriculture and discoverer of 580 indigenous medicinal herbs.
  * **Kinh Dương Vương (Lộc Tục):** Founding monarch of the Xích Quỷ confederation.
  * **Lạc Long Quân (Sùng Lãm) & Mother Âu Cơ:** Progenitors of the Baiyue hundred lineages ("Hundred Eggs").
* **Archaeology & Classical Literature:**
  * **Archaeology:** 656 engraved symbols on Liangzhu Jade Cong and Jade Bi; Hemudu wet-rice cultivation dating back 7,000 years.
  * **Literature:** Ancient *Song of the Yue Boatman (Việt Nhân Ca)*; *Mo Mường Epic (Birth of Earth and Water)*.

---

### 🌾 2. VĂN LANG & THE 18 HÙNG KINGS (4000 – 2300 BP)
* **Geographical Span:** The confluence of the Red, Đà, and Lô rivers at Bạch Hạc (Phú Thọ) down to the Red River delta.
* **Capital / Strategic Bases:** **Phong Châu Capital**, **Nghĩa Lĩnh Mountain Ceremonial Center (Mount Hùng)**, **Đông Sơn Village**.
* **Architectural Characteristics:**
  * **Boat-Roofed Timber Stilt Houses:** Carved with sacred *Chim Lạc* (flying cranes) and pounders, flood-resistant mortise-and-tenon wood frames.
  * **Outdoor Altar on Nghĩa Lĩnh Peak:** Open stone altar facing the river confluence to catch the sunrise.
* **Historical Figures & Heroes:**
  * **18 Generations of Hùng Kings.**
  * **Phù Đổng Heavenly King (Saint Gióng):** The iron-clad youth who uprooted golden bamboo to defeat invaders.
  * **Sơn Tinh (Lord of Tản Viên):** Master of flood-control dykes, symbol of resilience against natural calamities.
  * **Tiên Dung & Chử Đồng Tử:** Pioneers of free riverine trade in the Dạ Trạch wetlands.
* **Archaeology & Poetry:**
  * **Archaeology:** Phùng Nguyên, Đồng Đậu, and Gò Mun sites; Thousands of Dong Son bronze drums (Ngọc Lũ, Hoàng Hạ).

---

### 🌀 3. ÂU LẠC & AN DƯƠNG VƯƠNG (3RD CENTURY BC)
* **Geographical Span:** Cổ Loa alluvial plains (Đông Anh, Hanoi).
* **Capital / Strategic Bases:** **Cổ Loa Citadel (Spiral Fortress)**.
* **Architectural Characteristics:**
  * **9-Layer Spiral Rammed-Earth Walls:** Built with clay mixed with pebble gravel and broken ceramic shards, surrounded by water moats connecting to the Hoàng River.
* **Historical Figures:**
  * **Thục Phán An Dương Vương:** Unified Âu Việt and Lạc Việt to establish the Âu Lạc state.
  * **General Cao Lỗ:** Master military engineer who invented the **Repeating Bronze Crossbow**.
  * **Princess Mỵ Châu & Prince Trọng Thủy:** The tragic saga of national vigilance.
* **Archaeology:** Tens of thousands of **three-winged bronze arrowheads** excavated at Cầu Vực, Cổ Loa.

---

### 🌸 4. LĨNH NAM & THE TRƯNG SISTERS (40 – 43 AD)
* **Geographical Span:** Mê Linh, Hát Môn, and the northern Lingnan region.
* **Capital / Strategic Bases:** **Mê Linh Capital**, **Hát Môn Headquarters**, **Cấm Khê Fortress**.
* **Architectural Characteristics:**
  * Earthen ramparts combined with spiny bamboo barricades against cavalry charges.
  * Open-air ceremonial altars on the banks of the Hát River for prayers before military campaigns.
* **Historical Figures:**
  * **Trưng Trắc & Trưng Nhị (Hai Bà Trưng):** Queen leaders who liberated 65 northern fortresses.
  * **72 Female Generals:** Thánh Thiên, Lê Chân, Bát Nàn, Phật Nguyệt.

---

### 🌿 5. VẠN XUÂN & DẠ TRẠCH VƯƠNG (6TH CENTURY)
* **Geographical Span:** Tô Lịch River (Hanoi) and Dạ Trạch Marshlands (Hưng Yên).
* **Capital / Strategic Bases:** **Long Biên Capital**, **Dạ Trạch Swamp Fortress**.
* **Tactics:** Concealing troops in dense reeds by day; launching amphibious night raids in dugout canoes.
* **Historical Figures:**
  * **Lý Nam Đế (Lý Bôn):** Founded the **Vạn Xuân Empire** (544 AD) and Khai Quốc Pagoda (Trấn Quốc Pagoda today).
  * **Triệu Việt Vương (Triệu Quang Phục):** Master of marshland guerrilla warfare.

---

### ⚔️ 6. EARLY AUTONOMY & BẠCH ĐẰNG (8TH – 10TH CENTURY)
* **Geographical Span:** Nghệ An, Sơn Tây, and the Bạch Đằng Estuary.
* **Capital / Strategic Bases:** **Vạn An Citadel**, **Đại La**, **Đường Lâm**, **Bạch Đằng Stake Fields**.
* **Key Innovation:**
  * **Submerged Iron-Tipped Timber Stakes:** Dense stakes driven at a 45-degree angle to impale enemy warships upon falling tide.
* **Historical Figures:**
  * **Ngô Quyền:** Decisive Bạch Đằng Victory in 938 AD, bringing over 1,000 years of northern domination to a permanent end.

---

### 🏰 7. ĐẠI CỒ VIỆT & HOA LƯ CAPITAL (10TH CENTURY)
* **Geographical Span:** Hoa Lư limestone valley and Tràng An (Ninh Bình).
* **Capital / Strategic Bases:** **Hoa Lư Stone Fortress**, **Am Tiên Cave**.
* **Architectural Characteristics:**
  * Capital utilizing vertical limestone cliffs as natural ramparts, connected by brick defensive walls.
  * Lotus-patterned glazed roof tiles and Sanskrit-inscribed stone Buddhist pillars (973 AD).
* **Historical Figures:** **Đinh Tiên Hoàng (Đinh Bộ Lĩnh)**, **Lê Đại Hành (Lê Hoàn)**, **Empress Dowager Dương Vân Nga**.

---

### 🐉 8. ĐẠI VIỆT LÝ DYNASTY (11TH – 12TH CENTURY)
* **Geographical Span:** Thăng Long (Hanoi) and Cầu River defensive line.
* **Capital / Strategic Bases:** **Thăng Long Imperial Citadel**, **Như Nguyệt River Line**.
* **Architectural Highlights:**
  * **One Pillar Pagoda (Diên Hựu):** Elegant wooden lotus structure rising from a single stone pillar.
  * **Báo Thiên Pagoda:** 12-storey tower reaching into the clouds; Temple of Literature (Văn Miếu, 1070).
* **Historical Figures:** **Lý Thái Tổ (Lý Công Uẩn)** (*Edict on Transferring the Capital* 1010), **Lý Thường Kiệt** (*Nam Quốc Sơn Hà*).

---

### ⚔️ 9. ĐẠI VIỆT ĐÔNG A & YÊN TỬ (13TH – 14TH CENTURY)
* **Geographical Span:** Thăng Long, Thiên Trường (Nam Định), Lục Đầu River (Vạn Kiếp), Mount Yên Tử, Chu Đậu Ceramic Village.
* **Architectural & Craft Zenith:**
  * **Phổ Minh Pagoda & Bình Sơn Tower:** Unadorned native timber and fired brick.
  * **Chu Đậu Brown-Pattern & Cobalt Ceramics:** World-renowned underglaze lotus and crane motifs.
  * **Mountaintop Altar to Heaven:** Open stone platform where An Thái and Mộc Tĩnh Lan offer spring water and golden grain.
* **Historical & Canon Figures:**
  * **Trần Hưng Đạo (Trần Quốc Tuấn):** Supreme commander across 3 victories over Mongol-Yuan forces.
  * **Emperor-Monk Trần Nhân Tông:** Founder of Trúc Lâm Yên Tử Zen Buddhism and promoter of natural medicine.
  * **AN THÁI:** Dong A Swordsman and naval commander bearing the *Sát Thát* pledge.
  * **MỘC TĨNH LAN:** Master Chu Đậu ceramic artist and Trúc Lâm herbalist.
