## 🏛️ DETAILS OF RECOMMENDATION AND ACTUAL ARCHAEOLOGICAL EVIDENCE:

### 1. Thang Long Imperial Citadel Relics (18 Hoang Dieu & Kinh Thien Palace - Hanoi):
- 🍲 Royal Dishes & Cuisine: Unearthed tens of thousands of pieces of Celadon celadon bowls, clear white enamel of the Ly Dynasty with embossed images of a coiled dragon and a pair of sparrows in a lotus heart. Proof that aristocrats used thin, light porcelain with glossy jade-like glaze.
- 🏠 Royal Tiles & Tiles: Unearthed roof tiles with statues of dragon heads (Si Vi), terracotta phoenix heads, leaves carved with dragons of the Ly Dynasty nearly 1 meter long; Square floor tiles embossed with 8-petal lotus flowers and blooming chrysanthemums.
- 🛏️ Resting Props: Blue enamel porcelain pillow statue shaped like a boy holding a lotus flower (Tran Dynasty) and supporting lotus-shaped celadon incense bowl.

### 2. Chu Dau & Van Ninh Ancient Pottery Kiln Relics (Nam Sach - Hai Duong):
- 🏺 Brown Flower & Ash Glaze Ceramics: Excavation of hundreds of shuttle kilns, layer upon layer of kilns made of fired clay covered with rice husk ash.
- 🐟 Folk Ordinary Life Patterns: Brown iron oxide painted dishes with images of swimming gobies, lotus buds, feeding sparrows, reed grass branches waving in the water. Proof that ordinary people eat rice in brown enamel bowls and eel-skin ceramic bowls.

### 3. Bach Dang Stake Site (Yen Giang, Ma Ngua Dong, Van Muoi Dong - Quang Ninh):
- 🌲 Sa Truong Wood Survey: Discovered hundreds of ironwood, sapwood, and tau wood poles with diameters of 20-35cm, length of 2.5m - 3m, tilted at 45 degrees in the direction of receding water. The tips of the piles are sharpened and many still have traces of heated iron tips.
- ⚔️ Water War Tactics: Evidence of the tactic of taking advantage of the tides and river mud to defeat the Mongol navy in 1288.

### 4. Viet Khe Boat Tomb Site (Thuy Nguyen - Hai Phong - Dong Son Culture):
- 🛶 River Vehicle: The coffin is made of a hollowed out wooden tree trunk, 4.76m long, 0.77m wide, containing 107 burial artifacts.
- 🗡️ Archaeological Weapons & Props: Unearthing monolithic bronze swords with statue hilts, comedy-heeled axes, T-shaped daggers, bronze bells, rafts and especially the oldest piece of silk hemp weaving in Vietnam.

### 5. Co Loa Relics & Cau Vuc Arrow Tunnel (Dong Anh - Hanoi):
- 🎯 Lien Chau Divine Crossbow & Dark Qi: Unearthed a warehouse containing tens of thousands of three-sided swallow-shaped bronze arrows (Cau Vuc arrows) cast in sharp monolithic bronze and 3-step mechanical bronze crossbow levers.

### 6. Van Kiep Underground Base Site & Kiep Bac Temple (Chi Linh - Hai Duong):
- ⚔️ Dong A Gas: Unearthed iron spear tips, spear hooks with shock hooks, horse-slaying daggers, fishing nets and field terracotta pots to cook ginger porridge to relieve cold for soldiers who like the word Sat That.

### 7. Compound Tombs of the Tran & Le Dynasties (Banana Garden Mummy, Bac Ninh, Hung Yen):
- 👘 Examination of Actual Ancient Costumes: The corpse was wrapped in dozens of layers of Giao Linh silk shirt, Tu Than shirt sewn with indigo-dyed, brown-dyed silk, the fabric fibers were still intact.
- 🌿 Herbal Embalming: The bottom of the bra has a thick layer of locust leaves, star anise, cinnamon and dried tea buds to keep the body clean and odorless.

### 8. Relics of Dau Pagoda, Phat Tich Pagoda & Binh Son Tower (Bac Ninh - Vinh Phuc):
- 🏛️ Dong A Buddhist Architecture: Ly Dynasty lotus stone pedestal holding Amitabha Buddha statue; The 15-storey Binh Son ceramic tower is made of unglazed terracotta, perfectly earthquake-resistant.

### 9. Van Don Ancient Port Relics & Cong Dong Island (Quang Ninh):
- 🚢 Ocean Trade Port: Unearthed tens of thousands of pieces of Dai Viet celadon pottery mixed with Song - Yuan ceramics, Canh Hung bronze coins, tuna bones, lime vase fragments and Chu Dau ceramic teapots serving international merchant ships.

### 10. Ancient Shipwreck Ruins of Cu Lao Cham (Quang Nam):
- 📦 Silk Road & River and Sea Ceramics: Excavation of more than 240,000 Chu Dau ceramic artifacts with blue flower glaze and Tam Thai glaze depicting lotus flowers, chrysanthemums, and phoenix birds stretching their wings, proving Dai Viet's top level of handicrafts reaching the world level.

## APPENDIX (PART 2) — READING THE SITES LIKE READING A BOOK

### 1. Le Van's method of reading relics (5 steps)

1. **Look at the soil layer:** Black soil is the kitchen, red soil is the oven, gray soil is the grave - the color of the soil tells the profession before seeing the artifacts.
2. **Smell:** Fishy grave soil, smoky kitchen soil, strong furnace soil - the nose precedes the eyes.
3. **Touch:** Smooth ceramics are officials, rough ceramics are people; Heavy bronze is a ceremonial weapon, light bronze is a weapon.
4. **Listen:** When you knock on the ceramic, you hear "still" means it's healthy, "thump" means it's internally cracked; If you knock on the copper, it sounds hollow, if it sounds solid, it means there's soil inside.
5. **Record:** sketch 3 sides, measure 3 dimensions, record coordinates — do not pick up artifacts from the hole until recording is complete.

### 2. Diary of 1 day of excavation (to write scenes of Le Van working)

Morning: scan the pit, classify pieces (ceramic — bronze — stone — bone). Noon: eat rice balls under the shack, take notes. Afternoon: wash the pieces, test them, take photos and draw. Evening: compare bibliographies, mark "match - not match - contradiction". Night: listening to the rain falling on the canvas tent, thinking about the ancients who molded this piece of pottery - their hands were as muddy as mine.

## FIVE SENSES OBJECTS (PART 2 — excavation tools)

1. **Archaeological mason's trowel** — thin blade, wooden handle; Remove soil in 2cm layers. Le Van's fly is worn out in one corner - it hasn't been replaced in 10 years.
2. **Bamboo bristle brush** — soft, dusts artifacts; hear rustling. Brushing Dong Son drums is like combing an old person's hair - gently.
3. **Numbered canvas bags** — each bag has 1 hole number, 1 day; Nom letters + numbers. If the bag is torn, it must be sewn — losing the number means losing the original object.
4. **Waterproof notebook** — sketching, taking notes; Wet rain doesn't blur. The notebook is 500 pages thick — 3 books for 10 years.
5. **Flashlight covered in red cloth** — illuminates holes at night without glare; warm red light. Looking at a piece of pottery at night - ivory enamel has the most beautiful color at this time.
6. **Excavated salt and sesame rice balls** — eaten quickly, with bare hands; The rice has a delicious earthy smell. Fat U brought rice to the hole - the whole team ate from the same clay tray.
