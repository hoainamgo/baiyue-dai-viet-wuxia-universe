## 🌲 DIVISION I: NORTHEAST REGION & QUANG YEN — YEN TU SACRED MOON & GIANG DAU WARFARE

### 01. Ngoa Van Peak & Yen Tu Sacred Bamboo Forest
* **Coordinates & Terrain:** The mountain peak is over 1,000m high, covered with clouds all year round, steep cliffs, and vast bamboo forests.
* **Details that can be touched and grasped by the senses:**
  * *Touch:* The mossy stone is cold to the touch; Each 9-segmented bamboo segment is supple and smooth with traces of time.
  * *Sound:* The sound of the howling wind passing through the bamboo crevices in harmony with the deep ringing of the bronze bell at Dong Pagoda, Co Nhac.
  * *Scent:* The faint scent of Truc Lam agarwood mixed with the smell of pine resin and damp night dew.
* **Masterpieces & Legend:** The place where Buddha Emperor Tran Nhan Tong renounced the throne to found the Truc Lam sect; The hermitage place of Huyen Giac Chan Nhan practices bamboo swords without moves.

### 02. Ha Long Bay & Bai Tho Mountain Cliffs
* **Coordinates & Terrain:** The bay has thousands of limestone islands undulating amid the emerald green sea water.
* **Details that can be touched and grasped by the senses:**
  * *Tactile touch:* The rough limestone surface bears chisel marks in Nom poetry by Truong Han Sieu and King Le Thanh Tong; The salty sea breeze stings my lips.
  * *Color:* The ash-gray rock island reflects in the emerald green water; Dau Go cave contains iron-covered iron poles that have not rotted for thousands of years.
* **Characteristics & Legend:** The place where Mother Dragon brought down her cubs to spray pearls and pearls to prevent foreign invaders; where An Thai and Tinh Lan carved their vows on the cliff.

### 03. Luc Dau River Junction & Van Kiep Dai Headquarters
* **Coordinates & Terrain:** The confluence of six rivers (Cau, Thuong, Luc Nam, Duong, Kinh Thay, Thai Binh) creates a rolling tide.
* **Details that can be touched and grasped by the senses:**
  * *Color & Taste:* River water is red and brick-colored; smell the warm alluvial soil and the sweetness of young corn along the shore.
  * *Sound:* The sound of waves crashing against the sides of 500 Mong Dong warships with 100 rowers; The roar of the stakes echoed through the sky.
* **Notable People & Legend:** Headquarters of Hung Dao Vuong Tran Quoc Tuan; where the historic separation from Luc Dau wharf took place.

### 04. Upper Bach Dang River
* **Coordinates & Terrain:** The large seaport connects to the Gulf of Tonkin, the water rises and falls according to the fierce diurnal cycle of rising and falling tides.
* **Details that can be touched and grasped by the senses:**
  * *Tactile touch:* Touching the tip of a sharp, iron-covered ironwood stake buried deep in the mud, the stake's body is covered with sharp shells.
  * *Feeling:* The cold wind at dawn when the tide recedes, revealing a beach of sharp stakes like a forest of steel spikes.

### 05. Snowstorm Covered Mau Son Peak
* **Coordinates & Terrain:** Dangerous mountain range over 1,500m on the border of Lang country, in winter snow and ice turn the tree branches white.
* **Details that can be touched and grasped by the senses:**
  * *Feeling:* White snowflakes fall coldly on the shoulder of the indigo shirt; The warmth rising from the bowl of wine made from wild leaves warmed my chest.
  * *Flavor:* The strong and spicy taste of Mau Son wine is brewed from 30 herbal yeasts picked on high cliffs.

### 06. Van Don Commercial Port & Cat Ba Island

### 07. Tam Thanh Cave & Chi Lang Pass
