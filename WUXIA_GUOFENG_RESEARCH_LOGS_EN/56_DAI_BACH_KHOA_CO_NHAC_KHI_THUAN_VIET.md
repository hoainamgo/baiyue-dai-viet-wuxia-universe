## 🧭 I. OVERVIEW: RICE CIVILIZATION & BAMBOO MUSIC - BRONZE ware

The traditional music of the ancient Vietnamese people (Bach Viet and Dai Viet) did not originate from luxurious palaces, but was deeply rooted in **the natural ecological environment: tropical bamboo forests, alluvial rivers, coastal mudflats and brilliant bronze casting civilization**.

Unlike Western musical instruments that use a lot of industrial metal or steppe musical instruments that use rough animal skins, **Pure Vietnamese musical instruments have three unique characteristics**:
1. **Rustic natural materials:** Bamboo, jackfruit wood, dried gourd shell, lime-spun silk thread, volcanic stone and ancient brass.
2. **The art of pressing, stroking, and vibrating microtonals (Microtonal Nuance):** Without being confined to fixed keys, Vietnamese musical instruments have the ability to bend in soft pitches like human voices, sighs, and lullabies.
3. **Yin-yang harmony:** Harmonious combination of the deep and majestic sound of thunder (Dong Son Drum), the clear sound of the wind (Flute) and the deep whisper of the human heart (String instrument).

## 🏛️ II. DETAILS OF SPECIAL TRADITIONAL MUSIC GROUPS

---

### 1. 🥁 ANCIENT BRASS & ROCK MUSIC GROUP (THE THOUSAND-YEAR VOICE)

* **Dong Son Drum (Ngoc Lu, Song Da, Hoang Ha — 2,500 - 3,000 years):**
  * *Manufacturing features:* Cast in copper - tin - lead alloy with sophisticated two-half mold technique. The drum surface depicts the sun in the center, the Lac bird flying counterclockwise, the scene of rice pounding and dancers playing the flute.
  * *Tone & Emotion:* The drums have a rich internal sound, resonating with the ceremony yard, the sound of boats knocking and footsteps; creates a sense of majesty without attaching a specific frequency.
* **Ancient Stone Platform (Ndut Lieng Krak, Khanh Son, Binh Da — 3,000 - 4,000 years):**
  * *Manufacturing characteristics:* Consists of volcanic rock bars (*rhyolite, phthalite*) hand-cut in different thicknesses and lengths to create natural heights.
  * *Tone & Emotion:* The sound is elegant, crisp, resounding far away like the sound of water pouring through a marble cliff, carrying the wild, mysterious color of the great wilderness.
* **Bronze Bells & Gongs:**
  * Has a long, gentle ringing sound, often used in village festivals and ceremonies to thank heaven and earth, bringing peace and tranquility to the soul.

---

### 2. 🪕 GROUP OF PLUZING STRINGS & MYTHYEN CAM GROUP (VIETNAMESE EMOTIONAL SOULS)

* **String Instrument (Doc Huyen Cam — A Unique Musical Masterpiece):**
  * *Making features:* Unique structure with only **one string** hanging on a long wooden body, one end is attached to a dried gourd and the neck is made of flexible buffalo horn.
  * *Tone & Technique:* The craftsman plucks the nodes to create harmonics (*harmonics*), his left hand bends the neck to create hundreds of suspended pitches. The sound of string instruments is likened to **a sigh, a sob or a mother's sweet lullaby**, poignant and touching the listener's heart deeply.
* **String instrument (Thap Luc Huyen Cam — 16 Silk Strings Ly - Tran Dynasties):**
  * *Making features:* The body of the instrument is made of sycamore wood, bent into a spherical dome shape, and on the surface are 16 moving swallows. Traditional strings made from silk spun in lime water have a warm and woody tone.
  * *Tone & Technique:* The sound of the guitar is clear and sweet like rain falling on an eaves, gorgeous through the spread fingers (*glide fingers*), middle fingers and soft claw fingers.
* **Da Day Instrument (Rode Cam — The Instrument of Tra That & Ca Tru Thang Long):**
  * *Making features:* The guitar's body is rectangular but **the bottom is completely hollowed out**, and the neck is long and has high keys made of old bamboo.
  * *Tone & Emotion:* The sound of the guitar is dull, dry, and strangely deep. When strumming with the lower and middle fingers, the sound of the Day instrument is like the voice of a gentleman's self-talk, discussing the worldly affairs of a gentleman and a guest over a cup of tea.
* **Piba & Tinh (Then Tay - Nung):**
  * *The Pi Ba:* Has a pear shape, the sound is crisp and fast like the hooves of a horse galloping a long distance.
  * *Thanh Tinh:* The instrument's gourd is made of half a dried gourd, the neck is long, the instrument's soft sound is used in Then songs to pray for harvest, healing and community cohesion.

---

### 3. 🎻 ZITER GROUP (ETHNIC DRAG LAKE & NARRATIVE VOICE)

* **Erh zither (Stork zither / Ho zither):**
  * *Making features:* The organ is made of wood covered with python or lizard skin, two silk strings, the bow is inserted between the two strings and cannot be removed.
  * *Tone & Emotion:* The tone is sobbing and rattling like the sound of someone confiding. When performing the tunes Nam Ai, Nam Binh or Bolero, the long, humming sound evokes feelings of nostalgia, separation and attachment.
* **Dipper Instrument:**
  * The gourd is made of old coconut shells, the tone is warm, rustic, and rich in the flavor of the Southern rivers.

---

### 4. 🎋 BAMBOO STEAM GROUP (THE WIND OF THE VILLAGE & THE MOUNTAIN FOREST)

* **Northern Flute & Bamboo Flute:**
  * *Crafting features:* Carved from cork tubes or old male bamboo soaked in mud to prevent termites, oval-shaped blow holes create breath vibrations (*breathy sound*).
  * *Tone & Emotion:* The clear, melodious sound of the flute evokes images of children herding buffaloes flying kites along the dike, a windy river on a summer afternoon and golden rice fields in the distance.
* **Bamboo Musical Instrument (Vertical Blowing Bamboo):**
  * The bamboo trunk is thick, the tube is long, the sound is warm, quiet, and magical, evoking the feeling of seclusion in the foggy Yen Tu bamboo forest.
* **Khen Be & Ken Bau (Khen Dong Son / Khen H'Mong):**
  * Consisting of many bamboo pipes joined in parallel into a wooden gourd, it can emit many notes in harmony at the same time (*polyphony*), the bustling sound of the flute, resounding through the mountains and forests during seasonal festivals.

---

### 5. 🥢 Rustic Wood & Bamboo Percussion Group

* **Phat Tre Ca Tru:**
  * Includes a wooden amber table and two old male bamboo amber leaves. The sound of tapping *clack - clack* is sharp and decisive, keeping the delicate rhythm of time in the auditorium space.
* **Comm Drum & Adoration Drum:**
  * The drum's surface is covered with buffalo skin, the jackfruit wood is hollowed out, and sticky rice is attached to the center of the drum's surface to soften the sound, creating the bustling, warm rhythm of the traditional village festival.

## 🌾 III. RICHNESS & ETERNAL CULTURAL VALUE

The pure Vietnamese musical instrument system is **a mirror that completely reflects the spiritual life and history of the nation**:
* **Wartime:** Dong Son drums, bamboo beats, and trumpets rang out the country's spirit, uniting the entire people to defend the land.
* **Peaceful time:** The sound of kite flute, string instrument, and bottom instrument mixed with a cup of morning tea, lullabies for children in the hammock at summer noon.
* **Wild place:** The sound of stone and lute soothes hardships, heals the mind and connects people with nature, Mother Earth.
