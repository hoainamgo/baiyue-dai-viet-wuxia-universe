## ☀️ I. BACDHAO ORIGINAL sect (12 major sects of the main sect):

### 1. 🌊 LAC LONG THUY PHAI (Luc Dau Giang - Van Kiep):
* **Origin:** Ancestor Lac Long Quan $\rightarrow$ Water God General Yet Kieu $\rightarrow$ **An Thai Knight** (`An_Thai.png`).
* **Martial Arts & Weapons:** *Short Knife with Rice Leaves (18 forms)*, *21 Foot Strikes to Clamp the Neck and Triet Ha (Vovinam)*, *Cuu Long Chan Hai Quyet*. Bronze short swords with stingray handles, iron drills to carve boats.
* **Clothing:** Indigo shirt with slit on the chest, Giao Long tattoo, hands with the word *Sat That*.

### 2. 🌸 TRUC LAM MEDITATION & KIM NGO AN VE (Non Thieng Yen Tu - Ngoa Van Am):
* **Nature of power:** Community of potters, weavers, physicians, breeders and Zen masters around Yen Tu area; The defense, medicine warehouse or “fictitious capital” must be labeled when telling the story.
* **Origin & Character:** Tran Dynasty's Zen Truc Lam heritage class and folk herbs; Moc Tinh Lan is a fictional character, not a historical figure.
* **Martial Arts & Weapons:**
  - *Bamboo Sword Thien Tam (Lao Mai Quyen - 108 acupuncture points do not kill)*.
  - *Kim Ngo Pha Van Kiem Phap (Forbidden Martial Arts to Protect the Emperor)*.
  * *Folk medicine – herbs, care and rest; does not claim musical healing.*
* **Clothes & Weapons:** Rustic monk's brown robe, Chu Dau raw silk body; The inside is lined with soft heart-protective armor made of gold-plated bronze; Yen Tu bamboo sword, mulberry wood stringed instrument, bamboo medicine basket on the back with a faint scent of lotus and wormwood.

### 3. 🏔️ SON MON PLAN (Non Thieng Ba Vi):
* **Origin:** First Blessed God Tan Vien Son Thanh (Son Tinh) & Brave Hero Thach Sanh.
* **Martial Arts & Weapons:** *Tan Vien Chan Son Kinh (I have iron skin)*, *Khai Son Phu Phap*, *Hung Ke Quyen*. Dai Khai Son Phu (50kg iron ax), mulberry wood stringed instrument; The sound is for symbolic purposes only and does not treat armor or swords.
* **Clothing:** Indigo robe lined with old wild animal skin, barefoot, wrapped in red scarf.

### 4. ⚡ PHU DONG THIET GIA (Soc Son & Co Loa Mountains):
* **Origin:** Phu Dong Thien Vuong (Thanh Giong) & General Cao Lo.
* **Martial Arts & Weapons:** *Phu Dong Kim Than Quyet*, *Thanh Truc Truong Phap (Bagua Kun)*, *Thanh No Van Tien Battle*. Copper-covered ivory bamboo cone, Linh Quang Than No (crossbow crossbow that shoots thousands of arrows with 3 sides).
* **Clothing:** Leather armor studded with copper rivets, wrapped in a scarf with the word Dinh.

### 5. ⚓ HAI LONG MAI GIA (Ha Long Bay & East Sea):
* **Origin:** Mai An Tiem's ​​mettle, Ba Trieu slashes the orca $\rightarrow$ **Sea King Tu Hai** & **Vuong Thuy Kieu**.
* **Martial Arts & Weapons:** *The Overlord Slaying the Sea Knife Dharma*, *The Indestructible Diamond Body*. 80-pound Sea Dragon Slayer Great Sword, Rocket Bow & Arquebus.
* **Clothes:** Great sea king's brocade robe trimmed with fur, Hy's red phoenix robe.

### 6. 👑 KIM NGO THAN VE (Forbidden Guard of Thang Long Imperial Palace):
* **Origin:** The secret guard force of the Luong Dau court during the Tran dynasty.
* **Martial Arts & Weapons:** *Kim Ngo Pha Van Kiem*, bronze apricot blossom dart.
* **Clothes:** Tran Dynasty dragon embroidered brocade shirt with gold thread trim, jade belt, bronze tokens.

### 7. 🎋 DA TRACH TIEN DAO (Da Trach Lagoon - Hung Yen):
* **Origin:** Chu Dong Tu & Princess Tien Dung.
* **Martial Arts & Weapons:** *Void Body Technique (surfing on swamp water)*, *Immortal Staff Pointing*. Bamboo sticks & conical hats protect from wind and frost.
* **Clothes:** Coarse ivory white silk shirt, elegant like a fairyland.

### 8. 🐘 ME LINH TUONG PHAI (Me Linh Ancestral Land):
* **Origin:** Two Trung Sisters (Trung Trac - Trung Nhi) & Female General Bat Nan.
* **Martial Arts & Weapons:** *Me Linh Battle Statue Statue*, *Dual Sword Me Linh*. The Dong Son drum sounds the command, The two bronze swords have phoenix hilts.
* **Clothing:** Brass breastplate carved with Lac birds, crimson silk shirt.

### 9. 🥋 MOST SCREAMING MEN (Thanh - Nghe Tinh):
* **Origin:** The oldest sect in the Lam & Ma river basins.
* **Martial Arts & Weapons:** *Shout Helps Weapons to Destroy Enemies*, *Tie Chi Penetrates Bones (armour-piercing acupoints)*. Ironwood sticks.
* **Clothing:** Coarse indigo linen clothes, head covered with a tight black scarf.

### 10. 🦅 Bach Hac To Dan (Hac Tri River Junction - Phu Tho):
* **Origin:** Phong Chau capital during the reign of King Hung, mascot Bach Hac, the divine bird.
* **Martial Arts & Weapons:** *White Crane Traveling Sword (sword stroke as elegant as a dancing crane)*, *Divine Flying Strike*. Long sword of wind steel, Bow and arrow of crane feathers.
* **Clothes:** White silk ao dai with crane feather trim, elegant and worldly style.

### 11. 🏹 Cao Than No Mon (Co Loa Citadel - Dong Anh):
* **Origin:** The esoteric disciples inherited Cao Lu's magic crossbow manufacturing diagram.
* **Martial Arts & Weapons:** *Mind Eye Shooting Technique (blindfolded shooting at fallen leaves)*. Mulberry wood short bow, Hand crossbow with 5 shots.
* **Clothing:** Neat, tight deerskin armor camouflaged in the jungle.

### 12. 🐴 AN DUONG THIET KY (Luy Thanh Co Loa):

* **Martial Arts & Weapons:** *Iron Cavalry Spear Battle Technique*. The bronze spear has a flame-shaped spear tip.

## 🏺 II. NEUTRAL BLOCK, MERCHANT CLASS & HERMITS (8 GREAT sects):

### 13. 🏺 CHU DAU CERAMICS TRADE ASSOCIATION (Thai Binh River - Hai Duong):
* **Origin:** Hoa Nau & Lam Lam potters' ward of the Tran dynasty.
* **Features:** Holding underground economic lifeblood, casting hidden weapons in ceramic kilns and transporting by river.
* **Password:** Seal rice leaves and Lac birds on the bottom of the ceramic vase.

### 14. 🔨 BONG XAM BACH KIM GIA (Dong Xam Silver Craft Village - Thai Binh):
* **Origin:** Thousand-year-old silver carving village.
* **Martial Arts & Weapons:** *Bach Kim Toai Silver Acupuncture (non-metal acupuncture & hidden silver weapon)*. Super sharp silver carved knife.
* **Clothes:** Silver-gray silk shirt embroidered with water wave patterns.

### 15. 🍵 TAY HO LIEN HOA PHI (Thang Long Lotus Pond):
* **Origin:** Tea ceremony hermits and poets of Thang Long capital.
* **Martial Arts & Weapons:** *Lianhua Tea Ceremony of the Mind Dharma (using warm tea water to clear the meridians)*. Jade cups and terracotta teapots were thrown as hidden weapons.
* **Clothes:** Tu Tu shirt is the color of dew-covered lotus petals, exuding the pure scent of tea.

### 16. 🗿 DONG SON DONG MON (Bronze Casting Village Thieu Hoa - Thanh Hoa):
* **Origin:** Descendants of bronze-casting artisans of Dong Son Drum.
* **Martial Arts & Weapons:** *Prodigy of Body Protection*. Sledge hammer cast bronze, Dong Son drum protects the mind.
* **Clothes:** Fireproof buffalo leather apron, muscular arms.

### 17. 🌿 HUONG SON DUC TOC (Huong Son Mountain Range - Ha Tinh):
* **Origin:** The cult of picking Agarwood & Deer Antler medicine in the deep forest.
* **Martial Arts & Weaponry:** *Lac Diep Thao Than Phap (jumping off cliffs to pick medicine)*. Long-handled herbal harvesting sickle, bamboo basket with heat-retaining compartment.
* **Clothes:** Short-sleeved indigo shirt, belt braided with wormwood root to eliminate miasma.

### 18. 🎶 PHAI PHAI THANH THAN (Central Highlands - South Central):
* **Origin:** Lithophone belief 3,000 years ago.
* **Martial Arts & Weapons:** *Sound Ba Doat Phap Sound Wave (using the sound of tapping a stone to shake the enemy's internal force)*. Set of volcanic rock percussion bars.
* **Clothing:** Brocade outfit with brilliant sun patterns.

### 19. 🛶 TEN THUY CAC BRONZE (Southern region - Ancient Melaleuca Forest):
* **Origin:** Southern Delta river people.
* **Martial Arts & Weapons:** *Randomly Surfing Shells*, *Forks to Catch Water Monsters*. Bamboo-handled three-pronged spear soaked in mud.
* **Clothing:** Black shirt, bandana around the neck.

### 20. 📜 THANG LONG LIBRARY (Thang Long Temple of Literature):
* **Origin:** Famous scholars and calligraphers of Dai Viet.
* **Martial Arts & Weapons:** *Phan Quan Bu Phap (using a brush to dip ink to point at death points)*. Brush pen with soft steel tip, gemstone inkstone.
* **Attire:** Black shirt, elegant and scholarly turban.
