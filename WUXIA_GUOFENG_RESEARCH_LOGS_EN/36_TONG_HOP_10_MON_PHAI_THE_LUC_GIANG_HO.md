## 🥋 DETAILS OF 10 GREAT sects & forces from the beginning until now:

---

### 1. 🌊 LAC LONG THUY PHAI (DONG DAO & WATERFIGHT A)
* **Appears from:** Luc Dau Giang martial arts research session & character creation of An Thai.
* **Typical characters:** **An Thai Knight** (`An_Thai.png`), Water God General Yet Kieu, Da Tuong.
* **General group:** Van Kiep lagoon (Hai Duong) & Chu Dau river wharf.
* **Excellent techniques:** *Short Knife with Rice Leaves (18 techniques to control the long field)*, *Treat Ha's Neck-Clamping Leg Strike (predecessor of 21 Vovinam attacks flying 2m high)*, *Cuu Long Chan Hai Quyet (diving deep into the boat)*.
* **Weapons & Clothing:** Bronze short sword with stingray handle, iron drill bit. Indigo shirt with slit on the chest, Giao Long tattoo, hand with the word *Sat That*.

---

### 2. 🌸 TRUC LIN MEDITATION (THONONG MEDICINE & Ancient Music ARCHITECTURE)
* **Appears from:** Research session on Y Dao Tue Tinh, Chu Dong Tu Da Trach and Moc Tinh Lan.
* **Representative characters:** **Miss Moc Tinh Lan** (`Moc_Tinh_Lan_square.png`), Saint of Southern Medicine Tue Tinh.
* **General altar:** Ngoa Van Pagoda (Yen Tu) & Da Trach Lagoon.
* **Excellent techniques:** *Bamboo Sword Zen Heart (Old Mai Quyen - point 108 acupuncture points not to kill)*, *Da Trach Quy Tuc Cong*, *Shen Nong Duoc Thuat (580 herbal medicines)*.
* **Arms & Clothing:** Yen Tu bamboo sword, bamboo stick, magic leaf hat, 16-string silk instrument. Bright brown shirt, four layers of raw silk, peach wood brooch, and bamboo medicine basket on the back.

---

### 3. 🏔️ SON MON PILLS (IRON HAMMER AND SACRED PANTHER)
* **Appears from:** Research session on the Myth of Hung Vuong, Son Tinh Thuy Tinh & Thach Sanh.
* **Typical characters:** **Valiant Thach Sanh**, Tan Vien Son Thanh (Son Tinh).
* **Total group:** Ba Vi King Peak & Northwest Great Thousand.
* **Excellent skills:** *Tan Vien Chan Son Kinh (I have iron skin)*, *Khai Son Phu Phap (iron hammer to behead Chan Tinh)*, *Hung Ke Quyen (cockfighting fist)*.
* **Weapons & Clothing:** Dai Khai Son Phu (50-pound iron ax), Ancient stringed instrument, Than Than Co Nhac (disarming 18 vassal countries). Indigo robe lined with old wild animal skin, barefoot.

---

### 4. ⚡ PHU DONG THIET GIA (MASTER OF METALLURGY & GOD OF FIRE LIEN CHAU)
* **Appeared from:** Research session on Thanh Giong vs Giac An & Co Loa Magic Crossbow Archeology.

* **General:** Soc Son Mountain & Co Loa Military Arsenal.
* **Excellent skills:** *Phu Dong Kim Than Quyet*, *Thanh Truc Truong Phap (Bagua Kun Bamboo Ivory)*, *Thhan Nu Van Tien Formation*.
* **Arms & Clothing:** Iron-clad bamboo and ivory sticks, Linh Quang Than No (shooting thousands of bronze arrows on 3 sides of Cau Vuc). Armor with copper rivets, wrapped in a scarf with the word Dinh.

### 5. ⚓ HAI LONG MAI GIA (MASTER OF THE EASTERN SEA NAVIGATION)
* **Appears from:** Research session of Hai Vuong Tu Hai, Thuy Kieu, Mai An Tiem & Ba Trieu slashing orcas.
