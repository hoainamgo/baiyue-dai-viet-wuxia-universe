### ☀️ I. THIEN TANG (GENESIS, THE GREAT FLOOD & SACRIFICE TO THE LO THIEN HEAVEN)

#### 1. 《Lu Hong Origin Sutra》 (Epic of Creation & Rescue Gourd)
* **Archaeological date:** 5,000 years ago (Equivalent to the early Luong Chu - Phung Nguyen period).
* **Content:** Tells about the wild time, the Creator sent the Great Flood to destroy the evil world. The good man and woman hid in the sacred Gourd with the seeds. When the water receded on the top of the high mountain, they set up an open-air stone altar to thank God, sowing seeds to give birth to hundreds of Bach Viet brother tribes.
* **Excerpt from Hoan Nguyen:**
> *“The sky made the wind and rain pour down, high waves flooded the peaks of thousands of drifting clouds.*
> *The Gourd leans on the ocean waves, carrying the seeds of life through life's storms.*
> *The water recedes... the sun shines in the winter sky, the stone altar is built in the middle of the field to give thanks:*
> *Oh God, the source of nourishment, hundreds of children covering the mountains and rivers!”*

#### 2. 《Thien Lo Dien Altar》 (Original Heaven Sacrifice Ritual)
* **Content:** Absolute standards for setting up an Altar of Heaven made of open-air stone outdoors (no roof, no statue). The offering only uses **1 Bowl of Clear Spring Water** (purity) and **1 Bowl of Golden Grains** (life). Absolutely prohibit artificial incense smoke and superstitious black magic.
* **Excerpt from Hoan Nguyen:**
> *“Natural stone is placed in front of the yard, windy on all four sides to welcome the sunlight.*
> *Clear spring water washes away the dust of the world, golden grains are offered in devotion.*
> *Looking up at the blue sky, my heart is as clear as jade, without any evil thoughts.*
> *No smoke or incense, only gratitude to the Creator of all things.”*

#### 3. 《Seven-Colored Rainbow Covenant》 (Memories of Peace & Rebirth)
* **Content:** Philosophy of the 7-Color Rainbow that appeared after the great flood - symbol of the eternal peace covenant between Heaven and Earth. The origin of Ancient Music's sound law is natural harmony.
* **Excerpt from Hoan Nguyen:**
> *“The storm dissipated on the high mountain top, a rainbow of seven colors spread into the blue clouds.*
> *The eternal covenant of peace, storms and floods will not perish after thousands of years.*
> *The sound of the guitar vibrates in the air, as soft as a cool, sweet stream of rebirth.”*

### 🕊️ II. HIEU TANG (FAMILY TEACHINGS, CHILDREN'S TEACHINGS & FAMILY CEMETERY)

#### 4. 《Hundreds of Vietnamese Teachings》 (Eternal Teachings on Filial piety)
* **Date:** Engraved on bamboo cards and transmitted orally through folk songs.
* **Content:** Immortal declaration about parents' gratitude to heaven and sea, considering Filial piety as the root of all human morality and national prosperity.
* **Excerpt from Hoan Nguyen:**
> *“Father's work stands tall like a high mountain, protecting him from rain and sun even through hardships.*
> *Mother's meaning is like sweet water, flowing from a spring well into the night.*
> *As a child, keep the first sentence: Honor your father and love your mother, even though your hair is gray, it will not fade."*

#### 5. 《Rituals for Taking Care of Parents in Old Age》 (Rite of Taking Care of Parents in Old Age)
* **Content:** Ritual to take care of parents from food, sip of water, morning greetings to taking care of medicine when sick.
* **Excerpt from Hoan Nguyen:**
> *“In the morning, offer a cup of green tea, in the afternoon, give your parents a peaceful sleep.*
> *In the winter, warm clothes are given away, in the summer, the porch fan is cooled by the wind.*
> *Gratitude is as deep and heavy as time immemorial, a filial heart full of morning and noon.”*

#### 6. 《Husband and Wife Together Heart Letter》 (Strong Love between Husband and Wife)
* **Content:** Ancient Vietnamese philosophy of monogamous love. The image of a broken ceramic bowl divided in two - half carried into battle, half kept in the rear.
* **Excerpt from Hoan Nguyen:**
> *“A ceramic bowl divided into two, he sat down with a concubine waiting for news.*
> *A hundred years of keeping one word, the blue enamel will not fade, the love will not fade.*
> *Even though the road is long and willow, the earth is baked through fire waiting for the day to reunite."*

### 🚣 III. JIANG TANG (EPIC OF THE YANGTUS RIVER & RIVER CIVILIZATION)

#### 7. 《Yang Zi Gu Feng Luc》 (Canoeing Song South of Yangtze River)
* **Content:** Preserving the song *Viet Nhan Ca* and the epic songs of rowing over rapids on the Truong Giang river of the Man Viet, Dong Viet, Lac Viet tribes.
* **Excerpt from Hoan Nguyen:**
> *“The river is long and the waves are immense, bamboo boats swish in the middle of the Truong Giang river.*
> *The moon shines on the deserted shore, the Vietnamese chant echoes on both shores.*
> *Life is like a dream, the heart is still on the riverbank."*

#### 8. 《Cuu Long Ha Do Poetry Tap》 (Southern River Delta Poetry)
* **Content:** Describes the picture of the generous and generous nature of the delta, the repartee singing, the Ba Ba shirt and the coconut-shaded boat.
* **Excerpt from Hoan Nguyen:**
> *“Low water then high water, the boat takes passengers through Ham Luong region.*
> *Ba Ba's shirt is drenched in sunlight, and the bandana is lightly draped with the song.*
> *Ben Tre is shaded with green coconut trees, forever full of love for the river and good land.”*

#### 9. 《Dong Dinh Ho Co Ky》 (Legend of 100 Tribes at Dong Dinh Lake)
* **Content:** Records of the ancient gathering space of Bach Viet block around Dong Dinh lake, where Lac Long Quan met Au Co to establish the roots of Hong Bang.
* **Excerpt from Hoan Nguyen:**
> *“Dong Dinh Lake is filled with mist and smoke, dragons fly and phoenixes dance and thousands of flowers bloom.*
> *Fifty go down to the sea, fifty go back to the mountains.*
> *Expand the realm everywhere, forever remember the Dragon and Fairy race."*

### 🌿 IV. ORGAN PHARMACEUTICAL & AGGREGATE PHARMACY (THONONG MEDICINE & DONG SON WATER RICE)

#### 10. 《Shen Nong Botanical Medicine Taoist Sutra》 (580 Pure Herbs)
* **Content:** The first encyclopedia of the South records the medicinal properties of 580 species of tropical plants and roots according to the principle of yin and yang and the five elements harmonizing heaven and earth.
* **Excerpt from Hoan Nguyen:**
> *“Southern medicine cures men's diseases, mother earth's plants nourish the body and health.*
> *Dandelion quickly detoxifies, ginseng roots nourish life's vitality.*
> *According to God's rules, your mind will be pure and your body will be eternally peaceful."*

#### 11. 《Tue Tinh Truc Lam Duoc Pho》 (Yen Tu Zen Sect Medicine)
* **Content:** The combination of ethnic herbs and the spirit of Truc Lam Zen during the Tran Dynasty. The method of soaking in herbal medicine by female physician Moc Tinh Lan.
* **Excerpt from Hoan Nguyen:**
> *“Tobacco leaves are picked in the morning dew. On the fire, long nights of fragrance fly.*
> *Saving people who are suffering day after day, compassion spreads throughout Truc Lam's days.”*

#### 12. 《Agricultural Calendar Giao Chi Astronomy Dien》 (Dong Son Drum & Crop Calendar)
* **Content:** Decoding the 24 solar terms, water cycle and wet rice astronomy carved on the Dong Son Ngoc Lu / Song Da drum.
* **Excerpt from Hoan Nguyen:**
> *“The sun shines in the heart, the Lac birds flap their wings thousands of miles away.*
> *Sowing seeds in spring, flowering in summer, harvest of ripe rice, warm and full house."*

#### 13. 《Lac Long Water Battle Secrets》 (Martial Arts Sword Technique & Underground Pile Battle)
* **Content:** The famous naval warfare of the Lac Viet people, the art of using short swords and rice leaves, diving to destroy enemy boats and the Bach Dang underground stake battle.
* **Excerpt from Hoan Nguyen:**

> *The iron pile blockaded the river mouth, sinking the enemy ship amid the rising tide."*

#### 14. 《Dong A Hao Khi Hich Thu》 (Vow to Slay That Forever)
* **Content:** The immortal heroic text of the Tran Dynasty, the tenacious spirit of Dai Viet's army and people in the face of the firestorm of foreign Mongol invaders.
* **Excerpt from Hoan Nguyen:**
> *“The red flag flutters in the East A wind, the two words SAT THATT are carved into the arm.*
> *Even though my body is exposed to flying sand, I am determined to keep this land and rivers peaceful!”*

#### 15. 《Me Linh Uprising Battle Map》 (Art of War 65 Queen Trung Tri Citadel)
* **Content:** Four verses of the Hat River and the military deployment map of Hai Ba Trung and 72 female generals of Me Linh to wipe out the Eastern Han invaders.
* **Excerpt from Hoan Nguyen:**
> *“First, please wash away the enemy country, secondly, please rebuild the Hung family's old karma.*
> *Elephants roar in the waves of Hat Giang, their golden armor shines and splendors in the Southern sky!”*

### 🎨 VI. TANG TANG ART & TEST TANG (ANTIQUE MUSIC, CHU DAU CERAMIC & FOLK SINGS)

#### 16. 《Dong Son Five-Sound Classic of Classical Music》 (The Law of the Five-Note Sound System of Classical Music)
* **Content:** Tonal system Five-note system (Ho, Xu, Xang, Xe, Cong corresponding to Cung, Thuong, Gioc, Chuy, Vu), Co Nhac frequency recreates the subtle harmony of heaven and earth.
* **Excerpt from Hoan Nguyen:**
> *“The sound of a stringed lute is ringing one string, the stringed lute is pouring down the stream, ringing endlessly.*
> *The tranquil atmosphere blends, four hundred and thirty-two hectares (Co Nhac) heals the mind.”*

#### 17. 《Chu Dau Glazed Lam Thi Luc》 (Ceramic Industry & Vase Bottom Poetry)
* **Content:** The art of polishing brown flower ceramics, elegant blue glaze and the custom of writing Nom poetry/ancient characters on the bottom of clay pots by Chu Dau potters.
* **Excerpt from Hoan Nguyen:**
> *“The turntable smooths the soft ground, the strokes of the lotus brush bloom softly and turn blue.*
> *Burn the red furnace all night long, the thousand-degree terracotta turns into tri-sound."*

#### 18. 《Hundreds of Vietnamese Folk Songs》 (Collection of Xoan, Quan Ho & Mai Nhi Singing)
* **Content:** Thousands of folk songs of love exchange, response, and rustic lyricism passed down through hundreds of generations by the banyan tree's waterfront.
* **Excerpt from Hoan Nguyen:**
> *“Dear people, please don't go home, betel and phoenix wings promise tomorrow.*
> *Even through thousands of seas of mulberry trees, the Quan Ho song bridges the gap between friends."*
