### 🏛️ I. THE CORE MARTIAL PHILOSOPHY OF THE CORE MARTIAL PHILOSOPHY:

Unlike the fierce Northern martial arts that require a large body, Co Phong Dai Viet martial arts is built on 4 vital principles:
1. **"Doi Doi Truong" (Using the short to overcome the long):** Use short swords, short swords, daggers, and close fists to destroy the long spears, large halberds, and scimitars of the Northern cavalry.
2. **"Di Nhu Thang Cương" (Softness Over Hardness):** Avoid wriggling like an orca crossing the waves, borrow force to attack force, never use force to block directly.
3. **"Water Warfare & Sat That Step Dharma":** The step method adapts to swampy, muddy terrain, slippery boat floors and diving deep into the river bottom.
4. **"One Strike All Sat":** Attacks directly on vital dangerous acupuncture points (lower body, throat, armpits, hamstrings) with fingertips tempered as hard as steel.

#### 1. 🦵 MARTIAL STATION 01: "DAO BINH PHAT TECHNIQUE" (Forerunner of Vovinam Leg Clip)
* **Origin:** Hai Ba Trung Dynasty (year 40) and Tran Dynasty (1258–1288).
* **Strategy characteristics:**
  * Dai Viet soldiers ran close to the Mongolian war horses, stepped on the horse's knees and launched themselves straight into the air (2 - 3 meters high).
  * The legs are stretched out and rotated 180 degrees into a pincer position, **clinging tightly around the enemy general's neck** sitting on the saddle, using the twisting force of the waist to pull the opponent to the ground, breaking his neck, or pulling out a short knife to slit his throat right in the air.
* **Visual application:** An Thai's posture flying in the air, kicking and defeating the Mongolian general in the middle of the Luc Dau Giang battlefield.

---

#### 2. 🐓 MARTIAL STATION 02: "HUNG KE FIGHTING Dharma" (Chicken Fist Fighting Weakness to Win Strong)
* **Origin:** The art of imitating animals during the Bach Viet period was later summarized into the National Martial Arts form by Tay Son Nguyen Lu.
* **Strategy characteristics:**
  * His movements undulate, suddenly disappearing and appearing, wriggling under the opponent's armpit.
  * **The two hands turn into beaks and spurs:** The hand closes the index finger and thumb to peck directly at the Yet Pharyn point, eyes and temples (*Gain Plucking Pose*).
  * **Fighting technique:** A heel hook kick from below (*Wind Tearing Chicken Spur*) breaks the jawbone or cuts the hamstring of a large opponent.
* **Visual Application:** The scene where An Thai fights hand-to-hand to defeat the Black Phong Bang robbers at the pottery kiln.

---

#### 3. 🌊 WU 03: "LAC LONG THUY CHIEN CHIEN" (Secret Technique Yet Kieu Deep Diving to Boring Boats)
* **Origin:** Diving technique of Bach Viet residents along the Gulf of Tonkin and Luc Dau River (Hung Dao Vuong period).
* **Strategy characteristics:**
  * **Reed Breathing Technique:** Hold on to a small piece of bamboo reed that lies in ambush at the muddy bottom of the river for many hours.
  * **The "Crossing the Bottom of the Dragon" position:** Diving close to the bottom of the enemy's wooden boat, using **Sharpened iron drill** and copper hammer to puncture the bottom of the boat in the quiet night.
  * **The "Water Demon Takes Life" position:** When the enemy soldier falls into the water, the brave warrior uses his foot to hold the enemy's leg and drag him to the bottom, using a rice leaf dagger to cut off the anchor rope of the warship.
* **Visual Application:** The Battle of Bach Dang in 1288 buried thousands of enemy ships.

---

#### 4. 🌸 WU 04: "LAO MAI QUAN & TIEN PHU TRUC KIEN" (Resilient Qi before Storms)
* **Origin:** Yen Tu Zen Martial Arts (Truc Lam) and Binh Dinh Traditional Martial Arts.
* **Strategy characteristics:**
  * Take the image of an old apricot tree brave with years of moonlight: The body stands as steady as a stone table in front of a strong wind, but the hands dance as softly as an apricot branch catching the wind.
  * **"Lao Mai Sinh Chi" Stance:** Avoid a lightning strike by leaning close to the ground in a U-shape, and at the same time, using the position to bounce back, use a bamboo stick to hit the enemy's Dan Dien acupuncture point.
* **Visual application:** Canh An Thai and Moc Tinh Lan practice swords in the misty bamboo forest of Yen Tu.

---

#### 5. 🏺 Martial Arts Stance 05: "JADE TRAN DOAN KIEM Dharma" (Jade Cup Falling on Silver Platform)
* **Origin:** Ly - Tran Royal Palace and Thang Long Imperial Palace.
* **Strategy characteristics:**
  * The sword stance is elegant, changing like the movement of dew drops falling on lotus petals.
  * The sword does not go in a straight line but in a spiral shape (eight trigrams circle), the tip of the sword vibrates, emitting a buzzing sound like a bumble bee. It specializes in using a silver-wrapped short sword to repel hidden weapons and stab the enemy's wrist holding the sword.
* **Visual application:** Moc Tinh Lan's short sword self-defense technique.

---

#### 6. 🖐️ THE VO 06: "Nhat Nam Thiet Chi Cong" (Dharma Pointing to Chau Hoan Point)
* **Origin:** Ancient land of Nghe An - Thanh Hoa (Chau Hoan, Chau Ai thousand years).
* **Strategy characteristics:**
  * I trained my five fingertips to be as hard as steel bars by sticking them in a bucket of hot sand and mulberry bark.
  * When performing the move, the finger points straight through the leather armor, destroying the enemy's air meridians in the blink of an eye without having to draw the sword.
* **Visual Application:** An Thai's esoteric close combat martial arts when his weapon was taken away.
