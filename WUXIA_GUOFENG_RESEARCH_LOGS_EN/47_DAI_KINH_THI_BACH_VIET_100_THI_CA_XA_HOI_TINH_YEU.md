## 🌸 BOOK I: PHONG — COUPLE LOVE & IRON LIPSTICK (LESSON 01 - 20)

### 01. Phoenix Wing Betel Soup
> Green betel leaves plucked from the areca tree,
> Smear red lime into each other's love.
> Phoenix wings spread out sweetly,
> Receive a piece of betel and promise to last a lifetime.

### 02. Waiting by the Well at the Head of the Village
> Carry a pair of wooden barrels to scoop out clear water,
> Tilt your hat to hide your rosy cheeks.
> He stood next to the banyan tree at the end of the alley,
> Sparkling smiles on the riverbank.

### 03. Peach Silk Camisole
> Lotus flowers bloom in early summer,
> Peach bib dyed in the sun behind the bamboo grove.
> The wind blows the scent of lotus onto my loose hair,
> The boy from the neighboring village stood engrossed.

### 04. The Bamboo Bridge Welcoming the Bride
> The bamboo bridge sways along the canal,
> White hat with a big smile.
> Mother-in-law gave a string of fresh pearls,
> Welcoming the bride to a warm place.

### 05. Bride and Wife Slapping Water on a Moonlit Night
> Oh, you slapping water,
> The golden moonlight fell into the clear ditch.
> The two of them groaned in unison,
> The water overflowed the banks of the rice fields in full bloom.

### 06. Hastily Stitched Indigo Shirt
> Each needle sews the indigo shirt,
> Strong thread sent to loved ones.
> Tomorrow he goes on a long journey to trade,
> Dress warmly to reduce snow and frost.

### 07. Make a Promise Under the Bamboo Grove
> The full moon night lights up the bamboo tops,
> Words of love and remembrance whispered in my ear.
> Waiting for the rice crop to ripen tomorrow,
> Carry her back to the alley to conclude the couple's song.

### 08. Pottery Piece Waiting For The Day To Return
> Break the blue flower plate in half,
> Half of you keep thousands of miles away.
> Half of me hides under the roof,
> We will meet each other perfectly the day we meet.

### 09. Rowing a boat to pick you up
> The oar gently waves the mist,
> The sound of shouting and answering across the ditches and fields.
> Your boat carries a smile,
> My boat carries all my heart and anticipation.

### 10. East Sea Bridegroom
> Conforming to the wife and agreeing to the husband drains the deep sea,
> Even though we are poor, we still have gray hair together.
> Cold winter, sharing a rustic blanket,
> Simple happiness brightens the head.

### 11. Red Buttoned Scarf
> Took a handkerchief and sent it to him,
> Red thread woven in the shape of a pair of mandarin ducks.
> Traveling thousands of miles, missing my homeland,
> Maintain integrity and integrity.

### 12. Meeting at Dinh Market
> The market is bustling with fun,
> Buy pink thread, buy fresh grapefruit.
> Accidentally touching the thin hem of the shirt,
> To love and remember for a lifetime.

### 13. My hair is long and fragrant with the scent of grapefruit flowers
> I wash my hair with fragrant grapefruit leaf juice,
> Her hair falls down her back as smooth as silk.
> The boy carrying firewood walked through the alley,
> Stop and ecstatically remember all year round.

### 14. Hand-Handed Bowl of Green Tea
> The hot summer sun burns my shoulders,
> I carried a bowl of fresh green tea.
> Take a cool sip,
> Repay your kindness with a smile!

### 15. The Sound of a Shuttle Remembers People Far Away
> The shuttle rattles all night long,
> Silk weaving sends fishing balls.
> Remember the people from far away carrying water,
> Pray to God to help ease your sadness.

### 16. The Afternoon Ferry Waiting For You
> The sunset reddens the old wharf,
> Waiting for the sailboat to overcome the wind and rain.
> People who have been gone for years and have not returned,
> The sun sets beside the floating river.

### 17. Giving Each Other Areca Flower Branches
> White areca flower branches fall in front of the yard,
> Pick up a good relationship and give it to your friend.
> Fragrant flowers are as pure as the heart,
> Rustic, lasting happiness and longevity.

### 18. Lullaby to Husband
> The northeast monsoon blows through the trees,
> Wife lights a coal stove for extra heat.
> Boiled spinach soup with sweet tomato sauce,
> Husband eats well and sleeps peacefully.

### 19. Dye Your Teeth Loyalty Black
> Teeth are jet black,
> A charming smile holds the heart.
> Thousands of years old custom of Bach Viet people,
> Love each other forever and never fade away.

### 20. Full Moon Reunion
> The full moon shines brightly on the pond's edge,
> The couple together is excited.
> Holding hands and promising to be faithful,
> Heaven and Earth witness how beautiful it is!

## 🌾 BOOK II: AGRICULTURE & TECHNOLOGY — SEASON & LABOR (LESSON 21 - 40)

### 21. Planting Rice at Moonlight Night
> Wading through the mud to plant seedlings under the clear moonlight,
> Each young rice branch is planted in a straight line.
> Dripping sweat nourishes life,
> Waiting for the day the golden rice seeds bloom.

### 22. Pounding Rice on Both Sides
> The mortar and pestle tilted and the sound of pounding echoed,
> The boy and girl danced rhythmically.
> The rice husks flutter with the fragrant scent of sticky rice,
> The rice grains are white and warm in the village.

### 23. Pottery Village Turntable
> The clay is sticky under the wheel,
> The hands are skillfully shaped.
> The vase and bowl gradually take shape,
> Blows the soul of the nation every month and every day.

### 24. The Sound of the Hammer and Anvil of the Forge
> The fire is red and embers are blowing,
> Hammer pounding cold-forged steel.
> Plowshares and hoe blades for the fields,
> Nurturing people throughout all four seasons.

### 25. Van Phuc Silk Loom
> The sound of the shuttle running horizontally and vertically,
> Weaving thin silk sheets with cicada wings.
> Dye the tubers to a shiny golden brown color,
> Throughout Linh Nam, it is famous for its praise.

### 26. Early Morning Casting
> The bamboo boat glides gently on the calm river surface,
> Throw the net to make the fish jump.
> Fresh shrimp and silver fish fill the bamboo cavity,
> A bright smile greets the sunrise.

### 27. Dong Son Bronze Casting
> Melting brass and pouring it into molds,
> Lac bird pattern with fluttering wings.
> Dong Son drums shape the souls of mountains and water,
> Warm, deep echoes in thousands of directions.

### 28. Using Bamboo and Ivory to Build a House
> Go to the forest to cut bamboo poles,
> Weaving baskets and rafters to build a roof.
> Thatched roofs protect from rain and sun and wind,
> Cozy reunion for many generations.

### 29. Making Coastal Salt
> Bringing winter sea water into the drying fields,
> The scorching sun burns the sweat.
> Crystallizes white, salty salt grains,
> For a rich, fresh meal.

### 30. Harvesting Golden Harvest Rice
> The rice is ripe and golden throughout the fields,
> The loud sound of sickles cutting grass.
> Carrying each sheaf of rice to the brick yard,
> Warm laughter satisfies my wishes.

### 31. Knitting Baskets and Knitting Baskets
> Split each bamboo slat and smooth it to make it round.
> Weaving baskets and knitting iron-toned cotton balls.
> Storing rice and corn during harvest season,
> A girl's hands are neat and square.

### 32. Flute-Playing Buffalo Herd
> Riding the back of a buffalo, dreaming of grazing on young grass,
> The flute's sound whistles across the top of the dunes.
> White clouds float across the blue sky,
> Peaceful childhood in the countryside.

### 33. Cooking Banh Tet Banh Chung
> Wash fragrant sticky rice with green beans,
> Wrap pork in green banana leaves.
> Watching the pot over a warm fire all night long,
> The fragrant scent welcomes spring.

### 34. Tea Picking on Upland Hills
> Morning sunlight picks young tea buds,
> Stars on the cast iron pan hold round smoke.
> The green water has an acrid and sweet taste,
> Rich in love for the warm land of thousands of children.

### 35. Building a Wooden Boat
> Chisel each grain of wood to build bamboo boats,
> Pairing surfboards with thousands of bottles.
> Boats take fishermen out to sea,
> Bring back wealth and warmth to the world.

### 36. Milling Rice at Moonlight Night
> The sound of the bamboo mill creaking,
> The rice husks fall and the rice is revealed.
> The old mother winnows each batch of bran,
> Raising strong and stable children.

### 37. Growing Mulberries and Raising Silkworms
> Red River mudflat with green mulberry leaves,
> Raising silkworms to eat delicious food.
> Sew golden silk to make clothes,
> Weaving the love of the countryside with its elegant features.

### 38. Catching Crabs in the Waterway
> Wading along the ditch and digging into deep caves,
> Catching a basket of crabs makes a lot of noise.
> Cook a bowl of sweet spinach and jute soup,
> Dispel the sweltering summer afternoon heat.

### 39. Bell Hat Splice
> Split young palm leaves and dry them white,
> Sew each stitch with only three tam hats.
> Covering the rain and sun from the village women,
> The tilted white hat hides his gentle features.

### 40. Warm Full Meal After Harvest
> Freshly cooked rice is fragrant and soft,
> Plate of pickled eggplant and soy sauce, plate of braised fish.
> The whole family gathered around wooden mats,
> Thank God and Earth for warmth and prosperity!

## 🤱 BOOK III: RELIGION & FAMILY — Lullabies & Motherly Love (LESSON 41 - 60)

### 41. Summer Noon Hammock Lullaby
> Oh my... Let me sleep soundly,
> The stork's wings fluttered and fluttered.
> When you grow up, remember your roots,
> The merit of father and mother is deeper than the ocean and sky.

### 42. Mother's Hair Turns Gray According to the Rice Season
> Each season the rice ripens, my mother gets older,
> A hunched back carrying the burden of work near and far.
> My hair is gray because of the wind and dew,
> I've loved my mother for so many years I don't care.

### 43. Bowl of Spinach and Tomato Soup with Soy Sauce
> Traveling thousands of miles away, missing home,
> Remember the bowl of spinach soup my mother made.
> Marinate crispy eggplant with sweet sticky rice sauce,
> Rich in love without fading.

### 44. Father's Hands
> Father's hands are rough and calloused,
> Holding the hoe and plowing all day long.
> Support your child's life step by step,
> God's gratitude is comparable to no one.

### 45. New Bowl of Rice Offered to Mother and Father
> Harvesting the first batch of rice and shoveling the bowl of rice,
> Respectfully offer our parents the fragrant smoke.
> Fold your hands to give thanks to your birth mother,
> Raise your children to grow up with filial piety.

### 46. ​​The Shirt Mother Patched in Winter Night
> The peanut oil lamp flickers,
> Mom sat and mended her shirt with a needle.
> Warm clothes I bring to school,
> A mother's love warms the cold winter.

### 47. Warm Three-room House
> Three wooden houses with terracotta walls,
> Parents and grandparents live together.
> The laughter of children happily chirping,
> A thousand years of blessings are like mountains and rivers.

### 48. Tears on Departure Day
> The daughter followed her husband across the river,
> The old mother stood waiting with tears in her eyes.
> Advise your child to maintain good behavior as a daughter-in-law,
> Respect your mother and worship your father with full devotion.

### 49. Nan fan lulls a child's dream
> The bamboo fan blows in the gentle breeze,
> Mom stayed up for me all night long.
> Repel mosquitoes and repel baby flies to sleep,
> A mother's love is as vast as a gentle stream.

### 50. The Tao of Filial Respect for Parents
> I thank my parents to the sky,
> Early in the day, the servant dares to take care of the bag.
> Một chén trà thơm, lời hỏi han,  
> Fulfill the word Filial piety and happiness.

### 51. Grandma's Betel
> The betel nut she chewed turned her lips red,
> Telling fairy tales under the moonlight.
> Hundred-knot bamboo tree, young man Thach Sanh,
> Teach your children to behave and keep their hearts pure.

### 52. Carrying Firewood in the Winter Afternoon
> Father carried dry firewood and walked down the hill,
> The shirt and conical hat are soaked with sweat.
> Gathering a fire to warm the little cubs,
> I love my father who has worked hard all his life.

### 53. Pot of Onion Porridge to Relieve Colds
> Mom cooked a pot of hot onion porridge,
> Feeding your child makes you sweat.
> The warmth of love drives away illness,
> Healthy and full of joy and laughter.

### 54. Father's Teachings by the Fire
> My child, keep your heart upright,
> Hungry and torn until clean, torn until fragrant.
> Although poor and not greedy for money,
> Leave a good name for your grandchildren.

### 55. Jackfruit Wooden Cradle
> The jackfruit wooden cradle he carved,
> Rocking your baby to a sweet sleep.
> Three generations passed down deep love,
> Family roots are forever intact.

### 56. Tears Welcome Back Your Child
> I go to work thousands of miles away,
> The day I returned, I was happy to pass the summer.
> Hugging his thin old mother and crying,
> Happiness and peace at the old home.

### 57. A bowl of green tea for you
> I held a bowl of fragrant green tea,
> Let him sit and watch the sunset.
> He patted my head and smiled kindly,
> The family is peaceful and harmonious in the village.

### 58. Roasted Cassava in the Lost Season
> Year of drought, crop failure, lack of rice bowls,
> Mom divided deliciously grilled cassava roots.
> Give me a big piece, mom eats a small one,
> Tears fell through the blue sky.

### 59. Remembering Your Ancestors and Origins
> The first day of the full moon day lights up the mind,
> Thank our Ancestors for their hard work.
> Exploring mountains, breaking rocks and building villages,
> Leaving behind many children and grandchildren with good fortune.

### 60. Family Reunion on New Year's Eve
> New Year's Eve steamed banh chung,
> My grandchildren gathered around to raise a cup of tea.
> Wishing you many more years of life,
> Peace radiates throughout every home!

### 61. Tich Dien Ceremony of the King Plowing
> In early spring, the king went down to the fields to plow deeply,
> The plowed road opens the way to long-lasting prosperity.
> Hundreds of families cheered for the festival,
> Pray for good rice with mulberry seeds.

### 62. Red River Water Procession

> Scoop clear water into a pink enamel cup.
> Offering the Altar to Heaven to pray for good rain,
> Produces lush crops all year round.

### 63. The Sound of Kite Flute on the Dyke

> The wind-filled kite soars into the distance.

> The soul of the countryside soars in song.

### 64. Early Spring Wrestling Festival
> Drums stir up the bustle of the village,
> Village boys take off their shirts and wear red loincloths.
> Legs clamp the neck, turn the pieces face up,
> Thousands of years of stubborn martial arts!

### 65. Banh Chung and Banh Day to Celebrate Heaven and Earth

### 66. Dragon Boat Racing Association

### 67. Giao Long Tattoo

### 68. Ceremony of New Rice Offering to Heaven's Altar

### 69. Throwing Balls Festival
> Five-colored shuttlecocks flying high,

### 70. Sing Then on the Moonlit Night

> Pray for peace and banish evil spirits,
> Make the village prosperous and peaceful.

### 71. Spring Forest Torchlighting Association
> Torches flicker in the middle of the village,
> The bustling gongs resonated in thousands of directions.
> The winding circle next to the fire,
> Heaven and earth, people and animals are intertwined with love.

### 72. Tug of War Game
> The flexible rope pulls both sides,
> Cheers echoed through the bamboo groves.
> Pull east to the sunrise bridge,
> For a bumper harvest of rice.

### 73. Releasing Full Moon Flowers in July
> Thousands of candles float down the river,
> Revealing the deep remembrance of one's roots.
> Pray for the liberation of all sentient beings,
> Radiant human love in human life.

### 74. Peach Blossom Market on Tet Day

> The Tet market is bustling with vendors.

> Welcoming fortune and blessings.

### 75. Thanksgiving Ceremony at the beginning of the year

### 78. Dragon Dance on the Full Moon Night

### 79. Campfire in Muong Village

### 80. Harvest Festival

## 🤝 BOOK V: PEOPLE & CITIZENS — Neighbourhood, Compassion & Philosophy of Life (LESSON 81 - 100)

### 81. I Love You So Much
> My dear, I love you so much,
> Even though they are different, they are the same rig.
> Born from the same mother's womb,
> Fifty-four branches of one Vietnamese house.

### 82. Two Voices of the People
> Remember the flood from long ago,
> Mother Gourd survives the sun and rain.
> The two words "Compatriot" echo forever,
> Love and care are not enough.

### 83. Healthy Leaves Cover Torn Leaves
> Healthy leaves cover torn leaves,
> Divide into a bowl of rice sprinkled with salt.
> When it gets dark, the lights go out together,
> Neighborly love is as bright as a mirror.

### 84. Da San Dinh Original Water Well
> The banyan tree on the water dock and the roof of the old communal house,
> A place where people gather in the sun and rain.
> Respect the elderly and love the young with abundant love,
> Rustic features that have not faded for thousands of years.

### 85. Bowl of Robusta Tea Inviting Strangers
> Strangers passing by stop by to rest,
> The old lady brought a bowl of clear water.
> Do not take money for hospitality,
> Rich in love and affection in the Southern heaven.

### 86. Village Elder Tells the Story of the Flood
> Next to the red fire, the old man talked drunkenly,
> The story goes that the Gourd saved this life.
> The floods rose and flooded the mountain tops,
> Thanks to God's mercy to save humanity.

### 87. Xom Tuong Than Village
> Whose house has a new roof built?
> The whole neighborhood gathered to lend a hand.
> No credit, laugh out loud,
> Neighbors are peaceful and warm.

### 88. Bach Viet Bamboo Tree
> Green bamboo preserves the land, preserves the ancient village,
> Tough to endure wind and rain.
> Weaving rafters to make crossbows, weaving basket boats,
> With people who weathered storms in the past.

### 89. Food When You're Hungry with a Packet When You're Full
> A bite when hungry is worth thousands of gold,
> A package of sticky rice divided in half during difficult times.
> Deep love for those in need,
> Engrave the bones and heart with iron and cast iron.

### 90. Value people with virtue more than talent
> Thousands of years of virtue radiate light,
> Illegitimate talent turns to ashes.
> Live transparently, with an upright heart,
> Leave blessings throughout the world.

### 91. Giving Up Rice and Sharing Clothes
> A full person gives a portion to the hungry,
> Healthy clothes protect dirty clothes.
> A country that has been dedicated to humanity for thousands of years,
> Overcoming the storm and shining a kind heart.

### 92. Countryside Market Exchanges Rice for Salt
> Don't need money in the countryside market,
> Exchange a basket of fragrant rice for a basket of shrimp.
> Trust each other without deception,
> Peaceful, rustic, warm fragrant village.

### 93. Children Go To School To Learn The Words From The Teacher
> Thatched house with bamboo walls,
> The teacher corrected the letters of the past.
> Teach your children Filial piety and Meaning,
> Be an upright person, morning, afternoon, and noon.

### 94. Children's Laughter in the Courtyard
> Playing hide and seek around the old banyan tree,
> Clear laughter filled the sky far away.
> Peaceful childhood in my mother's hometown,
> Beautiful song of joy for thousands of years.

### 95. Bowl of Gratitude Porridge Feeding the Sick
> Neighbors cook bowls of soup,
> Visit sick people to get well soon.
> Neighborly love is better than blood relatives,
> Warm, sparkling human love.

### 96. Ancient Village Banyan Tree
> Hundred years of standing guard at the village gate,
> Provide shade for people walking home.
> Roots are planted deep in the warm mother earth,
> Proof of a thousand-year oath.

### 97. Giving Thanks to the Land Collector
> Offering incense to thank ancestors,
> Reclaiming land and establishing villages.
> Forest tarpaulins build dikes to prevent rough waves,
> Let our children and grandchildren now enjoy peace.

### 98. When Drinking Water, Remember Its Source
> Drink a bowl of clear water and remember your roots,
> When eating fruit, remember the person who planted the tree.
> Thousand-year-old morality of Vietnamese people,
> Deeply engrave your mind every month and every day.

### 99. Non River One Unity
> Fifty-four brotherly nations,
> Durable agglomerated hand grip.
> Despite experiencing three thousand trials,
> One heart to preserve the country.

### 100. Dai Hoan Ca Xa Tac Thai Binh
> The country has been at peace for thousands of years,
> The Heavenly Altar radiates the sunrise.
> Offer a bowl of spring water to thank God,
> Wishing everyone prosperity and prosperity!
> Bach Viet's entire autumn resounds with song,
> Dear compatriots, forever grateful!
