## 🌿 II. THE NATURE OF INCENSE OFFERING IN ORIGINAL BUDDHISM

### 1. Early Buddhism in India (VI century BC):
* **Geographical context:** India's climate is extremely hot and humid, many people gather to listen to lectures in Ky Vien forest (*Jetavana*).
* **Original origin of burning incense:** Shakyamuni Buddha and his disciples initially used **Sandalwood** and **Agarwood** powder to burn in the monastery room for two practical purposes:
  1. Eliminates body odor and cools the hot tropical air.
  2. Repel insects, snakes, and mosquitoes in the jungle.
* **Symbolization later:** Gradually, the fragrant smoke that spread without distinction between high and low was philosophized into **"Gioi incense, Concentration incense, Tue incense"** (The scent of inner purity).

## ⛩️ III. PROCESS OF IMMIGRATION INTO VIETNAM THROUGH PERIOD

### 1. Phase 1: Luy Lau Buddhist Center (1st – 2nd Century AD) — Localizing peace:
* Indian monks (like Khau Da La - Kaundinya) followed merchant ships to Luy Lau port (Bac Ninh) before Buddhism spread to Luoyang (China).
* **Interference with Dong Son culture:** When arriving at Giao Chi, the monks immediately received the source of **Dai Viet Agarwood (Aquilaria crassna)** and continued to burn the bronze censers of the Lac Viet people.
* The belief in wet rice agriculture has "localized" Buddhism into **Four Dharmas** (Phap Van - God of Clouds, Phap Vu - God of Rain, Phap Loi - God of Thunder, Phap Dien - God of Lightning) - essentially the belief of praying for good rain and wind of Bach Viet wet rice residents.

### 2. Phase 2: Ly - Tran Dynasties (11th - 13th centuries) — Thuan Viet Zen Buddhism (Truc Lam Yen Tu):
* During the Ly - Tran dynasties, Zen learning was **liberal, worldly, patriotic and closely linked to the nature of Dai Viet country**:
  * King Tran Nhan Tong founded the Truc Lam Zen sect on the windy Yen Tu peak, advocating: *"If you live in the world, you will be happy, let go of the path according to fate / Co Tac xan, don't be miserable, follow the fate"* (In life, if you are happy, follow the path / When you are hungry, just eat until you are full, sleep immediately when you are tired).
  * **Practice space:** Pine forests, thousands of streams, rustic thatched porches, incense smoke drifting through the mountain breeze.

### 3. Phase 3: Late Le - Nguyen Dynasty (17th - 19th Centuries) — Stage of Foreign Hybridization:
* This was the period when Northern Buddhism penetrated strongly from China, bringing with it a heavy hybridization of **Northern witchcraft Taoism and folk Tantra**:
  * Introducing complex systems of gods, La Sat prison, and a gloomy underworld into temples.
  * The following customs appear: **Stereotyped rattle bells, reciting sutras in Sino-Vietnamese sounds that do not understand the meaning, offering sacrifices, cutting predestined relationships, burning votive paper, fortune-telling, and praying for fortune**.
  * Incense smoke from being a *pure fragrance offered to Heaven and Earth* turned into *a blinding incense smoke praying for personal gain*.
