## 🏔️ PART I: LEGENDARY BIOGRAPHY & STORY OF 20 THAISON BAC DAU

---

### 1. Phu Van Thi Tien — Huyen Giac Chan Nhan
* **Personality:** Taoist hermit on Phu Van peak (Yen Tu), who holds the secret technique of 9-burnt bamboo sword combined with meditation.
* **Legendary story:** At the age of 20, he carried his sword and traveled throughout 100 Bach Viet tribes in search of the ultimate morality. After standing on Ngoa Van peak during a thunderstorm, he realized: *"A sharp sword is not equal to a pure heart, a sharp sword cannot surpass the way of Heaven"*. He broke the steel sword, replaced it with a bamboo sword, and used the wolf quill as his weapon.
* **Descendants:** **Moc Tinh Lan** (inherited the Zen mind & medical path) and **Dung Si An Thai** (inherited the moveless swordsmanship).

### 2. Six Headed Crazy Soldier — Tran Nguyen Han
* **Personality:** The naval war poet in the six-way intersection of Luc Dau River, with a generous and carefree personality, plays the Day Dan in one hand and holds a short rice leaf knife in the other.
* **Legendary story:** Once a boat sailed alone in the middle of a big storm on the Kinh Thay River, drinking wine and playing the lute singing *Luc Dau Trieu Lang*. When the Mongol invaders invaded, he led 500 suicide soldiers to dive to the bottom of the river, punctured 30 enemy warships, then looked up and laughed amid the red alluvial water.
* **Descendant:** **Admiral Nguyen Thuy Luc** (ancestor of An Thai).

### 3. Chu Dau Hoa Thanh — Wood Than Crane
* **Personality:** Third generation elder of Chu Dau pottery village, master of water and ink painting on celadon and brown ceramic.
* **Legendary story:** Spent 40 years of my life researching the white clay of the Kinh Thay River mixed with rice husk and rice ash. His painting *"Thien Co Lien Hoa"* on a 1m high ceramic vase fired at 1,200°C created a miracle: the blue enamel color transformed virtually with the sunlight, illuminating the delicate dew-filled lotus shadow down to each leaf vein.
* **Descendant:** Daughter **Moc Tinh Lan** (Great Artist Chu Dau).

### 4. Dong Son Dong Than — Mr. Nguyen Kim Duc
* **Personality:** Legendary bronze casting artist of the Van Lang - Dong Son period, who holds the secret to casting melted wax and determining the sound of the Dong Son Drum.
* **Legendary story:** When he was 30 years old, he dreamed of seeing a Lac bird flying through the sun with 14 rays of light. Waking up, he gathered brass, tin and pure gold from the whole tribe, burned the furnace continuously for 7 days and nights to create the first Dong Son Ngoc Lu Drum. When he beat the beat, the sound of the drum echoed 100 miles away, making animals prostrate and people's hearts at peace.
* **Descendant:** **Nguyen Kim Lac** (Head of Co Loa bronze casting ward).

### 5. Duong Tu Ba Tien — Ancient Fisherman
* **Personality:** A 90-year-old fisherman lives on a bamboo boat floating along the South Truong Giang and the Red River, holding 1,000 ancient Bach Viet folk songs.
* **Legendary story:** No one knows his real name, only that he memorized the song *Viet Nhan Ca* from the Luong Chu period 5,300 years ago. Every time a big storm arose, he put down his oar and played the doomsday string instrument. The sound of the instrument made the wind and waves automatically subside.
* **Transmission:** **Old Do Ba Ben** (the ferryman on Van Kiep wharf).

### 6. Truc Lam Medicine Saint — Great Master Tue Tinh
* **Personality:** The Holy Father of Vietnamese Herbal Medicine, the founder of the philosophy *"Nam Duoc Treats Men"*.
* **Legendary story:** Traveled throughout 580 mountains in the South, tasting every species of grass, tree, root and leaf to record the *Southern Medicine Divine Effect*. He refused the position of royal official, choosing Yen Tu grass hut as a place to make medicine to save poor people and heal war invalids with herbs combined with Co Nhac music.
* **Descendant:** **Medical Master Nuong Moc Tinh Lan**.

### 7. Mesmerizing Elephant King — Female Hero Vu Bat Nan
* **Personality:** Female general Tien La under the banner of Hai Ba Trung, master of the staff and riding elephants into battle.
* **Legendary story:** Riding a white elephant with two tusks wrapped in a bronze spear, waving a 40-pound iron staff, beating Dong Son drums to conquer 65 Linh Nam citadels, chasing away To Dinh invaders.
* **Descendant:** **Female Admiral Le Thi Chan** (the person who explored the land of Hai Phong).

### 8. Bach Dang Swordsman — Former Ngo Vuong Ngo Quyen
* **Origin:** Master of military and swordsmanship during the Autonomous period, inventor of the Bach Dang underground stake battlefield in 938.
* **Legendary story:** With his genius vision of the tide, he sharpened thousands of iron-clad ironwood trees and planted them in the Bach Dang riverbed, burying the Southern Han army, ending 1,000 years of Northern domination, opening an era of eternal independence.
* **Descendant:** **National Duke Tiet Trung Hung Dao Great Vuong Tran Quoc Tuan**.

### 9. Tay Ho Tra Thanh — Academic Diep Moc Chi
* **Personality:** Master of ancient style tea ceremony and Thang Long ca tru library.
* **Legendary story:** The person who created the art of marinating lotus tea in Tay Ho: rowed a dugout boat at midnight, put pinches of fragrant tea into dew-covered lotus buds and tied them, early the next morning caught every drop of morning dew on the lotus leaves to boil water to make tea.
* **Transmission:** **Diep Thanh Tam** (Female Knight in the Lotus Dress).

### 10. Co Loa Than Co — General Cao Lo
* **Origin:** The greatest military engineer of ancient times, the inventor of the **Divine Lien Chau Crossbow** and the 9-ring Loa Citadel.
* **Legendary story:** Researching the elasticity of buffalo horns and wild mulberry wood, he created a magic crossbow that shot hundreds of three-sided bronze arrows at once, powerfully penetrating all iron armor, firmly protecting the Au Lac river.
* **Descendant:** **Bui Thuy Loi** (Captain of Van Kiep Crossbowmen).
