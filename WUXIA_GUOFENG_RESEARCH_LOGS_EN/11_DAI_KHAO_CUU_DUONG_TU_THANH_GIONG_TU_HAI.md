1. **Wangcheng of Liangzhu (5,300 years ago - Lower Yangtze, Zhejiang):**
   * **Scale:** More than 3 million $m^2$ wide, built with basalt soil and load-bearing clay, surrounded by moats and a system of 11 artificial irrigation dams to control the earliest flood water in humanity.
   * **Central Palace (Mac Giac Son):** An artificial mound 10m high, 300,000 $m^2$ wide, on top are rows of monolithic wooden pillars with a diameter of more than 1 meter.
   * **Jade Culture:** Unearthed thousands of treasures **Jade Tong, Jade Tong** carved images of *Gods riding Divine Beasts* - proving that Bach Viet society had a regime of kings, nobility and a powerful army.
2. **Rich Gui Capital — Shijiahe & Khuatjialing (4,500 years ago - Dongting Lake):**
   * Located in the middle of the Yangtze River, surrounding Dong Dinh Lake (where the Kinh Duong Vuong Ancestor established his residence). This is a complex of ancient citadels and moats with the early bronze and jade dragon statue casting industry.
3. **The Imperial Capital of Vietnam (2,500 years ago - Spring and Autumn Period of the Warring States):**
   * The palace's upper floors reflect on the Tien Duong river, where the thousand-year-old stainless sword **Viet Vuong Cau Tien** was cast, combined with thousands of warships fighting on the rivers and seas.

## ⚡ PART II: THE HISTORICAL NATURE OF THE HOLY GONG FIGHTING THE ENEMIES

### 1. Which country is An in history?
* **The Kingdom of An** was the **Shang Dynasty (An Shang - 1600 BC - 1046 BC)** in the northern Yellow River basin (the capital was *An Khu* - An Duong, Henan, present-day China).
* During the reign of King **Vu Dinh (Vu Dinh Trung Hung - 13th century BC)**, the An dynasty launched a large-scale war of expansion and invasion south of the Yangtze River to rob copper and tin ores and land from the Bach Viet ethnic groups.

### 2. Which Bach Viet country did Saint Giong help?
* When the An army invaded the South, **King Hung (Hung Huy Vuong - Hung Dinh Vuong of Van Lang country / Bach Viet Alliance)** faced the risk of destruction.
* **The image of Saint Giong (Phu Dong Thien Vuong):**
  * Representing the **Iron Age technological leap** of the Vietnamese people: The boy stretches to become a tall warrior, wearing **Iron Armor**, riding an **Iron Horse**, holding an **Iron Whip**.
  * When the iron whip broke, he **plucked bamboo and ivory** from the side of the road and continued to wipe out the enemy $\rightarrow$ Symbol of the combination of *Advanced iron and steel weapons* and *Rustic weapons of Dai Viet's great nature*.
  * Defeating the An enemy at the foot of Soc mountain (Soc Son), Giong flew to heaven $\rightarrow$ Immortal symbol of the spirit of defending the country without regard for fame and profit.
