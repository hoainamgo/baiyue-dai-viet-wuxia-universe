# 20 ANCIENT VIETNAMESE CONCEPTS — CANONIZATION

> **Preferred Source:** [`58_CANON_VISUAL_DONG_A_TRAN_1225_1400.md`](58_CANON_VISUAL_DONG_A_TRAN_1225_1400.md)
> Concepts using fictional characters An Thai - Moc Tinh Lan are all creative, not historical memoirs.

## General rules

- Each scene has a landmark, season, hour, main occupation, materials and traces of wear and tear.
- Giao Long is a river tattoo/totem; do not turn into a Northern dragon flying in the sky.
- Before the war, Dong A did not use elephants, pagoda towers, Western artillery, kimonos or Han costumes.
- An Thai specializes in naval warfare; Moc Tinh Lan specializes in ceramics, herbs and caring for wounded soldiers.

## 1. Mother Earth Banh Chung

- **Scene:** Kitchen painted before Tet, green dong leaves, hot water pot, thermometer and tray.
- **Activity:** An Thai chopped into pieces; Moc Tinh Lan wraps the cake and ties it with bamboo instead of plastic string.
- **Material:** Dong leaves, bamboo slices, rice, beans, clay pot, charcoal.

## 2. Ben Chu Dau Betel Shrimp

- **Scene:** Late afternoon at the wharf requires water, green and red betel leaves.
- **Activity:** The couple exchanged betel leaves as a promise before going to war.
- **Material:** Betel leaves, lime juice, areca bark, dried dong leaves.

## 3. Ben Cat Song Hong

- **Context:** Low water season, sandy beach and reeds, afternoon wind makes the edges of leaves stand up.
- **Activity:** Moc Tinh Lan draws lotus flowers on Do paper cards; An Thai reinstalled the cracked jar handle.
- **Materials:** Sand, reeds, dong leaves, do paper, ink, clay pot.

## 4. Bronze Crossbow and Arrows

- **Context:** Preserving the crossbow in the workshop, sunlight shines on the green bronze blade.
- **Activity:** The craftsman checks the crossbow string; An Thai and Moc Tinh Lan learn how to read materials.
- **Material:** Ironwood, horn, copper, silk thread, animal skin.

## 5. Rainy Season Deserted Island

- **Scene:** Black rocky beach, gray sky, waves breaking into tropical forest.
- **Activity:** Check water sources, ropes and dry food; Do not explore "uninhabited islands".
- **Material:** Salt, moss, seashells, rope, oilcloth.

## 6. Ivory Bamboo Forest

- **Scene:** Forest on the mountainside, light filtering through the foliage, sudden showers.
- **Activity:** Block bamboo trees, reinforce tent bundles and train cones.
- **Material:** Ivory bamboo, lian rope, stone, rainwater, campfire.

## 7. Tan Vien Stone Cave

- **Scene:** Mossy cliffs, small fires, the smell of rotting leaves and the sound of underground water.
- **Activity:** Prepare water, medicine and vines; no divine demon beast appears.
- **Material:** Stone, hemp rope, fire, ceramic bowl, medicinal leaves.

## 8. Me Linh Soldier Statue Memories

- **Context:** A historical memory of the Trung Sisters period; An Thai – Moc Tinh Lan only appears in the modern retrospective class.
- **Historical images:** Female generals and male soldiers are equipped with wooden armor, spears, drums, and bronze shields; elephants only appear if the subject requests it and is cited.
- **Material:** Wood, leather, brass, silk thread, drum.

## 9. East Sea After Ba Trieu

- **Context:** The East Sea has big waves during a storm, small boats avoid the reefs.
- **Activities:** Observe the water flow, fix oars, share food; keeps the epic feel without turning Ba Trieu into a super-powered character.
- **Material:** Wood, cordage, mesh, tar cloth, salt.

## 10. East Sea Tu Hai Dynasty

- **Setting:** Sixteenth century seaport, brown sailboat, lighthouse on cliff.
- **Activities:** Check sails, cargo and news; An Thai – Moc Tinh Lan appeared as guests.
- **Material:** Sails, ropes, ironwood, hand-painted maps, enamel.

## 11. Luong Chu Relic Site

- **Context:** The relic site and tomb foundation are made of stone, the roof is preserved, no fake "Jade Great Hall" is built.
- **Activity:** Observe the tomb foundation, ceramics, jade and irrigation system; distinguishes Luong Chu heritage from Dong Son.
- **Material:** Stone, terracotta, jade, bamboo, modern conservation.

## 12. Lotus Tea at Sunset

- **Scene:** Lotus garden after the rain, tea table under the canopy, steam rising.
- **Activity:** Moc Tinh Lan brews tea; An Thai fixed the pot handle and prepared dinner.
- **Material:** Lotus leaves, water tapioca, ceramic bowl, jackfruit wood, oil lamp.

## 13. Ben River Fair Night

- **Scene:** Riverside market, paper lanterns, boats carrying vegetables and fish.
- **Activities:** The salesman buys and sells, the cosmetics carrier, two characters help find the way when there is a traffic jam.
- **Material:** Bamboo, leaves, do paper, silk, bamboo, fish, cake.

## 14. Practice Close Combat Leg Attacks

- **Context:** Ready ground at Luc Dau, not aerial techniques.
- **Activity:** Practice techniques for gripping necks, clinging to walls, and throwing heavy objects; controlled training.
- **Material:** Soil, woven gloves, training poles, rough fabric.

## 15. Poem on the Sea Cliff

- **Scene:** Foggy Ha Long Bay, boat through cliffs, salt water and moss.
- **Activity:** The instructor introduces the engraving; The character copied it instead of claiming to be an authentic treasure.
- **Materials:** Stone, squid, wooden boat, rope and sharpening stick.

## 16. Planting Rice in the Afternoon

- **Scene:** Flooded rice fields, slanting afternoon light, early storm in the distance.
- **Activity:** Both in real groups, divide pillow pots, check ditches and agricultural warehouses.
- **Material:** Young bamboo, water, bamboo, conical hat, bottle handle.

## 17. Rainy Night at the Ferry Station

- **Scene:** Wooden shop, rain knocking on the roof, empty riverbank.
- **Activity:** Moc Tinh Lan taps the beat to keep the beat; An Thai repairs stringed instruments and distributes food.
- **Material:** Wood, fiber, bamboo sticks, ceramic bowls, candles.

## 18. Tuyet Mau Son

- **Scene:** High pass, snow clinging to pine branches, cold wind, oil lamp in hut.
- **Activities:** Repair wires, check wages and keep warm; Do not turn the pass into a steppe.
- **Materials:** Snow, wood, oil, textiles, raw metal.

## 19. Eagle On The Border Mountain

- **Context:** Northwest rocky peak under vast forest, low clouds, wind from the border.
- **Activity:** Observe animal tracks and wind; Archery training on targets is not called releasing birds for hunting.
- **Materials:** Stone, feathers, leather, string, wooden bow, salary bag.

## 20. Chu Dau Pottery Kiln Khai Hoan

- **Background:** After the war, the village called to reopen the pottery kiln; turntable, ash, glaze and assembled ceramic pieces.
- **Activity:** An Thai and Moc Tinh Lan exchanged the newly fired vase and signed the time with the community.
- **Outcome:** There is no royal will; vitality comes from profession, community and common work.
