## 📜 PART II: SELECTION OF 100 EXAMS FROM PHU HOAN NGUYEN SINGING (5 GREAT VOLUMES)

Below is the structure and typical representative poems in 100 masterpiece poems:

### 🌊 BOOK 1: THE BLOOD WAR OF THE SCHOOL & THE EASTERN MARINE WAR A (20 ARTICLES)
*(Theme: Sat That, Bach Dang underground stake, short sword with rice leaves, the spirit of thousands of miles of mountains and rivers)*

#### ⚔️ Lesson 01: *Bach Dang Da Nguyet Dao Quang* (Author: Bach Dang Kiem Guest · Passed by: An Thai)
> *Rolling waves with thousands of moonlight shining,*
> *Short blade with cold rice leaves.*
> *Forest of stakes buried thousands of enemies,*
> *The river is a strip of eternal light.*
> *Tonight raise a cup at the front of the ship,*
> *Smiles arrogantly and sends the wind drifting away!*

#### ⚔️ Lesson 02: *Heading Out to the Luc Dau Giang Army* (Author: Tran Nguyen Han)
> *Green Head of red water rolling with silt,*
> *The third watch drum is filled with the sound of cocks.*
> *The embroidered flag of Sat That flutters in the wind,*
> *Swear to protect our country!*

*(... Collection continues from Lesson 03 to Lesson 20: *Phu Van Tram Qi, Van Kiep Pulling the Bow, Mong Dong Going into Battle, Cao Lo Arrow...*)*

---

### ☁️ BOOK 2: TRUC LAM MEDITATION & SACRIFICE TO LO THIEN HEAVEN (20 LESSON)
*(Theme: Heavenly Altar without incense and smoke, offering clear spring water & golden rice grains, liberating the dust of the world)*

#### ☀️ Lesson 21: *Thien Lo Dien Altar* (Author: Huyen Giac Chan Nhan)
> *Arrange marble slabs on top of thousands of mountains,*
> *The wind is blowing in all directions and the clouds are vast.*
> *Offering a bowl of water to a calm mind,*
> *Offers a bowl of golden rice to thank God.*
> *No incense, no smoke, no evil images,*
> *A night of steadfastness that shines everywhere.*

#### ☁️ Lesson 22: *Crouching Clouds in the Rainy Night* (Author: Buddha Emperor Tran Nhan Tong)
> *Rain falling on the bamboo roof with elegant drops,*
> *Holds the sword and looks at the jade light.*
> *The world of fame and fortune is like bubbles,*
> *Meditating one thought can reach the sky.*

*(... Collection continues from Lesson 23 to Lesson 40: *The Morning Bell of Phu Van, Stream Reo Yen Tu, Seven Colors of the Rainbow...*)*

---

### 🏺 BOOK 3: MEN LAM CHU DAU & THANK YOU (20 LESSON)
*(Theme: Ceramic wheel on a rainy night, blue lotus drawing, dividing the promised ceramic piece in half, eternal love)*

#### 🌸 Lesson 41: *Poetry for Chu Dau Ceramic Vase Bottom* (Author: Moc Than Hac · Inheritance: Moc Tinh Lan)
> *White soil Sutra Master molds a lotus flower,*
> *The thousand-degree blazing fire turned into blue glaze.*
> *Send half as a souvenir,*
> *Even though it is weathered, the skin will not fray.*

#### 🏺 Lesson 42: *Tea Porch at Full Moon Night* (Author: Diep Moc Chi)
> *Jade well water boils lotus tea buds,*
> *Smoke obscures the moon's shadow.*
> *Nâng chén đàm đạo câu tri kỷ,*  
> *Forget all the wandering around in despicable dusty places.*

*(... Collection continues from Lesson 43 to Lesson 60: *Brown Flower Pottery, Releasing Lanterns from Luoc River, Double Broken Bowl...*)*

---

### 🌿 BOOK 4: MEDICAL HERBS & 580 SOUTH MEDICINES (20 LESSON)
*(Theme: Men's medicine to treat men, ink grass, betel leaves, lotus flowers, ancient music to heal)*

#### 🌿 Lesson 61: *Song of Nam Duoc Saves Soldiers* (Author: Great Master Tue Tinh)
> *The ink grass along the ditch stops the bleeding,*
> *Lá trầu rễ nứa ấm lòng son.*  
> *Five hundred and eighty species of plants,*
> *Nurturing Lac Hong breed for thousands of years.*

#### 🌿 Lesson 62: *Harmony of Ancient Music Y Dao Khuc* (Author: Moc Tinh Lan)
> *Strumming a string instrument under the moonlight,*
> *The sound of silk sixteen stops the anxiety.*
> *The scarred wound is now closed,*
> *The soul is as peaceful as the clouds.*

*(... Collection continues from Lesson 63 to Lesson 80: *Area Root, Tam That Nuong Da, Pomelo Fragrance Afternoon Smoke...*)*

---

### 🗡️ BOOK 5: THE GAMBLING DAO & THE PROUD CAMERA (20 LESSON)
*(Theme: Qiao Chou, "Ha ha ha ha" laughter, escape from worldly life, proud of heaven and earth)*

#### 🗡️ Lesson 81: *Smiling Arrogantly Dong A Great Xuong* (Author: Lao Ngu & Tran Nguyen Han)
> *LAUGHTER... THE SEA WAVES ARE RISE!*
> *SOUNDING Flute... INCREDIBLE AUTOBIOGRAPHY!*
> *Sword guarding the Luc Dau riverbank,*
> *The bamboo wine gourd dries up and sends the clouds drifting.*
> *Fame and profit are fleeting like a dream,*
> *Arrogant smile that won't move for a thousand years!*

#### 🗡️ Lesson 82: *Thuong Hai Nhat Thanh Tieu Bach Viet Khuc* (Author: 20 Thai Dau Chorus)
> *Ha ha ha ha! Immense wanderlust,*
> *Waves crash onto the sandy beach and the moonlight illuminates the clear stream.*
> *People come and go for generations,*
> *Only mountains and rivers remain!*
