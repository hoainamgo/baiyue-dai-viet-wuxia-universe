## 🤝 V. 40 CUSTOMS, TRADITIONS & TESTIMONY OF THE VILLAGE (FOLK TRADITIONS)

1. **The custom of "Exchange of Work" in the Harvesting Season:** The whole neighborhood harvests together, rotating the fields from house to house, laughter drowning out the sounds of fatigue.
2. **The custom of taming betel and phoenix wings:** A piece of betel, skillfully tamed in the shape of a phoenix wing, with spicy lime and areca leaves, opens a meaningful story.
3. **Custom of the First Anniversary of Pork Portion:** A piece of boiled meat wrapped in banana leaves is given to an elderly person in a corner of the village to show kindness.
4. **Custom of Cooking Robusta Water Waiting for People from Plowing:** A copper kettle is placed at the end of the alley so that anyone who comes home from working in the fields can quench their thirst.
5. **Custom of Lighting Fires for Heat at Winter Nights at the Alleyway:** A pile of dry bamboo stumps smolders at the corner of the street for the night watchman to warm up.
6. **Custom of Offering New Rice on Harvest Day:** Offering a bowl of sticky rice at the beginning of the season to heaven and earth to give thanks for good weather and good weather, the smell of new rice fragrantly fills the house.
7. **The custom of tying the wrist with a red thread to pray for peace:** The grandmother tied a red thread to her child's wrist, hoping that he would eat well and grow up quickly.
8. **Singing Tradition on the Field Bank:** The male team cultivating rice sings back and forth to tease the female team on the other side of the ditch, making the fields noisy.
9. **Custom of Carrying Well Water on a Moonlit Night:** A pair of heavy corrugated iron barrels carry each load of clear rainwater to fill the jars.
10. **The Custom of Visiting Each Other's Bowl of Soup When Sick:** A bowl of steaming onion and perilla porridge or fresh ginger branches are brought by relatives to ease the fever.
