### 🔍 DETAILED RESEARCH OF THE MOST FEATURED STORIES:

1. **Muong People — Flood of Si Tree (*Mo Muong: Giving birth to land, giving birth to water*):**
   * *Content:* It rained heavily for 90 days and nights, uprooting the giant Si tree. Dense branches and leaves blocked the river and sky, causing water to flood the mountain. Ms. Da Kit and her two children crawled into a hollowed wooden gourd. After the water receded, they set up a shrine to thank God and gave birth to a prosperous generation.
2. **The Kho Mu - The Pregnant Lady Reborn All People (Dien Bien):**
   * *Content:* The God of Thunder punishes the world with a great flood. A gentle couple was told in a dream that they crawled into a sacred gourd carrying two chickens and some seeds. When the gourd reached the top of the mountain, the wife gave birth to a large gourd. When tapping on the gourd: the Xa people come out first, then the Thai people, Kinh people, Muong people... split up to reclaim land and start a business.
3. **Ba Na & Xo Dang People — Floating Elephant Skin Drum (Central Highlands):**
   * *Content:* The Water God's waves flooded the Ngoc Linh mountain forest. The two brothers crawled into an empty elephant skin drum, added a handful of seeds and a pair of babblers. The water receded, the birds flew to the ground, they went out to rebuild the village and vowed never to forget the Creator's grace to save their lives.
4. **Hmong people — Thunder Flood (Ha Giang):**
   * *Content:* The God of Thunder poured water over the tank. The couple climbed into a watertight round wooden drum and drifted for 100 days and nights until they hit the Ma Pi Leng cliff.

## 📜 PART II: CULTURAL TRAITS IN FOLK POETRY, SONGS & PROverbs

The memory of the Great Flood has not disappeared but has been transformed into every Vietnamese folk song, proverb, ritual and song:

---

### 1. 🍈 Folk Song "My Love, My Love" — Immortal Memories of the Flood
```text
« Bầu ơi thương lấy bí cùng,
Tuy rằng khác giống nhưng chung một giàn. »
```
* **Symbol decoding:** *"The same trellis"* and *"Different breeds"* in the folk subconscious are the memories of **all species and peoples taking refuge in the Mother Gourd on the waves and floods**. When coming from the same root, even though they have different customs and traditions, all people are still blood brothers.

---

### 2. 👑 The Two Words "FELLOWS" & The Legend of "Hundred Eggs"
* **Semantic origin:** **DONG (同 - Together)** + **BAO (胞 - Pregnancy wrap / Mother's womb)**.
* **Link:** The image of Mother Au Co giving birth to a clutch of a hundred eggs hatching a hundred children is the **artistic codification** from the memory of the Mother Gourd of salvation of 54 ethnic groups. All Vietnamese people are born from the same group, so they call each other **COMBINATIONS**.

---

### 3. 🌊 Epic of Son Tinh - Thuy Tinh — Memories of Treating Water After the Great Flood
```text
« Núi cao sông hãy còn dài,
Năm năm báo oán đời đời đánh ghen! »
```
* Reflecting the resilient efforts of the Red River wet rice residents: Building dikes to prevent floods, honoring Son Tinh (Tan Vien God) as the First Phuc God to protect life.
