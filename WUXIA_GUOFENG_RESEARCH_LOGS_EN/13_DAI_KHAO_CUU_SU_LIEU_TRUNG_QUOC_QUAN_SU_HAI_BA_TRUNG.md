## 🏛️ I. WHAT DO THE ANCIENT SCRIPTURES OF THE NORTHERN RECORD ABOUT "SOUTH PEOPLE" (BACH VIET)?

To understand why the Southerners rose so strongly, we need to look at what Northern historians (*Si Ma Qian, Pham Diep, Lich Daogen*) surprisingly admitted in official history:

```
                      [ CỔ THƯ PHƯƠNG BẮC GHI CHÉP VỀ BÁCH VIỆT ]
                                           │
    ┌──────────────────────────────────────┼──────────────────────────────────────┐

[ SỬ KÝ TƯ MÃ THIÊN ]             [ HẬU HÁN THƯ (PHẠM DIỆP) ]           [ HOÀI NAM TỬ & THỦY KINH CHÚ ]
 • "Đoản phát văn thân"            • "Trưng Trắc dũng hãn phát động"     • "Ở nước giỏi bơi, ở núi giỏi trèo"
 • Cắt tóc ngắn xăm mình           • 65 Thành Lĩnh Nam hưởng ứng         • Thạo dùng đoản đao, nỏ bắn tên độc
```

---

### 1. Excerpts from *Historical Records of Sima Qian* & *Huainanzi*:
* **Custom of "Doan Phat Van Than (Short hair cut, tattoo)":**
  * *History* records: Bach Viet people in the South cut their hair short and tattooed Giao Long (water dragon/canoe) all over their bodies so that when they dive into the rivers and seas to fish and fight, the sea monsters that see them will think they are of the same kind and will not harm them.
* **Personality & Combat Terrain:**
  * *Hoai Nam Tu* wrote: *"Vietnamese people in the South are good at swimming underwater, used to climbing passes and wading streams, using boats as carts, using oars as horses, suddenly appearing in the reeds of the swamp, the Northern army came thousands of miles away but could not catch them"*.

### 2. Excerpt from *The Book of the Later Han — Ma Yuan List of Stories*:
* **Admission about Hai Ba Trung:**
  * Pham Diep wrote: *"In the 16th year of Kien Vu (40), a woman in Giao Chi named Trung Trac and her younger sister Trung Nhi rebelled and attacked the district. Trung Trac was the daughter of the Lac general of Me Linh district, with a brave and decisive personality... The districts of Cuu Chan, Nhat Nam, and Hop Pho all responded, conquering 65 citadels in the Linh Nam region, establishing themselves as King"*.
* **Ma Yuan's horror:**
  * When Ma Vien (General Phuc Ba) brought 20,000 troops, he had to face the miasma of toxic gas, swamp lightning, and huge herds of war elephants, to the point that he had to exclaim: *"The land of Linh Nam is filled with toxic fog, poisonous water evaporates, birds flying in the sky fall into the water and die"*.

The Hai Ba Trung uprising completely defeated 65 strongholds in just a few months **not a spontaneous outbreak, but the culmination of the most elite military organization in ancient times**:

### 1. Infantry Division I: Me Linh Infantry Statue — Mobile Fortress:
* **Tactics "Overcoming Cavalry":**
  * Northern cavalry is proud to be invincible in the world, but Northern war horses are very sensitive to the smell and roar of elephants. When faced with a herd of roaring elephant soldiers, war horses automatically turned around in panic and trampled each other.
* **Statue tower structure:** The elephant carriage carries 4 female warriors carrying bronze crossbows and 4m long spears, forming an invulnerable cluster of long-range and short-range firepower.

### 2. Infantry Type II: Crossbow of Hundred Viet Gods & Arrows Tainted with Forest Trees:
* Inheriting Dong Son bronze casting technology and Co Loa crossbows, Hai Ba Trung's army owned tens of thousands of crossbows made of ironwood covered with buffalo horns.
* **Poison Arrow Specialty:** Using venom extracted from the latex of the sui tree (Bach Viet poison tree) and deep forest cobra venom, just a small scratch can cause the enemy general to be poisoned and die in battle within a few moments.

### 3. Infantry Division III: Female General Team & Close Martial Arts "Short Infantry Breaking Cavalry":
* **System of 72 Heroic Female Generals:**
  * **Female General Bat Nan (Vu Thi Thuc):** Guarding Tien La, brandishing two swords on the back of a war elephant.
  * **Female General Le Chan:** Established a base in Hai Phong, commanding the sea surface navy.
  * **Female General Thanh Thien:** Maneuvering military strategies, defending the Northern Pass.
* **Close combat martial arts:** The female soldier has a supple body, quickly sneaks under the foot of the war horse, uses the **Rice Leaf Short Knife** to cut off the tendons in the horse's leg and throws herself into the air using the **leg gripper** to knock the cavalry to the ground.
