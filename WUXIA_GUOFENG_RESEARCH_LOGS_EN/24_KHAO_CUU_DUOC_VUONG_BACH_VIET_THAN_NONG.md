## 🏛️ PART I: THE Ancestral Medicine Vuong THAN NONG — THE KING OF THE SOUTHERN HOT COUNTRY (BACH VIET)

### 1. Shen Nong (Yem Emperor) In History & Ancient Books:
* **Location & Origin:**
  * *Yem De* literally means **"King of the Hot Land" (Warm South around the Yangtze River basin and Dongting Lake)**.
  * According to *Dai Viet Su Ky Toan Thu* (Ngo Si Lien), **The Patriarch of the Vietnamese people was Kinh Duong Vuong (ruler of Xich Quy country) who was the third generation grandson of Emperor Yan of the Than Nong family**. Therefore, the entire Bach Viet ethnic group since ancient times have recognized Shen Nong as the founder of agriculture and medicine.
* **Legend "Shennong Tasting Hundreds of Medicinal Plants" (Shennong Tastes Hundred Herbs):**
  * In order to save the villagers from diseases and poisonous gas, the sacred forest and poisonous water, Shen Nong personally traveled throughout the thousands and tasted hundreds of types of plants, distinguishing which plants were curative and which plants were poisonous.
  * Legend has it that he had a red stick (*Xich Tieu Truong*) to tap on tree trunks to know the cold/heat properties, and had a transparent belly that could see through the internal organs to observe the reaction of herbs' medicinal properties.
  * This is the origin of the world's oldest medical book: **"Shen Nong Ban Thao Kinh"** - most of the precious medicinal plants come from the tropical monsoon climate of the South of Bach Viet (Mugwort, Wind Ginger, Cinnamon, Agarwood, Polygonum multiflora, Sam areca...).
