## 1. The Seven Core Worldview Pillars

### 1.1. Comparative Historical Discipline
- Honor folk mythology and oral lore without asserting unverifiable legends as empirical facts.
- Documented historical events must preserve exact names, dates, locations, and chronological sequences. Stories may be dramatized, but outcomes cannot be falsified.
- Fictional characters must be clearly designated as fictional; historical figures must not be used to mask fabricated events.

### 1.2. Geography & Native Ecology
- **Northern / Eastern Regions:** Lục Đầu River basin, Vạn Kiếp, Mount Yên Tử, Phù Vân, Chi Lăng pass, Đông Bộ Đầu.
- **Delta Plains:** Thăng Long imperial capital, Thiên Trường palace, Red River system, Đỉnh Nhĩ dyke, Chu Đậu ceramics village.
- **Maritime Coastal Zones:** Vân Đồn trade port, Bạch Đằng estuary, bamboo coracles (*thuyền nan*), monsoon cycles, tidal shifts.
- **Northwestern Highlands:** Dense tropical rainforests, terraced hills, waterfalls, herbal mineral streams. Never refer to these tropical landscapes as "steppes" or "deserts".

### 1.3. Material Culture & Daily Craftsmanship
- **Ceramics & Textiles:** Chu Đậu pottery, brown-patterned stoneware, underglaze blue, Vạn Phúc silk, Phong Khê *Dó* handmade bark paper.
- **Daily Artifacts:** Conical palm hats (*nón mê*), bamboo daybeds, woven rush mats, earthenware bowls, coconut-shell ladles, straw cooking hearths.
- **Military Gear:** Laminated leather armor, siege crossbows, long spears, war canoes, iron-tipped wooden stakes, leather-reinforced wooden shields. Must strictly match each historical era.
- Every described item must possess defined raw materials, fabrication methods, and visible traces of practical use.

### 1.4. Belief Systems & Theological Boundaries
- **Heaven Reverence (*Tế Trời*):** A foundational cultural worldview encompassing reverence for Heaven and Earth, oaths of integrity, thanksgiving to the Creator (*Ông Trời*), and communal solidarity.
- **Trúc Lâm Zen School:** Specific to the 13th-century Tran Dynasty; situated accurately in historical context without conflating with folk superstition, star worship, or magical talismans.
- **Sacred Hymn Tradition:** Strictly segregated as an independent biblical ecosystem; no cross-mixing of theology, ritual iconography, or characters.
- Martial disciplines and natural herbalism are never presented as magical immortality panaceas.

### 1.5. The Five Great Martial Sects (Ngũ Đại Môn Phái)

| Martial Sect | Domain & Environment | Key Archetypes / Roles | Signature Weapons / Materials | Core Value Ethos |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Lạc Long Thủy Phái** | Rivers, seas, delta marshlands | An Thái, Yết Kiêu | Short daggers, spears, war skiffs, fish skin gear | Valor, patriotism, river mastery |

*Note: Sect names and structural hierarchies are controlled worldbuilding concepts, not historical government ministries.*

### 1.6. Canon Character Guidelines
- **An Thái & Mộc Tĩnh Lan:** Fully original fictional protagonists; not historical persons.
- **Trần Quốc Tuấn, Yết Kiêu, Lê Phụ Trần, Trần Thủ Độ:** Verified historical figures; strictly bound by historical chronology and recorded roles.
- **Thạch Sanh, Sơn Tinh, Hai Bà Trưng, Lady Triệu:** Legendary and epic historical tiers; clearly categorized.
- The marital and emotional bond between An Thái and Mộc Tĩnh Lan is an original creative narrative anchored in filial piety, craftsmanship, and wartime resilience.

### 1.7. Tropical Wuxia Worldbuilding Realism
- All worldbuilding must be rooted in tropical and subtropical Southeast Asian ecology: rivers, mangrove swamps, monsoon storms, rice paddies.
- Characters operate through physical biomechanics, empirical tool use, and craft knowledge; no sudden technological anachronisms or ungrounded mystical powers.

## 3. Common Misconceptions to Avoid

### 3.1. Giao Long vs. Imperial Dragon
- **Giao Long:** The ancient aquatic totem of Dong Son, linked to river life, tattoos, canoes, and marshlands.
- **Imperial Court Dragon:** Formal dynastic regalia influenced by Sinic court protocols.
- Five-clawed northern court dragons must never be attributed to ancient indigenous Baiyue folk.

### 3.2. Disentangling the Three Mongol Invasions
- **1st Invasion (1257–1258):** Led by Uriyangkhadai; battle of Bình Lệ Nguyên, Lô River, Thiên Mạc, Đông Bộ Đầu counter-offensive.
- **2nd Invasion (1284–1285):** Led by Toghon, Sogetu, Omar; nationwide mobilization, Diên Hồng Conference.
- **3rd Invasion (1287–1288):** Led by Toghon, Zhang Wenhu (grain fleet destroyed at Vân Đồn), Omar; decisive Bạch Đằng River trap.
- Never mix commanders, naval fleets, troop counts, or battlefield locations across these three distinct campaigns.

### 3.3. Heaven Reverence (*Tế Trời*) Context
- Emphasize its cultural, ethical, and worldbuilding integrity without claiming all historical folk rituals were identical to original monotheism.
