## 🔍 II. DETAILED EVALUATION OF 4 CORE ROUTES

---

### 1. ⚔️ Main Character Axis: An Thai $\leftrightarrow$ Moc Tinh Lan
* **Excellent strengths:**
  * **Don't fall into the toxic tragic motif:** The love of two people is built on understanding, respect for each other's ideals and unwavering loyalty.
  * **Peak artistic symbol:** Souvenir *"Piece of Chu Dau pottery divided in half"* directly connects the two spaces of the field and the pottery kiln, providing a profound metaphor for fidelity and triumphant faith.
  * **Pure morality:** Before the day of battle, both of them stood in front of the **Open Heaven Altar** to thank God, offering bowls of clear spring water and golden rice grains - completely eliminating superstition.

---

### 2. 🌳 Family Axis & In-law Relationship (Nguyen $\leftrightarrow$ Moc Family)
* **The natural connection between the two families:**
  * **An Thai's mother (Ms. Le Thi Dieu):** The woman weaved rough cloth and indigo shirts, sacrificing her whole life to raise her children.
  * **Tinh Lan's mother (Ms. Tran Thi Y Lan):** The great physician of the pottery village often gives nutritious medicine to Mrs. Dieu.
  * **Meaning:** The love between the two families is an exemplary symbol of **Pfilial Respect to Parents and Neighborly Love**, reflecting the true identity of Dai Vietnamese traditional family culture.

---

### 3. 🌸 Next Generation Axis: Nguyen An Ninh (17 years old) $\leftrightarrow$ Moc Tinh Uyen (16 years old)
* **Bright highlights, emotional balance:**
  * **Nguyen An Ninh (An Thai's younger brother):** Good marksman with a crossbow, courageous, straightforward, representing the enthusiasm of youth ready to take on the country's affairs.
  * **Moc Tinh Uyen (Tinh Lan's younger sister):** The muse specializes in drawing lotus flowers and writing Nom poems on the bottom of ceramic vases, gentle and pure.
  * **Youthful relationship:** Uyen often wrote Nom poems on Ninh's crossbow. Ninh gave Uyen colored stones picked up by the riverbank for her to grind to use as paint. This is a clear secondary emotional line, bringing hope for rebirth after the war.

---

### 4. 🌿 Master's Axis & Two Lamps of Spiritual Illumination
* **Pham Thiet Giap (Master of Dao Phap):** Represents **Vo Dao Viet Quoc** (Resilient, straightforward, forging swords for peace, not for killing).
* **Zen Master Huyen Giac (Medical Master):** Represents **Medical Compassion & Philosophy of Living According to Nature** (580 herbal medicines, pure mind, saving people without discrimination).
* **Combination:** Creates for An Thai and Tinh Lan a solid foundation in both **The Spirit of Swordsmanship** and **The Compassionate Wisdom of Truc Lam**.
