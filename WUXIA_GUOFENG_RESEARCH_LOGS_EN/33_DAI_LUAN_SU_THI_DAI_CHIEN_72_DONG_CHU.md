# EPIC DISCUSSION: FOUR BATTLES REFERENCE

> **Public Document · Updated 2026-09-25**
> Battle names, fictitious content and statistics need to be compared with Eastern A canon.

## 1. Me Linh, 40 AD

- **[TRUE HISTORY]** The uprising against Ma Yuan started from Me Linh; The spread and number of strongholds according to sources should be cited.
- **[CONTROLLED FICTION]** Descriptions of elephants, armor, drums, formations, terrain, food, and female soldiers.
- Do not use Dong Son drums as infrasound transmitters to control elephants; If present, the drum is a signal of celebration, devotion or legendary memories.

## 2. Co Loa and crossbow techniques

- **[CONDITIONAL INTERPRETATION]** Co Loa, crossbows and bronze arrows are archaeological materials that require the name of the site, number of artifacts, date and excavation report.
- Do not automatically attach "thousands", "500 meters", "armor piercing" or trigger mechanisms if there is no power.
- In fiction, crossbowmen, names and formations may be described, but must be labeled.

## 3. Bach Dang, 1288

- **[REAL HISTORY]** The Bach Dang naval battle was associated with wooden stakes, boats, riverside and tidal strategies.
- Describes tides, currents, fog, stakes and grenadier positions; Avoid turning piles into "absolute technology".
- Do not use the details of the year 1258 for Bach Dang 1288.

## 4. Chi Lang - Xuong Giang and life after the war

- **[REAL HISTORY]** Main milestones need to state date, place name and source; Operational details, supplies and fictitious situations must be labeled.
- The story needs to show occupations, materials, negotiations, caring for wounded soldiers and rebuilding communities, not just war.

## 5. Epic rules

- Fictitious names, costumes, weapons and nicknames must not be masqueraded as historical records.
- Data must have a source or “interpretation” label; Absolutely do not make up army numbers to increase drama.
- Historical characters use the correct name, correct role and correct era; fictional characters have their own names.
