## 🕊️ PART II: THE FLOW OF MEMORIES OF THE GREAT FLOOD INTO THE MIND OF THE ANCIENT VIETNAMESE PEOPLE

After Noah's descendants scattered across the earth, the memory of the Great Flood and the ritual of **Sacrificing to Heaven in the open** were passed down through thousands of generations of the Bach Viet ethnic groups:

### 1. Legend of "Au Co's Mother's Gourd & The Flood Rescue Gourd":
* In the mythological treasures of the Vietnamese and other ethnic groups (Muong, Tay, Thai, Kho Mu): A great flood flooded the mountains and hills, a couple of men and women crawled into a **large gourd** drifting on the water and survived. $\rightarrow$ Reborn humans.
* This is **Memories of the Hundred Years' Flood** which is an absolute parallel to the story of Noah's Ark!

### 2. Why did the ancient Vietnamese call it "GOD" and set up "ALTAR TO HEAVEN"?
* **Respect for the Only Creator:** Inheriting the tradition of Noah, the ancient Vietnamese knew that among all creatures there is a single Supreme Being who holds life and protects humans through storms $\rightarrow$ Call Him with a sacred and simple call: **"GOD"**.
* **Thien Lo Open Altar (Outdoor Stone Incense Tree):**
  * Imitating the stone altar of ancestor Noah: The Vietnamese Altar of Thien must be placed outdoors, under a clear sky, with wind on all four sides so that prayers can fly straight to the Creator's throne.
* **Historical Fact: DO NOT LIGHT INCENSE:**
  * Noah offered a sincere heart and a pure offering. The early Vietnamese people also **absolutely did not use incense sticks, incense sticks or charms**.
  * **Pure offering:** 1 Bowl of Clear Spring Water (symbolizing purity after the flood washed the world), 1 Bowl of Golden Rice (first crop of new life).
