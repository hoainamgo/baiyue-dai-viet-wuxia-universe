## 🌾 PART I: THE FOUR GREAT ARCHAEOLOGICAL PHASES OF THE RED RIVER CIVILIZATION (HUNG KING'S PERIOD)

In order for the worldview of martial arts to have a solid realistic foundation, Vietnamese archeology has established four stages of technological evolution from the Neolithic to the brilliant Bronze and Iron Ages:


1. **Phung Nguyen Culture (Phu Tho - 4,000 years ago):**
   * Unearthed tens of thousands of jade bracelets, jade earrings, fluted stone earrings, delicate beads and S-patterned ceramics.
   * Proving that the ancient Vietnamese people of the 1st Hung King period had excellent taste in jewelry and art.
2. **Dong Dau & Go Mun Culture (Vinh Phuc - 3,500 - 3,000 years ago):**
   * Excavation of bronze casting molds, bronze arrows, bronze fish hooks, bronze axes with scales.
3. **Dong Son Culture (Thanh Hoa, Hanoi, Hai Phong - 2,500 - 2,000 years ago):**
   * **Dong Son Drum National Treasure (Ngoc Lu, Hoang Ha, Co Loa, Song Da):** Depicts 18 Lac birds flying counterclockwise, a warship carrying warriors holding spears and axes, a double pestle and rice mortar, and Giao Long dragon and crocodile image.
   * **Dao Thinh Bronze Jar:** The lid of the jar is cast with 4 pairs of statues of men and women having sex $\rightarrow$ Symbol of the Vietnamese people's belief in fertility and fertility.

### 1. 🍃 THE LEGEND OF THE BELLY AND ARECA (FRIENDLY FAMILY & FAMILY FAMILY):
* **Era:** The reign of the 3rd Hung King (Hung Quoc Vuong).
* **Epic story:**
  * Twin brothers Cao Tan and Cao Lang both love a girl named Luu. Because of a misunderstanding, the younger brother left and exhaustedly turned into a limestone rock on the bank of the stream. The brother looking for me collapsed and died, turning into a straight areca tree. The wife looks for her husband, hugs the areca tree trunk, turns it into a betel trellis, and climbs around.
* **Martial Arts & Ancient Style Symbols:**
  * A betel piece soaked in phoenix wings, a spicy red lime $\rightarrow$ The iron-clad oath of fraternization between the knights and the thousand-year-old pure Vietnamese wedding ceremony.

---

### 2. 🌾 BUNCHONG BAKES (PRINCESS LANG LIEU & GOD OF HEAVEN AND EARTH):
* **Era:** The reign of the 6th Hung King (Hung Huy Vuong).
* **Epic story:**
  * Prince Lang Lieu was rustic and poor. He was told by the gods to take sticky rice, green beans, pork, and wrap it into Banh Chung (square symbolizing Earth - Mother) and pound white sticky rice to shape it into Banh Giay (circle symbolizing Heaven - Father).
* **The meaning of martial arts:** The philosophy of honoring the pearl of heaven (wet rice agriculture) and filial piety, defeating all the lavish delicacies of other princes to succeed the throne.

---

### 3. ✨ CURE THE PUPIL & TIEN DUNG (FREE LOVE & SEDIMENTARY PAIR):
* **Era:** 3rd Hung King's reign.
* **Legends & Space:**
  * A poor boy without a loincloth to cover his body, Chu Dong Tu met Princess Tien Dung bathing on the sandy beach of Man Tru (Red River). Overcoming feudal rituals, Tien Dung accepted him as her husband.
  * The two people studied the Immortal Way of Zen master Phat Quang, were given the **Magic Stick and Conical Hat** $\rightarrow$, built the magnificent Da Trach citadel in one night and then flew to heaven together.
* **Application of martial arts:** Origin of **"Da Trach Than Cong"** and the art of using conical hats and bamboo sticks as meditation weapons to protect the body.

---

### 4. ⚔️ THUC PHAN AN DUONG KING'S FIGHT AGAINST 500,000 QIN ARMY (YEAR 214 BC):
* **Research historical facts:**
  * Emperor Qin Shi Huang ordered general Do Thu to bring 500,000 troops to invade Bach Viet.
  * Leader Thuc Phan and the Lac generals gathered the people of Tay Vu and Lac Viet to retreat into the deep forest. At night, they went out to fight guerrillas against the Qin army.
  * After the victory, Thuc Phan united Au Lac, proclaimed An Duong Vuong, and moved the capital to Co Loa.

### 5. 🏹 Cao Lo's Crossbow and the Tragedy of Co Loa's Tears:
* **Archaeology:** Tens of thousands of three-pointed bronze arrows and crossbow bolts from Au Lac period have been unearthed.
* **Legend:** Magic crossbow shoots thousands of arrows to protect the country. My Chau credulously gave the crossbow bolt to Trong Thuy, leading to loss of country. A drop of My Chau's blood fell into the sea and turned into pearls, the water of Co Loa well washed bright pearls in $\rightarrow$ An elegy about vigilance to protect the border.

### 6. 🐅 MAI AN TIEM & METAPHOR OF THE SEA OF NGA SON ISLAND:
* **Life philosophy:** *"What is given is a worry, what is given is a debt"*. Crossing the deserted island, taming the red watermelon (*Tay Qua*) and sending it back to the mainland with the waves $\rightarrow$ Self-control, never giving up in the face of difficult challenges.
