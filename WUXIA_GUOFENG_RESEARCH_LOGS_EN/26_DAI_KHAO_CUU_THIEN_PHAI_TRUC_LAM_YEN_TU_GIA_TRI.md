## 🏛️ I. ORIGIN OF THE NAME: WHY IS IT CALLED "TRUC LAM YEN TU"?

The name **"Truc Lam Yen Tu" (竹林安子)** is the crystallization of 4 layers of symbols of history, geography and indigenous consciousness of Dai Viet:

```
                              [ TRÚC LÂM YÊN TỬ ]
                                       │
            ┌──────────────────────────┴──────────────────────────┐
            ▼                                                     ▼
     [ TRÚC LÂM (竹林) ]                                    [ YÊN TỬ (安子) ]
• Cội nguồn thực tế: Rừng trúc mai Yên Tử             • Địa danh: Ngọn núi thiêng sương khói
• Biểu tượng quân tử: Ngay thẳng, dẻo dai, rỗng ruột  • Ý nghĩa: Đất an lành, nơi tâm trí bình yên
• Điển tích lịch sử: Hồi sinh Tịnh xá Trúc Lâm cổ     • Đỉnh Phù Vân: Nơi mây trắng ngưng tụ
```

### 1. Meaning of the word "Bamboo Forest" (竹林 - Bamboo Forest):
* **Field survey & Eco-archaeology:** Yen Tu mountain range (Uong Bi, Quang Ninh) has been abundant since ancient times with **bamboo forest and yellow apricot trees**. The Yen Tu bamboo tree has a small, flexible stem, grows crowded on steep cliffs, and receives the East Sea breeze all year round.
* **Vietnamese Personality Symbol:**
  * **Burning upright bamboo (Upright):** Even though the bamboo tree is withered, it still maintains a straight trunk, symbolizing the bravery and courage, refusing to yield to the tyranny of the North.
  * **Hollow bamboo body (Empty mind):** Symbol of letting go of greed, attachment to fame and profit (*Void mind, lost path*).
  * **Bending in the storm (Judo):** The storm crushed the crouching bamboo tree and then stood up straight, reflecting Dai Viet's military philosophy: *"Using softness to overcome hardness, short soldiers control the long battle"*.
* **Inheriting & Transforming ancient legends:** During the Buddha's time, there was Truc Lam Monastery (*Veluvana*) in India donated by King Tan-ba-sa-la. Tran Nhan Tong borrowed this name again but **localized it 100% using the actual bamboo forest on the sacred mountain of Dai Viet**.

### 2. Meaning of the word "YEN TU" (安子 - Yen Binh Mountain):
* **Meaning of the word "Yen" (安):** Peaceful, peaceful, no more war and chaos. After 3 great victories against the Mongol invaders, climbing Yen Tu peak carries the meaning: **Ending the war, creating eternal peace and prosperity for all people**.
* **Phu Van Peak (Bach Van Son):** The mountain is over 1,000m high and covered with clouds all year round, symbolizing an absolutely pure space, separate from the dust and dirt.

## 🎯 II. PURPOSE OF ESTABLISHING THE TRUC LAM MEDITATION sect (WHY DID THE KING TURN TO THE MEDITATION?)

The fact that **Buddhist Emperor Tran Nhan Tong** ceded the throne to his son (King Tran Anh Tong) to go to Yen Tu mountain to practice and establish a sect was not an act of pessimism or escape from life, but carried **3 great National Strategic Purposes**:

```
                       [ 3 MỤC ĐÍCH CHIẾN LƯỢC CỦA TRẦN NHÂN TÔNG ]
                                            │
   ┌────────────────────────────────────────┼────────────────────────────────────────┐
   ▼                                        ▼                                        ▼
[ 1. ĐOÀN KẾT ĐỘC LẬP TƯ TƯỞNG ]     [ 2. QUAN TRẮC BỜ CÕI PHƯƠNG BẮC ]       [ 3. XÂY DỰNG LỐI SỐNG AN LẠC ]
• Hợp nhất 3 dòng thiền cũ           • Yên Tử là tiền đồn chiến lược           • Dẹp bỏ mê tín, dâm từ
• Thoát ly hoàn toàn Bắc tông        • Đứng trên đỉnh cao canh giữ non sông    • Thực hành Thập thiện & Tùy duyên
```

### 1. Unifying ideas, creating national cultural independence:
* Before the Tran dynasty, Dai Viet had three separate Zen lineages: *Bhi-ni-da-luu-chi* (India), *Vo Ngon Thong* (China) and *Thao Duong*.
* Tran Nhan Tong **merged all three Zen lineages into a single Zen lineage with Pure Vietnamese identity: Truc Lam Zen**. This is a step to unify the ideology of the entire nation, affirming absolute independence from the North in both politics and culture.

### 2. Forward lookout guarding the border (Military geopolitics):
* Yen Tu Mountain is located in the Northeast, overlooking Dong Trieu Bay, Bach Dang Giang and the critical gateway to the North.
* A Supreme Emperor / Famous general who defeated the Mongol empire sat on top of Yen Tu as the **supreme strategic monitoring station**, reassuring the people and deterring foreigners from daring to invade.

### 3. Educate the people, build a prosperous moral society:
* He walked throughout the villages, personally **eliminating superstitious temples and shrines, eliminating fortune-telling, offering stars, and burning votive papers**, instructing people to keep the 5 precepts and practice 10 good deeds (*Ten good deeds*).
