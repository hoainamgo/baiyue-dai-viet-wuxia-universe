1. **CEREMONY (Ceremony & Ceremony):**
   * The first is the **Great Sacrifice Ceremony to Heaven**: The ritual of offering 1 Bowl of Clear Spring Water & 1 Bowl of Golden Rice Grains to the roofless Altar of Heaven.
   * Ceremony: Bowing to parents morning and night, worshiping ancestors, teaching and teaching.
2. **MUSIC (Sound and melody):**
   * Proficient in playing the sixteen-string silk instrument, playing the ancient string instrument, playing the bamboo flute and beating the Dong Son drum.
   * Music is not just for enjoyment but is a **spiritual weapon** to purify negative thoughts, build courage and support the spirit; Does not confirm cure.
3. **Shooting (Crossbow Marksmanship):**
   * Ability to shoot the three-pronged **High Crossbow God Crossbow** to hit the bull's eye 100 steps away in the dark; archery on galloping horseback.
4. **HORSE (Control Beasts & Ships):**
   * **Ngu Tuong:** Riding a war elephant with two tusks wrapped in a bronze spear across the Me Linh swamp.
   * **Capital Ship:** Steer the **100-rower Mong Dong warship** over Luc Dau Giang rapids and surf the waves of the East Sea.
5. **LETTER (Inscriptions & Inscriptions):**
   * Master 3 types of writing: **5,300-year-old Luong Chu script** (engraved on jade), **Dong Son Khoa Dau script** (engraved with axes on bronze jars), and **Dai Viet Nom script** written on the bottom of a ceramic vase.
6. **NUMBERS & MEDICINE (Numerology, Astronomy & Herbology):**
   * Calculate the agricultural calendar of high tides in Bach Dang; understand the Lac Viet I Ching; Identify and interpret 580 traditional herbal medicines.
