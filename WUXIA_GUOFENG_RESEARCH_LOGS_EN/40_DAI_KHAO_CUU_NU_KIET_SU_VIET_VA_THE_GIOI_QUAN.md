## 🐘 CLUSTER I: FEMALE GENERAL OF SA TRUONG & AROUND LIET UPrising

### 1. TWO LADIES TRUNG (TRUNG TRAC & TRUNG NHI) — BEGINNING THE PRINCIPAL OF BACH VIET FEMINISM
* **Personality & History:** In 40 AD, Hai Ba Trung raised the flag of insurrection at Hat Mon, expelled the Prefect To Dinh, conquered 65 strongholds of Linh Nam, and proclaimed themselves kings to build the country.
* **Special Weapons & Martial Arts:**
  * **Displaying the King's Twin Swords:** Pair of Dong Son bronze swords with hilts cast in the image of Mother Bach Viet, the swordsmanship is spacious and magical.
  * **Statue of the God of Battle:** Riding a white elephant with two tusks covered in bronze and armored, using the Dong Son Drum to give orders to the troops.
* **Imprints in the Wanderer's Worldview:**
  * The origin of **"Mesmerizing Immortal Soldiers"** and the tradition of *Female Admirals* leading the battlefield.

### 2. FEMALE GENERAL LE CHAN & FEMALE GENERAL THANH THIEN — FORMER MARINE WARRIORS & KNIGHTS
* **Le Chan (Female general of Hai Phong coastal area):** Exploited the land of An Bien, mastered naval warfare, established coastal defense lines to block the enemy.
  * *Weapons:* **Ba Vuong Slashing Ma Dao** & Short sword for naval combat.
* **Thanh Thien (Bac Giang):** Outstanding female cavalry general, her cavalry shot hundreds of times, causing enemy general Ma Vien to complain: *"I have never seen such a brave female general in my life"*.
  * *Weapon:* **Grindwood Bow Inlaid with Mother of Pearl** & Lotus bud spear.

### 3. MRS. TRIEU (TRIEU THI TRINH) — THE METAPHOR OF Climbing Mountains and Riding Waves
* **Immortal declaration:** *"I want to ride the strong wind, step on the fierce waves, kill the whales in the East Sea, take back the country, take off the yoke of slavery, and not bend down to be your concubine!"* (Year 248).
* **Visual image:** Wearing golden armor, ivory clogs, golden brooch, riding a white elephant with one tusk into battle amid the storm.
* **Integrated World View:**
  * Representing **The Pride of Riding the Waves to Slay the Whales**, the immortal inspiration for female knights when facing tyranny.

### 4. ADmiral BUI THI XUAN — FIRST GOD OF THE LONG AND FEMALE GENERAL OF TAY SON
* **Excellent martial arts skills:** **Double Swords of Tay Son** is as graceful as a flying dragon and phoenix dancing, a master of training a team of soldiers to fight for hundreds of victories.
* **Mettle:** Standing tall in front of a herd of fierce elephants without changing color, the ultimate symbol of indomitableness.

## 👑 CLUSTER II: WOMEN GOVERNOR, MOTHER OF THE NATION & PEACE DIPLOMACY

### 1. QUEEN MOTHER DUONG VAN NGA — FOR YOUR COUNTRY FORGET YOURSELF, GIVE UP THE LONG ROBE
* **Great action:** Overcoming the family boundary, giving the Long Con shirt to Thap Dao General Le Hoan to unify the army and people, defeating the invading Song army in 981.
* **Meaning:** Wisdom puts country and society above the family throne.

### 2. NGUYEN CONFEDERATE / THE LIM OF QUEEN HAU Y LAN — MOTHER OF THE GREAT COUNTRY OF VIETNAM (LY TRIEU)
* **Talent in ruling the country:** Twice regent when King Ly Thanh Tong and Ly Nhan Tong were absent, the country was peaceful and prosperous, the people revered it as *"Quan Am Nu"* (*Ly Trieu Avalokiteśvara*).
* **Humanitarian policy:** Redeeming poor daughters who were sold as concubines and married to widowers; prohibit stealing and indiscriminate killing of buffaloes and cows to preserve plowing power for farmers.
* **Integrated World View:**
  * Representing **Politics to Rule the Nation & Compassion to Save Humanity**.

### 3. PRINCESS HUYEN TRAN — TEARS OF PEACE, OPEN THE SOUTH
* **Legend:** In 1306, according to the words of her father King Tran Nhan Tong, she put aside her personal love to become the bride of Champa, bringing to Dai Viet two continents O and Ly (Quang Tri, Thua Thien Hue).
* **Artistic image:** The poised Phong Dai princess, strumming a stringed instrument on the shore of the sea, sacrificing personal happiness for the peace of all people and the integrity of the country.
* **Integrated:** Inspiration for nostalgic and profound Co Phong songs (*"The mountain water is thousands of miles away"*).

### 4. NATIONAL MOTHER TRAN THI DUNG — ALL-POWERFUL QUEEN OF THE oriental period
* **Historical merits:** During the resistance war against the Mongols in 1258, she personally took care of evacuating the royal court and common people, manufacturing weapons, gathering food and provisions, and maintaining absolute safety in the rear.
* **Integration:** Underground military supply and security network in the towns and cities of Dai Viet Gypsies.

## 🌿 CLUSTER III: FEMALE MEDICINES, MASTERS & FOLK ARTISTS

### 1. FEMALE LEAD MOC TINH LAN — TRANSMISSION OF THE MEDICAL THANH NONG & TUE TINH
* **Personality:** Exiled ceramic artist Chu Dau, anonymous disciple of Ngoa Van Yen Tu temple.
* **Medical Education:** Fully inheriting **580 herbal medicines** of Zen Master Tue Tinh (*Southern medicine to treat men*), using plants and flowers around the garden to save the poor.
* ** Ancient Music Acoustics: ** Hands sharpening ceramics combined with plucking a **16-string string instrument** and **Ancient string instrument Ancient String Instrument Co Nhac**, using rustic sounds to soothe pain and block toxic gas for knights in the battlefield.

### 2. ANCIENT SILK WEAVER ARTIST & FEMALE POTERER Chu Dau
* **Silk Weaving Grandmother:** Using Day River's golden silkworm cocoons to weave raw silk sheets of celadon green, chicken fat yellow, and rose brown - the authentic costumes of women in the Ly - Tran dynasties.
* **Female artist Chu Dau:** Skillful hands draw motifs of lotus flowers, water waves, and Lac birds on brown flower ceramics - a unique culture that shines throughout Asia and Europe.

### 3. THANG LONG DAO NGUYEN & THE SOUNDS OF THE FUNDAMENTAL FRUIT
* **Image:** The woman holds a long barrel 3-stringed Day lute with high keys, sings in a deep, warm voice, recounting the epic stories of building and defending the country among the taverns of the capital.
