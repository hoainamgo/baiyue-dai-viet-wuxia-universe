## 🏛️ PART I: ARCHAEOLOGICAL REPORT & TRUSTED SCIENTIFIC BASIS

### 1. Stratigraphic Traces of the Great Flood (4,200 – 4,300 years ago):
* **Geological Event 4.2ka BP**: At deep geological drills in the Northern Delta (Red River, Day River) and Taihu basin (Southern Truong Giang), scientists have confirmed a 1 - 2.5m thick layer of peat mud and mangrove marine clay deposits dating to ~2250 BC.
* **Water level on the cliff**: Layers of fossilized sea shells embedded deep on limestone cliffs at a height of tens of meters in Ninh Binh, Quang Binh, Ha Giang prove that the sea level once rose and flooded the entire low-lying area.

### 2. Luong Chu Civilization Site (5,300 - 4,300 years ago):
* The Liang Chu people (Tien Bach Viet) owned the earliest giant earthen irrigation dam system in the world.
* The capital Liangzhu was suddenly submerged under a layer of fine mud and sand in a great flood around 2250 BC, forcing residents to migrate to the South and high mountains.
* The artifact **Jade Tong Luong Chu** carries the philosophy: Square on the outside (Mother Earth) — Round and hollow on the inside (Father Heaven & Lifesaving Chamber).

### 3. Indigenous Archaeological Music & Literature:
* **Ndut Lieng Krak Stone Instrument (3,000 - 4,000 years old)**: Evidence that residents on the high mountains of the Central Highlands used natural stones to play five notes to thank Heaven and Earth after the flood event.
* **Dou and Dong Son Drum script**: Original Tadpole characters (Noi ◯ - Tadpole 𖡡) on Sa Pa ancient stone and Dong Son Ngoc Lu Drum pattern (Sun in the center = Heavenly Father; boat rim and gourd = Mother Earth). Absolutely **no images of Northern Feudal Dragons**, only images of **Thuong Luong (Crocodile/Ancient Dragon)**, **Lac Bird**, **Sacred Turtle** and **Frog calling for rain**.

### 📜 FIRST GOD: THE WRATH OF THE UNIVERSE & THE SALVATION OF GOD
*(Father Heaven gave the design of the Ark to the Good Father Noah)*

In the past, humanity betrayed good things and abandoned the sacred land,
The sky was torn apart, black rain poured down for forty days and nights.
The water in the deep abyss rushed up, submerging thousands of palaces,
Mother Earth buckled and sank deep under countless fierce waves.

Looking at the children standing on the brink of destruction,
**The heart of Heavenly Father (Hoang Thien) has immense mercy!**
He guided His eyes to light the way for the Good Father (Noah) to maintain righteousness all his life,
Ordered to cut thousands of years of cypress wood on the top of the high mountain.

The person carved each grain of wood and bent it into a **GIANT BOX BOAT BOAT**,
Covered with tree resin and wild beeswax, the three-storey chamber was tightly closed.
That boat is the **Great Fetus of Heaven and Earth**,
Carrying a pair of men and women, carrying many varieties of upland rice, corn, and pairs of gentle animals,
Floating on the back of fierce waves, overcoming the dark night!

### 📜 SECOND GOD: MOTHER EARTH WELCOME THE BOAT & PURE SMOKE OF INCENTIVES TO HEAVEN
*(Original ritual of establishing an Open Stone Altar to thank the Creator)*

Spending hundreds of days and nights tumbling amid the storms and rolling waves,
Mother Earth slowly raised the sacred mountain (Tan Vien / Ararat) from the bottom of the abyss to receive Thuyen Bau.
The water gradually receded, Mother Earth spread a layer of warm red alluvial mud to welcome the cubs back.

The compartment door opens!
Heaven's Father's light shines down on Boat Bau's heart,
Hundreds of life sprouts rushed out to new land, breathing a thousand primordial breezes.

The Father steps down, does not set up false golden statues, does not worship evil spirits in the abyss,
He and his children gathered rough, bare stone slabs and arranged them into an **EXCLUSIVE SACRIFICE ALTAR**.
Pick up dried branches of agarwood, peel old cinnamon bark and forest mugwort and offer fragrant smoke straight to Father Heaven.
The pure herbal smoke blends between Earth and Heaven,
Thank you Heavenly Father for giving me the life-saving ship, thank you Mother Earth for protecting and nurturing me!

Heavenly Father draped the **SEVEN COLOR RAINBOW** across the sacred mountain as a covenant of eternal peace:
*“The incense smoke is still flying, Mother Earth is still sprouting,
Then the species coming out of the Boat Bau will never perish forever!”*

### 📜 THIRD GOD: ONE HUNDRED TRIBES TO FIFTY-FOUR BRITISH ETHNICS
*(Journey of historical change, separation due to the Northern War)*

From the Boat Bau Fetus, hundreds of life sprouts sprout,
Transformed into **One Hundred Hundred Vietnamese Tribes** radiant throughout the southern mountains and rivers.
They carved the word Khoa Dau on the cliff and cast the Dong Son Drum to simulate the Father's Sun and the Mother's Gourd.

Then the evil enemy from the North descended for thousands of years, causing trouble.
One hundred tribes were scattered and thousands of tribes were lost.
To keep the honest blood and sacred seed:
- **Fifty children following Heavenly Father's call**: Climbed to the high peaks of Hoang Lien Son, Tay Con Linh, Truong Son $\rightarrow$ Transformed into brothers H'Mong, Dao, Tay, Nung, Thai, Ba-na, Ede, Gia-rai, Xo-dang, Co-tu...
- **Fifty children following Mother Earth's milk**: Down to the Red River Delta, Ma River, East Sea coast, taming the fierce waves and crocodiles $\rightarrow$ Transforming into brothers Kinh, Muong, Cham, Raglai, S'tieng...

The word Khoa Dau was lost due to animal skins rotting in the rain of the tropical forest.
People on high mountains used word of mouth to call the Three-Deck Wooden Ship **"Mother Gourd"**;
The person who casts metal is called **"Dong Trong Dong Son"**;
The person who molded the pottery called it **"The Ceramic Jar of Salvation"**.

One hundred primitive tribes,
Now converging to become **FIFTY-FOUR BRITISH NATIONALS IN THE MOTHER LAND OF VIETNAM**!
