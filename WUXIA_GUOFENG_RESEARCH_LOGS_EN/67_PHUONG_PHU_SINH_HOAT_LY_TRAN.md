## 🍲 PART I: FOOD, DELICIOUS DISHES & TASTES OF THE LY — TRAN

### 1. Son Hao Seafood & Delicious Dishes Royal Court / Nobility:
- Anh Vu fish from the Red River, Tien Vua: A precious fish that lives at the confluence of the Bach Hac River (Viet Tri), only eats rock moss in fast waters; Cartilaginous fish snout is crispy and sweet, steamed with ginger and lolot leaves or braised in an ash-glazed earthenware pot to pay tribute to the royal court.
- West Lake Carp & Dam Sen Shrimp Cake: Carp caught from Tay Ho lotus pond, fragrant lotus meat, marinated with yellow turmeric root, old galangal grilled on charcoal wrapped in banana leaves.
- Ly - Tran Royal Banquet: Silk rolls wrapped in banana leaves and crushed by hand in a stone mortar, spring rolls with phoenix decorated with chrysanthemums peeled from white radish, soup with pork rinds soaked in ginger wine with wood ear mushrooms and wild shiitake mushrooms.
- Sticky rice with Cai Hoa Vang from Jackfruit wood: fragrant sticky rice with round shiny seeds, sticky rice with golden yellow gardenia seeds dyed or vermilion red gac sticky rice offered as offerings to the heavens.

### 2. Rustic Folk Meal & Gypsy Guests:
- Pot of Braised Goby with Soybean Soybean: Boiled river goby braised with rotten bones in rich sweet and sour Soybean sauce, lining the bottom of the pot with green guava leaves and crushed ginger root.
- Field Crab Soup with Malabar Spinach & Compressed Salted Eggplant: Hand-pounded field crab, filtered with water, cooked with crab bricks, served with a bowl of crispy eggplant and eggplant with compressed salt in an eel skin clay pot.
- Banh Tro Banh Cot (Banh Gio): Sticky rice soaked in mistletoe charcoal ash and sticky rice straw, wrapped in boiled dong leaves, dipped in sticky red molasses.
- Luong Kho Sa Truong Dong A: Lam rice grilled on male bamboo tubes, buffalo meat dried in soapberry smoke, sesame salt roasted with lemon leaves stuffed in a cloth waist bag.

## 🍊 PART II: EARTH FLOWERS & SOUTHERN FRUITS (TRIP TO GOD)

### 1. Imperial & Tribute Fruit Varieties:
- Thieu Tien Vua Lychee (Le Chi Phuong Nam): Smooth thorny red skin, thick, succulent pulp as transparent as jade, sweet and fragrant (once a tribute during the time of the Mai Hac Emperor, which surprised Truong An).
- Hung Yen longan (Longan Tien Vua): Drained thick pulp, veins covered with black seeds like dragon eyes, dried into dried longan decocted with sedative medicine.
- Essay Red Grapefruit & Doan Hung Grapefruit: Fragrant red grapefruit peel from outside to inside, peach-colored succulent shrimp offered to ancestors.
- Ngu Dai Hoang Banana: A bunch of bananas as small as a finger, with a thin, golden skin like silk, with a sweet and noble aroma.

### 2. Wild Mountain & Countryside Fruits:
- Wild Black Canarium & White Canarium: Braise canarium in warm water with pork belly or soak in salt and wild chili to eat to eliminate cold in winter nights.
- Seedless Persimmons Soaked in Stream Water: Sweet, crispy persimmons soaked in the Yen Tu rocky stream for 3 days and 3 nights to remove the astringency.
- Guava, Soursop, Honey Pineapple: Growing around the garden, the fragrant fruit attracts birds to chirp around the porch.

## 👘 PART III: CLOTHES, DRESS & JEWELRY

### 1. Nobility, Mandarins & Royal Beauties:
- Co Phuc Giao Linh & Vien Linh: Giao Linh wide-sleeved cross-tie shirt made of Van Phuc silk, woven silk with lotus motifs, rolling water waves and clouds.
- Four Points of Permeability: Ivory white silk pants inside, outside covered with many layers of thin silk shirts of similar colors (lotus pink, jade green, chicken fat yellow).
- Jewelry & Hair: Women wear their hair in a high bun with a gold brooch carved with chrysanthemums, silver lotus-shaped earrings, and a jade belt with a silk ribbon hanging down to the heels; The man had his hair tied in a bun and wrapped in a black scarf.

### 2. Dong A Folk Costumes, Potters & Soldiers:
- Four-body Ao Dai & Short-winged Indigo Ao Dai: Made from rough linen fabric, hemp fabric dyed with brown root (durable brown color) or soaked with indigo root (river-black blue color).
- Walking Barefoot & Cloth Loincloths: The habit of walking barefoot helps feet grip firmly in the mud of fields and rafts; His feet have calluses that are thick with weather and weather.
- Giao Long Tattoo Custom: All over the thigh, chest, and arm are tattooed with the sea monster Giao Long so that when diving into the water, the sea monster will think that its fellow creatures will not harm it (national custom from the Hung King period to the end of the Tran dynasty).
- Like the word Sat That: A warrior of the Tran dynasty engraved the two words Sat That in Chinese ink mixed with python honey on his arm, vowing not to fight against the Mongol invaders.

## 🛶 PART IV: TRANSPORTATION & RIVER TRAVEL

### 1. Waterway (Dai Viet Traffic Artery No. 1):
- Royal Bronze Mongoose: A long warship that glides quickly, with dozens of synchronized oars on both sides, its copper-coated bow touches the head of a dragon and phoenix, carrying kings and mandarins to patrol the rivers and animals.
- Bamboo Boats, Basket Boats & Bamboo Rafts: Bamboo boats woven with buffalo dung and sawdust to prevent salt water, wriggling through the mangrove creeks of Bach Dang, Day River and Duong River.
- Fragment of Bamboo Cay Giang Rung: Grafted from hundreds of bamboo trees carrying ironwood and cinnamon down the Ma and Da rivers to the capital of Thang Long.

### 2. Road & Cargo:
- Son Son Thiep Vang palanquin: Curved palanquin carried by 4 people or 8 people for the royal family, with silk curtains to cover the wind and wind.
- Bach Horse & Bach Viet Hill Horse: Grass horses are small in stature but extremely resilient in climbing Yen Tu rocky mountains and Hai Van pass.
- Walking Barefoot & Carrying Bamboo Poles: A pair of woven rattan poles and curved bamboo poles rhythmically follow the footsteps of the mother and wife going to the village market.

## 🍵 PART V: GREAT VIETNAMESE TEA CULTURE & BEVERAGES

### 1. Tay Ho Lotus Tea & Royal Tea (Natural Fragrance Art):
- Vietnamese Tea Style: Different from Japanese foamed Matcha tea powder or Northern cake-pressed tea, Dai Vietnamese people drink **Loose Leaf Bud Tea Marinated with Natural Flowers**.
- Early Lotus Tea: Row a bamboo boat to Tay Ho lotus lake at dusk, put young tea buds in the heart of a newly opened lotus flower, tie a sticky rice straw and keep it covered overnight; Early the next morning, catch every drop of morning dew on the lotus leaf and boil a terracotta pot to make tea.
- Mountain Chrysanthemum Tea & Moc Te Tea: Translucent green tea buds on a cast iron pan marinated with dew-dried yellow chrysanthemums, with a deep sweet and astringent taste.

### 2. Therapeutic Folk Drinks:
- Warm Boiled Green Tea Bowl: Crumble fresh green tea leaves into a terracotta pot, boil in boiling water and cover with sticky rice straw to keep warm all day.
- Robusta Water & Grilled Robusta Buds: Robusta buds are brewed in ceramic jars and dried in the sun, making the water a bitter, sweet and refreshing drink, digesting food, and cooling off during the summer of rice harvest.
- Cai Hoa Vang Glutinous Wine Aged with Earth: Leaf yeast wine brewed for 100 days underground in ash glazed ceramic jars, fragrant with the smell of new sticky rice.

## 🧺 PART VI: BATHING, WASHING & NATURAL BODY CLEANING

### 1. Herbal Bath & Hair Care (Moc Tinh Lan Medicine):
- Grilled Botanicals & Grapefruit Peel: Fragrant grilled Botanicals over broken charcoal and boiled with grapefruit peel, lemongrass and basil leaves. The shampoo leaves hair silky black and fragrant with a forest scent that lasts for three days.
- Old Coriander Leaves & Five Spice Bath Water: Cook the old coriander tree with its fragrant flowers and seeds on the afternoon of the 30th day of the Lunar New Year and the full moon day to wash away dust from the world and eliminate miasma of body and mind.
- Steamed Water for Feeling Cold in the Corner of the Yard: Bamboo leaves, lemon leaves, perilla leaves, mugwort, boil in a clay pot and cover with a blanket to sweat to relieve the cold and evil spirits.

### 2. Riverfront Laundry & Cleaning of Items:
- River Village Laundry Wharf: Women gather at the river wharf, placing clothes on smooth, flat stone slabs.
- Natural Detergent: Use Soap fruit to boil in water to create natural soap foam to clean raw silk; Use wood stove ash soaked in clear water (reduced water) to bleach cotton fibers.
- Wooden Pestle for Beating Fabric: A jackfruit wooden pestle taps rhythmically on the wet fabric on the riverbank stone, creating a bustling sound at the village wharf.
- Drying Clothes on Rattan Rope & Bamboo Fence: Clothes dry in the crisp sunlight on the hibiscus fence, soaking in the fragrance of the sun and wind of the countryside.

## APPENDIX — TEA WAY — CLOTHES — CONTRAST TRAVEL

### 1. 3-layer tea ceremony (people - officials - meditation)

- **People's tea:** Green tea braised in a pile of straw, earthen bowl, drank gulp from the field - astringent, sweet aftertaste, smell of straw smoke.
- **Coffin tea:** lotus tea marinated 7 times, purple tea pot, celadon bowl - drink each sip, discuss water affairs.
- **Zen tea:** Yen Tu stream bud tea, boiled spring water, wooden bowl - drink in silence, listen to the pine trees ringing.

### 2. 3-layer clothing (fabric - dye - wear)

Fabric: linen, ramie, silk (mulberry silk). Dyeing: indigo (indigo plant), brown (brown root), black (mud + almond leaves). Wearing: Giao Linh (civilian) shirt, Vien Linh (mandarin) shirt, brown robe (meditation, purely Vietnamese reform). Scarf: crow's beak (female), pleated (male), votive (old). Each character has an outfit that goes throughout the story - if it breaks, it's patched, and patched becomes a mark.

### 3. Travel 4 ways (foot — water — statue — horse)

Set: Bat Trang tiled main road; water: bamboo boat, copper boat; statue: Me Linh war elephant (5m wide elephant path); code: captured Mongol horse (only generals can ride). An Thai walks + boat; Tinh Lan walked + stretcher; Wherever Fat U goes, he's always a beat late.
