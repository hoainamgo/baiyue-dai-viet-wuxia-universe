## I. ORIGIN & ACCENSION TO THE THRONE (1218–1225)

**[REAL DATA]** — *"姓陳，諱煚，先諱蒲…帝之先世閩人…有名京者，來居天長即墨鄕。生翕，翕生Home誕生…時大八歲，為李朝祗應局祗候正，有從叔陳守度為殿前指揮使，帝因得入侍宫""*

- The king's name is Canh (煚), formerly known as Bo. The founder of the Man people (there is a legend in Guilin) ​​named **Kinh** came to live in **Xi Mac village, Thien Truong district**, **everlastingly worked as a fisherman** (世以漁為業). Born in 1218. He ascended the throne at the age of 8 (1225) — in 1226 the king was **9 years old**.
- **Story writing point:** Can Boi (Tuc Mac) salt flat is the hometown of the Tran family, originally from fishing - Ly Tuan landed on the ancestral land of the dynasty he was about to support. This is pillar Q1.
- Ngo Si Lien's statement about Thai Tong: *"帝寬仁大度，有帝王之量…然䂓畫國事，皆陳守度所為，閨門多慙德矣"* (The king is merciful and generous. king... but **all planning of state affairs was done by Tran Thu Do**, in the country there were many shameful things).

- August 10: **Tran Thu Do kills Ly Hue Tong** at Chan Giao pagoda. Original text of Huizong's curse before committing suicide: *"我家天下已㱕于汝，汝猶弑我。今日我死，異時汝之子孫亦猶是也。"* (Our family and the world have returned to our hands) You, you still kill me. **Story writing points (gold mine Q1–Q5, Q13, Q25):**
1. **Nguyen Non occupied Bac Giang, Doan Thuong occupied Hong Chau** exactly in 1226, bandits gathered at Tan Vien mountain, Quang Oai - our salt flat is located in the middle of this region. The "lord camp" outside the Thu Do circle is real, no need to make it up.
2. **Thu Do's way of suppressing: appointing kings to create division**, promising to give titles to those who have not surrendered, being strict and clear - true to his "caught but decisive" nature. Ly Tuan learned this lesson in District 5.
3. **Princess Thien Cuc - King Ly's wife married to Thu Do** - is the most dangerous woman in Thu Do's palace. Primers Q2–Q3.
4. **The Curse of Hue Tong** ("Your descendants will also be like this") - Ngo Si Lien sour: later the emperor was strangled, the former queen was killed, "how did you meet" (出乎爾反乎爾). This is the detonator in District 17 of the Lu Thi case.

## III. 1227 — BLOOD Oath CEREMONY (PURE VIETNAMESE BELIEF, NO FOREIGN BUDDHISM)

**[REAL HISTORY]** — Kien Trung 3 (1227): *"宣盟誓條…其儀每年四月四日，宰相百官鷄鳴時詣城門外，昧爽進朝…出城西門，至銅皷山神祠會盟歃血。中書檢正，宣誓書云： 「為臣盡忠，居官清白，有渝此盟，神明殛之。」宣訖，宰相點閘""*

- Every year **on the 4th of the fourth month**, the prime minister goes out of the west gate of the city to the **temple of Dong Co Mountain god** and swears blood: "If I lose my loyalty, I become an honest official. Anyone who violates this oath will be killed by the gods."* Whoever is deficient will be fined 5 ton of money. People from all over stood and watched.

## IV. 1228–1232 — SETTLEMENT, REDEFINITION OF MEMBERS, CONSTITUTION OF THE APPARATUS

**[REAL DATA]**
- 1228 (Mau Ty): worshiped **Lieu as Thai lieutenant**. *"閲定清化府帳籍故事，每年春首社官〈今社長〉開報人口，謂之單數"* — approval of Thanh Hoa household books; At the beginning of spring every year, the **commune official** (now the commune chief) declares the population, called the **unit number**; clearly state the clan, civil and military officials, soldiers, ca luu, royal male, old, long old; If you have a mandarin, then your descendants will be warm enough to become mandarins. If you are rich and strong and do not have a title, you will be drafted into the army and live as a soldier.
- December 1228: **Nguyen Non attacks and kills Doan Thuong** — Thu Do's division begins, the two warlords destroy each other.
- 1227–1232: examination of the three teachings (Confucianism, Taoism, Thich); clerical rules, applying the law to sell instructions.

**Story point:** The household registration book is the real weapon of the Tran Dynasty - who has a title, who is a soldier, who is in hiding. Ly Tuan went from measuring reconnaissance to "measuring people" as a natural path (Q8, Q10).

## VIII. 1237–1238 — THE LU THI AFFAIR AND THE REBELLION (GOLD MINE OF THE STORY)

**[REAL DATA]** — *"丁酉六年…納兄懷王柳妻順天公主李氏為順天皇后，降昭聖為公主。時昭聖無子，而順天懷娠國康三月，陳守度與天極公主密謀於帝曰：「宜冒取之，以賴其後。」故有是命。柳於是聚眾，下大江作亂。 ""*

- Plan: The king took his wife, Hoai Vuong Lieu (Thuan Thien, Princess of the Ly family) to be his queen; **promoted Chieu Thanh to be a princess**. Reason: Chieu Thanh is immortal, Thuan Thien is 3 months pregnant with Quoc Khang. **Tran Thu Do and Princess Thien Cuc secretly plotted with the king: "Should take it, so that you can rely on me in the future."**
- Lieu gathered the crowd and went down the Dai Giang River to cause chaos.
- **The king fled to Yen Tu** to stay with **Phu Van Quoc Su** (Thai Tong's old friend). Thu Do led the court to welcome the king, the king refused: *"朕以幼冲，未堪重寄…不敢宅帝位，以辱社稷。"* Thu Do tried to ask but couldn't, so he told everyone: *"凣乘興所在，即是朝廷"* — **Wherever the price is far away, that is the royal court** — then built Thien An palace, guarding Doan Minh right in the mountain. The national teacher said: *"陛下宜速回銮，毋使𢦤弟子山林"* (Please return quickly, Your Majesty, don't let the disciple die in the forest) → the king returned to the capital.
- **Two weeks later, Lieu secretly took a dugout boat and pretended to be a fisherman to ask for goods.** The king was on the boat, the two brothers were **crying together**. When Thu Do heard the news, he immediately went to the royal boat, drew his sword and roared: "Kill the Lieu enemy!"** The king hid Lieu in the cabin, said: *"Phung Can King is coming"* and then **covered himself**.
- **THE MOST EXPENSIVE SENTENCE IN THE SERIES:** Thu Do was so angry, **threw his sword into the river**: *"我是走狗耳！安知爾兄弟之順逆乎？"* — **"I'm just a hunting dog! How do I know if your brothers are good or bad?"**
- The king reconciled, took **An Phu, An Duong, An Sinh, An Hung, An Bang** as a hamlet for Lieu, and appointed **An Sinh Vuong**; Rebel troops in Dai Giang were killed.
- **Story writing points:**
 1. The sentence "I'm just a hunting dog" is the biggest point of the character Thu Do - reserved for Q19, not used in other books.
 2. The mastermind was not the king but Thu Do + Princess Thien Cuc (Ly Hue Tong's wife) - just like the curse of 1226: descendants will repay.
 3. Lieu surrendered by **dug out boat, impersonating a fisherman** — a natural reconnaissance detail for Ly Tuan to arrange a retreat (Q18).
 4. Thu Do built a palace in the mountain just to force the king to return - the tactic "wherever the king lives is the court" is a way to build power without the need for soldiers.
- 1252: *"安生王柳卒，年四十一，加大王"* — An Sinh Vuong Lieu died in 1252, at the age of 41, and was appointed great king.

- **April 1242:** ordered generals to guard the Northern border, invaded Bang Tuong land — *"初，自元太宗崩後，関塞往往不通"* (**after the death of Yuan Thai Tong, the border crossing was often not open**). → Northern News DUT from 1241: the perfect excuse for Ly Tuan to build a news network (Q14).
- **1248 (Mau Than year 17):** *"令諸路築堤防水，名曰鼎耳，自原頭至海岸，以遏洪水漲溢。置河堤正副""* → **Build Dinh Nhi dike from the source to the sea; appointed Ha De as deputy envoy; If you occupy people's fields, the price will be PAID**. (Edit DocC: **Ha di was the official deputy official position from 1248**, not an interpretation.)

- **1250 (Canh Tuat):** earthquake; edict to the people **calling the king "nation"**; Changing the Do Ve Palace to become the Three Monasteries (Phung Tuyen, Thanh Tuc, Hien Chinh); took Le Phu Tran as lieutenant general.
- **1251 (Tan Hoi, from February changed to Nguyen Phong):** The king himself wrote an inscription to the princes. Married Princess Thien Thanh to King Trung Thanh - **Prince An Sinh, Prince Quoc Tuan (Tran Hung Dao) took over, the princess returned to Quoc Tuan**.

- **1255 (At Mao):** ordered to build Thanh Hoa dike; **recruiting mandarins to be Ha dike, deputy envoy of all routes; During leisure time, he urged the army to build dikes and open sluice gates to prevent floods.

**Story writing point:** 1242 broken news from the North + 1248 Dinh Nhi dike to pay for fields + 1255 ha dike porcelain and highways are three real landmarks for Ly Tuan to attach his work to; Q14 (news network) and Q12 (dykes) do not need to be fabricated.

## X. 1257–1258 — HEAD OF THE EAST: WHO IS THE RULER, WHO IS THE ONE WHO WANT TO RUN

**[REAL DATA]** — *"丁巳七年…秋八月，㱕化寨主何屈驛奏元使來。九月，詔左右將軍將水步軍禦邉， ""*

- August 1257: **owner of Quy Hoa Ha Khuat farm** rode his horse to announce the arrival of a Nguyen envoy. September: The general ordered the left and right army to guard the border, **under the control of QUOC TUAN** (Tran Hung Dao, then ~29 years old). November: ordered people to repair weapons. **December 12: General Nguyen Ngoc Luong Hop Dai invaded Binh Le Nguyen.**
- Battle of Binh Le Nguyen: the king personally directed the war, *"官軍少却，帝顧左右，惟黎輔陳單騎出入賊陣，顔色自若…輔陳力諫曰：「今陛下特一孤注耳，宜避之」"* — the army retreated, leaving only **Le Phu Tran** with one horse to go in and out of the enemy battle; Father Tran warned: **"Now your Majesty is just a gamble, you should avoid it."** The king retreated to the Lo river, Father Tran acted as queen, **using the boat's plank to cover the king** to escape. The enemy was strong and retreated to defend the Thien Mac River.
- **THE MOST EXPENSIVE SCENE - contrasting two people:** The king rides a small boat to ask for advice from the Crown Prince **NAT KIEU** (the king's younger brother): Nhat Kieu is leaning on the boat, unable to sit up, just **dip in water and write the words "NHANH TONG"** (入宋 - go to Song to take refuge) on the side of the boat. The king asked where his Tinh Cuong army was and replied: *"Call did not come."*
- The king moved the boat and asked Grand Master **Tran Thu Do**: *"臣首未至地，陛下無煩他慮"* — **"My head has not yet fallen to the ground, don't worry about anything else."**
- **Story writing points:**
 1. The general commander of batch 1257 was **Tran Quoc Tuan**, not "Nhat Hao" (edit general outline).
 2. The one who wanted to run away was **Nhat Kieu** (Kham Thien Dai Vuong, the king's younger brother) - the detail "dipping water to write words" is one of the most expensive scenes in the entire book, reserved for Q25.
 3. The sentence "The god's head has not yet fallen" comes right after the sentence "entering the Song Dynasty" - placing the two sentences next to each other will have a concluding point.
 4. Le Phu Tran (Le Tan) - the man who covered the king with a boat plank - is a real supporting character, used as a counterexample.

## XII. THREE PORTRAITS LOCKED

**1. Tran Thu Do** — the person who did all the planning of the country's affairs ("䂓畫國事，皆陳守度所為"); defeat warlords by **dividing titles** rather than killing; pressure the king by **building a royal court right where the king resides**; was the person who killed King Ly and then **took King Ly's wife as his wife**; when the king shields his enemy, he **throws his sword into the river** and cries "I am just a hunting dog"; and finally said the words **"My head has not yet fallen to the ground."** Ngo Si Lien called him a king-killer; History also records him as the man who made his fortune. Keep it both ways, don't explain.

**2. Tran Thai Tong** — ascended the throne at the age of 8, lived in the hands of Thu Do; to be generous (the judge said "to be merciful and generous"); both ruling and being led: the 1237 incident was not the king's intention but Thu Do's plot; When Lieu came to the line, the king cried and covered himself. Write like someone learning to be a king in the shadow of a very powerful teacher.

**3. Linh Tu national mother / Princess Thien Cu** — wife of Ly Hue Tong, demoted to princess and then married to her husband's murderer; By 1237, he was the one who secretly plotted with Thu Do to steal Lieu's wife. This is a powerful female portrait with the most real motive, no need to make it up.
