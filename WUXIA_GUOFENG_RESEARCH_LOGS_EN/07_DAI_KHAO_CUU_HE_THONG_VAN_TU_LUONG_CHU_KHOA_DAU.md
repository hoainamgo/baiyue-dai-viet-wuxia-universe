## 📜 PART III: NORTHERN HISTORY ACCEPTS THE BACH VIET WRITING SYSTEM

Not only modern archeology, but also the most ancient historical books of the North also record the existence of ancient Vietnamese script:

1. **Thong Dien (Do Huu - VIII century) & Luc Thu Co (Dai Dong):**
   * Clearly stated: *"During the Hung King period, the Giao Chi people had a tadpole-shaped script, called Khoa Dau (蝌蚪文), written on leaves, bamboo cards, turtle shells..."*
2. **The Great Story and Histories of Sima Qian:**
   * Record of the event during the reign of King Zhou (1042 - 1021 BC): The envoy of **Viet Thuong Thi** (belonging to Bach Viet) went to the Zhou capital to offer white pheasants, bringing with him diplomatic documents written in **the South's own script**. The Zhou dynasty had to go through many translations to understand it.
3. **Theory of Explanation of Literature (Hua Than - 2nd Century AD):**
   * The first Chinese dictionary recognizes many hieroglyphs reflecting the agricultural life of wet rice, boats and Southern products absorbed from the Bach Viet culture.
