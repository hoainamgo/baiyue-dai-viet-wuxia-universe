## I. RED RIVER dyke at the beginning of the Tran Dynasty — 1236 AND 1248

[REAL HISTORY — DVSKTT Annals volume V, Tran Thai Tong era]

- **1236 (Binh Than):** *"in the summer of June, the great water broke the Le Thien palace"* - that year the great water broke the dike in the capital city (a real disaster), and it was that flood that revealed the affair of An Sinh Vuong Lieu (see political SSOT section VII). There is no document "building the Co Xa dike in 1236" in the record - this case belongs to another record; if used, paste [CONTROLLED REFLECTION].
- **1248 (Mau Than) — the real dyke landmark of the Tran Dynasty:** *"ordered the construction of water-blocking dikes, called Dinh Nhi dikes, from the source to the coast, to prevent floodwaters from overflowing. He placed Ha dike as the deputy governor. Where to build, look at the people's fields, measure the amount of embankment, according to the price paid. The construction of Dinh Nhi dike started from here."*
- **1255 (At Mao):** *"ordered to build dikes in Thanh Hoa; recruited mandarins to be Ha dike chief deputy envoy of roads; during leisure time, he directed troops to build dikes and open sluices to prevent floods."*
- Lessons from the people's strength: the 1248 dike paid MONEY for the people's occupied fields - the Tran Dynasty learned not to force the people. Twenty thousand people could not stop it with bows and arrows (copy story in political ssot).

## II. COMMUNE — COMMUNE COUNTRY, HUONG TRUONG, HA DE

[ACTUAL HISTORY — District 4, Thien Ung Chinh Binh (1240), commune official regulations]

- Rule 1240: each commune chose a person of great fame to be a commune official. The Complete Book records the king's intention: commune officials must "help those in need, love them like children in their own family" and "should not be greedy and appropriate other people's property".
- Above the commune are the village chief and the commune chief - the ranks connecting the commune with the highway and palace. The list of specific tasks (farming supervisor, border inspector, foreman) is [CONTROLLED THOUGHT] deduced from household and tax records.
- Expensive evidence: the "virtue" part states that Thu Do once "approved household registration numbers"; Linh Tu, the mother of the country, asked for a person to be a dong dong (minor position in the commune). When he arrived at the commune, he told that person "to cut off a toe to distinguish him from others". Household books are approved at the commune level, with specialized positions in the commune.

Land to build dikes and occupy people's fields is **according to the price paid**. ## III. TAXES – LABOR TRANSACTIONS

[TRUE HISTORICAL DATA - An Nam chi comb, Le dien su of the Tran dynasty]

- Public fields - the state assigns people to plow, rent three parts for one part; Fourth field - ten parts get one part. These two numbers are the backbone of the Tran dynasty's land tax.
- Land allocation: in 1237, Yen Phu, Yen Duong, Yen Sinh, Yen Hung, Yen Bang were cut off for Tran Lieu to build a thang moc hamlet (seed of Yen Sinh king); The data for each manor was copied later, so in the story, the numbers are left vaguely according to people's mouths.
- Servant labor: dyke builders (the Co Xa incident shows a scale of twenty thousand people), soldiers following the army, serving at the village (1253, 1258). On public days, there are no longer official documents at the beginning of the court - the convention "one period per three days" is [CONTROLLED IDEA], stated as village custom, not as country law.
- Salt tax: salt fields are under state management, salt workers pay products and services; Carrying salt with lightning. The monopoly on the salt trade became a custom inscribed in later generations; At the beginning of the dynasty, it was written in the form of "salt payment tax" — [CONTROLLED IDEA], comparing SSOT of the salt industry.
- Which arc should I write in: Q4 Salt and Rice (salt tax, trade war 1); Q10 (balancing tax and services).

## IV. WAREHOUSE – WAREHOUSE PLAN

[Indirect TRUE DATA — DVSKTT District 4, records of drought diagnosis]

- Public warehouse: during Thai Tong's time, in the year of drought, taxes were exempted, grain allowance was opened, and then general amnesty - state grain warehouses were organized, opened when people were hungry; Real background of District 11.
- Warehouse of the wealthy: edict 1246 on rice loans proves that the rice of the wealthy is outside the public warehouse, controlling the life and death of the poor; The manor's warehouse is the real weight of the chief.

## V. FARMERS' LIFE IN THE FLOOD SEASON — RICE LOANS, RICE DEVELOPMENT

[ACTUAL HISTORY — DVSKTT District 4, 13th Thien Ung Chinh Binh (1246), edict on rice lending]

- Decree 1246 redefined rice lending rules, with an interest rate of "2/10", showing compassion for the poor oppressed by the rich. When writing dialogue for the character to say "ten originals for two", don't throw around modern-day numbers.
- Flood season: Red River floods in July and August; If the dyke breaks, fields will be lost, houses will float away, and grain after grain will be hungry. "Poor season forecast" and the title "wind reader" (Q11) are creations of corpus.
- The practice of destroying rice when hungry: ancient documents do not correctly write the word "destroying rice"; In fact, in the year of famine, people flocked together to take warehoused rice and field rice, and those who had warehouses had to watch at night - can be written with the label [CONTROLLED IDEA], matching "destroying the false warehouses of the gods" in Q11.
- The price of famine: The complete book records the famine of 1290, three kilograms of rice per tain of money - outside the frame of the story, only used for comparison outside the story.
- Which arc should I write in: Q11 (starvation scene, rice loan, warehouse destruction); Q12 (dyke breakage opens the case).
