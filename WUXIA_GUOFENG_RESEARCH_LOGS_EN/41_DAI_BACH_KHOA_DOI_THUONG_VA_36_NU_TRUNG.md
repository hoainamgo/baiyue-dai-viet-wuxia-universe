### 1. Details of 6 Great Female Poets:

#### 1. 🪶 XUAN HUONG HO — "THE LORD OF POETRY" (GYANGHO'S STUPID SPIRIT)
* **Method & Style:** Nom poetry is as sharp as a forged knife, arrogantly overthrows male prejudice, uses everyday folk language but is extremely profound.
* **poetry context:** Next to a cup of wine at a tea shop in Khan Xuan ward, a paper fan spreads cool summer air, a ripe jackfruit tree, a cliff with a poem taunting the fake grape fame.
* **Poetic style:** The crisp rhythmic beats, sharp bouncing notes, Mezzo's arrogant, free-spirited, and generous voice.

#### 2. 🏯 MRS. THANH QUAN DISTRICT (NGUYEN THI HINH) — "THE SOUL OF THE ANCIENT POETRY OF THANG LONG"
* **Character & Style:** Elegant, noble, exemplary words, nostalgic for the rise and fall of the country.
* **poetry context:** In the late afternoon, I walked through Ngang Pass (*"Stop and stand still in the sky and water / A piece of love between me and you"*), watching the moon shadow illuminating the mossy Cua Bac citadel, the oar let go of the Nhi river wharf.
* **Poetic style:** A tragic string orchestra, a string instrument strumming melancholy notes, a choking bamboo instrument, a Contralto voice thick with velvet.

#### 3. 📜 THI POINT TROUBLE — "HONG HA NGUYEN SI" (MASTER OF TRANSLATION & HERBAL MEDICINE)
* **Temperament & Style:** Extremely erudite, excellent translation of *Chinh Phu Ngam* from Chinese characters to pure Vietnamese song-seven-six-eight poetry that touches millions of hearts; both teaching and taking medicine to save lives.
* **poetry context:** The oil lamp next to the bookcase of Phu Xa village, the paper drenched in grape ink, the smell of golden herbal medicine warms the thatched house on the bank of the Red River.
* **Poetic style:** The sound of a young felt string bending notes, the poetic rhythm of 7/7/6/8 is full of emotion, the clear Soprano voice is choking.

#### 4. 📰 SUONG NGUYET ANH (NGUYEN THI KHUE) — "VIETNAM'S FIRST FEMALE PEN OWNER"
* **Character & Style:** Mr. Do Chieu's daughter, although blind in both eyes and widowed, stubbornly became the editor of the newspaper *Nu Gioi Chung* (The Bell of Women), promoting patriotism and women's rights.
* **poetry context:** The sound of waves crashing at Ba Tri wharf (Ben Tre), the tip of an iron pen plowing on the page, the heart is as persistent as an apricot flower blooming in the middle of winter.
* **Poetic style:** Majestic rhythm, urging horns and drums, proud and sharp poetry.

#### 5. 🪕 DAO NUONG (DAO THI HUE) & ARABIC ARTISTS OF CA TR
* **Character & Style:** The talented female artist used her singing voice and a cup of wine to make the Ming enemy general drunk in Dao Dang village, helping the insurgents liberate their homeland.
* **poetry context:** The sound of the bass guitar is deep and dull, the bamboo beater makes a crisp clicking rhythm, the flower mat spread in the middle of the village communal house, the cup of spicy wine against the humidity of the moonlit night.
* **Poetic style:** Traditional Ca Tru melodies, unique low vibrato notes, combined with modern beats to create Co Phong Auditorium style.

#### 6. 🌸 PRINCESS NGOC HAN (LE NGOC HAN) — "Who is immortal"
* **Character & Style:** The talented queen of Emperor Quang Trung, the author of poetry *Ai Tu Van* mourned her husband so tragically that it shook the mountains and rivers.
* **poetry context:** The smoke of incense hovering over Phu Xuan city, the sound of wind roaring through the top of Hai Van pass, small tears on tattered brocade war robes.
* **Poetic style:** The long and mournful sound of the String Instrument (Nhi), the rumbling thunder and the distant Tay Son battle drums.
