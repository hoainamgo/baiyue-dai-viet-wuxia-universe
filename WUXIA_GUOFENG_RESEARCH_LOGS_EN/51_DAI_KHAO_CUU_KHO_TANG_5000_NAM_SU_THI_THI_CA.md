## 🔍 PART I: ARCHAEOLOGICAL & HISTORICAL EVIDENCE ABOUT THE EXISTENCE OF THE 5,000-YEAR-OLD BACH VIET EPIC

A civilization with **a complete writing system from 5,300 years ago (Luong Chu Culture)** and a flourishing wet rice agriculture must necessarily possess **an extremely vast treasure of epics, philosophy and poetry**.

### 1. Living Evidence From Oral Epics Heritage:
Despite thousands of years of natural disasters and brutal policies of destroying documents, the epic treasures of the Bach Viet descendants still stand tall in folk memory:
* **Epic "Giving Land to Give Birth to Water" (Mo Muong):** More voluminous than **20,000 epic verses**, recounting the entire universe from the beginning of the wilderness, the great flood, the magic tree that gave birth to all species, the process of establishing villages, keeping fire and building the country.
* **Epic Dam San, Ot Ndrong (Central Highlands - South Asia):** Tens of thousands of verses portray the hero conquering nature, protecting villages and respecting the Creator.
* **Viet Nhan Ca (Vietnamese Songs - 6th Century BC):** The lyrical epic in the ancient Bach Viet language, transcribed in *Thyet Uyen* by Liu Huong, is iron evidence of the sublime poetic art of the boatman on the Yangtze River.

### 2. Why are written biographies broken but the soul of poetry is immortal?
* **Book burning & heritage destruction:**
  * Year 43 AD: **Ma Vien** confiscated all Dong Son drums, burned all books with bamboo cards (bamboo gian), and wiped out the Khoa Dau literacy schools.
  * Year 1407: **Ming Dynasty (Truong Phu)** issued an order to destroy: Confiscate all books, epitaphs, and genealogies of Dai Viet, transport them to Kim Lang or burn them to ashes.
* **Magical vitality of oral tradition:** Vietnamese people have transformed the entire epic treasure into **Folk Songs, Folk Songs, Dances, Luc Bat Poems and Xoan / Quan Ho Sings**, transmitted from mother's mouth to children's ears for 5,000 years without interruption.
