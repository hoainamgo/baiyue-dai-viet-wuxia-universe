## 🧭 I. THE PRINCIPLES OF "IMMORTAL TRADITION" (THE IMMORTAL TRADITION RULE)

## 🎨 III. THE ART OF COMBINATION "ANTIQUE - METAL TOGETHER"

In order for the work to have both an elegant ancient flavor and a close, contemporary feel:

### 1. Formula "Starting Ancient - Connecting Metal" (From the Past Touching the Present):
* **Ancient setting:** Creates the image of an old boat, smoke from a straw stove, and the sound of silk weaving in a mulberry field.
* **Timeless concept:** Promoting love of homeland, filial piety, unwavering loyalty - values ​​that young people today still feel moved to sympathize with.

### 2. Formula "Multi-Sensory Space":
* Portraying natural sounds (the sound of a stream, the sound of a kite flute, the sound of oars) and simple everyday images.
### 3. "Emotional Anchoring Food & Senses" Formula:
* Instead of saying *"I miss you so much"* (abstract), write:
> *"I remember the pot of goby braised with spicy pepper, my mother kept the charcoal stove,
> Remember the cool bowl of jute soup to dispel the scorching summer sun."*
* 👉 This **Real-life sensory detail** awakens the childhood memories of 100% of Vietnamese listeners anywhere in the world!
