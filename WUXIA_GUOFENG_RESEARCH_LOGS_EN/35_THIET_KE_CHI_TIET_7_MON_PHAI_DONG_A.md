## 🏛️ I. SUPREME PALACE & MILITARY POWER:

### 1. THE LUONG-TRUNG ROYAL (THE DAI VIET GOVERNMENT):
* **Special structure:** **Thai Thuong Hoang** (regent in Thien Truong - Nam Dinh) combined with **Reigning Emperor** (reigning in Thang Long). The two-headed dragon model helps the country never fall into a situation of empty power when the young king goes to war.
* **Secret Service Apparatus — KIM Ngo Than Ve (Cam Y Ve Dai Viet):**
  * *Functions:* Prohibited secret protection troops, specializing in internal investigations, hunting down O Ma Nhi spies and handling treasonous forces.
  * *Martial arts:* *Kim Ngo Pha Van Kiem*, specializes in using short swords hidden in his sleeves and bronze darts shaped like apricot blossoms.
  * *Costume:* Tran Dynasty dragon embroidered brocade shirt with gold thread trim, jade belt, bronze tag engraved with the words *"Kim Ngo Cam Ve"*.

### 2. VAN KIET TIET PHU (GENERAL MILITARY DIVISION):
* **Dominant:** **Hung Dao Dai Vuong Tran Quoc Tuan**.
* **Headquarters:** Van Kiep Fortress (Hai Duong) — the intersection of 6 rivers (Luc Dau Giang).
* **Army:** 200,000 warriors like the word **"SAT THAT"** on their arms, ready to die to protect every inch of the Southern land.

## 🥋 II. SYSTEM OF 7 GREAT sects & powers of the Gypsy DETAILS:

---

### 1. 🌊 LAC LONG THUY PHAI (DONG A & DOAN DAO SAILORS)
* **General group:** Secret water of Van Kiep lagoon & Chu Dau river wharf.
* **Grandmaster / Representative:** **An Thai Knight** (`An_Thai.png`) — Great and true disciple of Water God General Yet Kieu.
* **Internal structure:**
  * *Hai Long Duong:* Specializes in diving deep into enemy ships.
  * *Street Rice Leaf:* Short-sword special forces team for close combat on land.
* **Martial Arts & Realm System:**
  * *Mind Dharma:* **Cuu Long Chan Hai Quyet** (3 levels: *Water Water $\rightarrow$ Insight $\rightarrow$ Long Soak*).
  * *Special moves:* **Short Knife with Rice Leaves** (18 techniques to restrain the long field) & **Leg Strikes to Clamp the Neck and Take Down** (21 foot blows flying 2m high to break the neck of a horse-riding general).
* **Weapons:** Bronze Short Knife with stingray handle engraved with Lac bird, iron drill with boat chisel.
* **Clothes & Identification:** Indigo shirt with chest slit woven with hemp dyed with dark blue indigo root, tattooed with Giao Long, wrist with the word *Sat That*.

---

### 2. 🌸 TRUC LIN MEDITATION (THIS NONG MEDICINE & SWORD ARCHAEOLOGY)
* **General altar:** Ngoa Van Pagoda (Yen Tu Sacred Peak) & Cam Giang Pagoda (Hai Duong).
* **Patriarch / Representative:** **Miss Moc Tinh Lan** (`Moc_Tinh_Lan_square.png`) - The proud successor of the Great Zen Physician Tue Tinh.
* **Internal structure:**
  * *Vuong Pharmacy Institute:* Gathered 580 herbal medicines, prepared sa truong paste.
  * *Cam Kiem Cac:* Practice the string instruments Co Nhac and Bamboo Sword to neutralize evil intent.
* **Martial Arts & Realm System:**
  * *Mind dharma:* **Da Trach Quy Tuc Cong** (Qigong regulates breathing like morning mist).
  * *Excellent skill:* **Bamboo Sword Thien Tam (Lao Mai Quyen)** - winding bamboo sword hits 108 major acupoints, absolutely not killing; **Shen Nong Herb Acupuncture Technique**.
* **Weapons:** Sharpened Yen Tu Bamboo Sword, Bamboo Stick & Chu Dong Tu Conical Hat, sixteen-string silk string instrument.
* **Clothes & Identification:** Bright brown shirt made of jute or rustic Chu Dau silk, peach wood hairpin, and wearing a bamboo medicine basket with the scent of lotus and wormwood on the back.

---

### 3. 🏔️ TAN SON MON (IRON HAMMER AND ANCIENT STRING INSTRUMENT)
* **General group:** King Peak of Ba Vi Mountain (Tan Vien Son) & Northwest Deep Forest.
* **Patriarch / Representative:** **Brave Descendant of Thach Sanh**.
* **Internal structure:**
  * *Tie Phu Duong:* A team of brave woodcutters used iron axes to open mountains.
  * *Linh Dieu Duong:* The archery team shot a hundred steps through the leaves.
* **Martial Arts & Realm System:**
  * *Mind Dharma:* **Disperse Chan Son Kinh** (My inner strength is as strong as iron skin, but it is difficult for a knife and spear to hurt).
  * *Excellent skills:* **Kai Son Phu Phap** (Hammer position to split rocks in half, behead the demon beast Chan Tinh) & **Hung Ke Quyen** (Tay Son fighting cock fist).
* **Weapons:** Dai Khai Son Phu (50-pound iron ax with wooden handle), Ancient stringed lute Than Co Nhac disarmed 18 vassal countries.
* **Clothing & Identification:** Indigo robe lined with old wild animal skin, barefoot, red fringe scarf wrapped around head.

---

### 4. ⚡ PHU DONG THIET GIA (MASTER OF METALLURGY & GOD OF ENERGY)
* **General:** Soc Son Mountain & Co Loa Military Arsenal.
* **Patriarch / Representative:** **Descendant of General Cao Lu**.
* **Internal structure:**
  * *Hoa Lo Vien:* Casting bronze, forging iron to create armor and 3-pronged arrows.
  * *Thanh Xa Co:* Training archers to shoot inter-continental crossbows.
* **Martial Arts & Realm System:**
  * *Mind Dharma:* **Phu Dong Kim Than Quyet** (Condensing blood and energy into diamond armor).
  * *Special skills:* **Spirit Bamboo Staff (Bagua Kun)** & **Van Tien Quy Tam Shooting the Divine Crossbow**.
* **Arms:** Iron-clad ivory-covered bamboo iron, Linh Quang Than No (inter-continental crossbow that shoots thousands of copper arrows on 3 sides of Cau Vuc).
* **Clothes & Identification:** Leather armor studded with copper rivets, wrapped in a scarf with the word Dinh, wearing copper armor on his hands to protect his body.

---

### 5. ⚓ HAI LONG MAI GIA (MASTER OF THE EASTERN SEA NAVIGATION)
* **Total group:** Ha Long Bay, Cat Ba Islands & South China Sea.
* **Patriarch / Representative:** **Hai Vuong Tu Hai** & **Vuong Thuy Kieu**.
* **Internal structure:**
  * *Decapitation Fleet:* 500 three-masted sailing ships dominate the sea route.
  * *King Fishery Enterprise:* Elite marines dive to catch pearls and fight pirates.
* **Martial Arts & Realm System:**
  * *Mind Dharma:* **Diamond Indestructible Divine Body** (Body trained in the face of ocean waves).
  * *Awesome technique:* **Ba Vuong Chop Hai Dao Phap** (One slash cuts off the enemy's ship's mast).
* **Weapon:** Hai Dai Dao (Weighs 80 pounds carved with sea dragon), Rocket Bow & Divine Mechanical Musket.
* **Clothes & Identity:** Great sea king's brocade cloak with dark blue fur trim, pearl-encrusted belt; Happily dressed in red phoenix robes for the majesty of the main palace.
